# Port records: French scan-check corrections carried into the translations

The working records of two finished ports of corrections from the scan check of the French text (French first) into the English and Spanish SGA. Each folder has its own `README.md` and `METHODOLOGY.md`.

- `sga7_port_20261109/`: SGA 7 I and II, rounds 1–5, into the English (this repository) and the Spanish (sga-es). Batches, prompts, model answers, decisions, audits, logs and scripts. The changes are in sga-en commits up to 202e743 and sga-es commits up to c42c103.
- `sga4_port_20261112/`: SGA 4 into the English: pilot and full run, decisions and scripts. The changes are in sga-en 18468f6. The Spanish SGA 4 is not yet ported.

The model answers are from GPT-6.1 Sol (OpenAI API, Flex tier); the batches, audits, hand review and application are by Claude Opus 5.5. The French corrections themselves are recorded in the French repository, https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/sga-fr (`records/`).

Text records only: build PDFs and TeX logs are left out. Local paths are anonymised ("user").
