This work takes up six lectures given by P. Deligne at Arcata in August 1974
(AMS Summer School on algebraic geometry), under the title ``Inputs of etale
cohomology''. A seventh lecture became the ``report on the trace formula'',
in this same volume. The aim of the lectures was to give proofs of the
fundamental theorems in étale cohomology, stripped of the surrounding
non-sense in SGA 4. We have not sought to state the theorems in their most
general form, nor to follow the sometimes ingenious dévissages that their
proofs require. On the contrary, we have emphasized the ``irreducible''
cases which remain to be treated after all the dévissages have been made.

We hope that this text, which makes no claim to originality, will help the
reader to consult the three volumes of SGA 4 profitably.

\medskip
\noindent\underline{Convention.} We shall consider only quasi-compact
schemes (= finite unions of affine open subsets) which are quasi-separated
(= such that the intersection of two affine open subsets is quasi-compact),
and shall simply call them schemes.
