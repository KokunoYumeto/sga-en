# Observed glossary variation

This report enumerates every multiple rendering in the glossary. The vocabulary combines the source terminology in `build_scripts/en_glossary.py` with an enumeration of all emphasized source spans in the 271 supplied translation fragments. `GLOSSARY_SOURCE_CENSUS.json` retains every French emphasis span; `GLOSSARY_SOURCE_SPANS.tsv` records raw French and English spans, their source fragment, the exact index pairing, and each typography projection. Twenty-three unequal emphasis sequences have explicit reviewed maps. Long mathematical statements remain in that evidence inventory; their content is not shortened into a term. Entries are counted only when the French phrase and the English rendering occur on paired printed pages. Counts are English occurrences on those paired pages, not an inferred one-to-one word alignment. English case is counted exactly: Cartesian and cartesian have disjoint occurrence counts. The selected corrected sides of the original human sisi records are counted once. The glossary also counts unchanged named TeX notation. `GLOSSARY_EVIDENCE.tsv` gives examples.

Terminology has been left as in the released English. The records below identify variation; a matching page may contain a different construction, and the evidence does not justify replacing a valid technical rendering merely to reduce the number of forms.

Glossary rows: 1326. French terms with observed alternative renderings: 217.
