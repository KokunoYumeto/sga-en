One again obtains essentially the same results for \(\Gr^\bullet\) in place of \(K_\bullet\) or \(K^\bullet\), with the same reservation concerning the bijectivity assertion in (i). In the case where one restricts to \(\Gr^1=\Pic\), this bijectivity assertion is valid when \(X\) is noetherian and \emph{normal}, for in this case, for every open subset \(U\) of an affine space over \(k\), one sees immediately that \(\Pic(X)\longrightarrow \Pic(X\times_k U)\) is surjective (EGA \(\mathrm{Err}_{\mathrm{IV}}\) 53, 21.4.13).
