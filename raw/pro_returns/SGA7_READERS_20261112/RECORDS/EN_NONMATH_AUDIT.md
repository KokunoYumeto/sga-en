## Exact reconstructions and checks

There are **54 exact native-English-span operations**, with no changes to the corrected English body. Every target is a unique substring of its native released component; reconstructed spans have balanced TeX groups and math delimiters; the operations have no internal overlap. At this audit snapshot, **54 of 54** complete printed target spans occur in the English diplomatic projection. Rerunning this audit after the English build refreshes that integration check.

The stricter component check also passes for **54 of 54** operations: the corrected target occurs exactly once in its assigned component, the diplomatic reading adds exactly one occurrence of the printed target relative to that component’s corrected baseline, and the corrected target no longer occurs in that diplomatic component. This distinguishes a correctly placed reconstruction from an unrelated matching phrase elsewhere in the exposé.

These operations restore numerical references, bibliography volume numbers, repeated Weil/Weyl misnamings, formal/holomorphic functions, Segre/Veronese, even/odd dimension, alone/zero, irreducible/indecomposable, and the missing nonzero qualification. They also repair incomplete earlier reverse transfers: the cube-root-of-unity prose following mu= and the unattested geometric generic point. The Corollary 6.4 punctuation receives separate treatment because it changes the scope of condition (LV).

The corrupt adjective annexe in XX 1.5.3 is retained underlined and untranslated only in diplomatic English under decision ED-EN-XX-ANNEXE. Corrected English remains connected. A determinate replacement such as annexed would add an interpretation absent from the source.

## Limits

This audit classifies the final supplied source-pair differences, not fresh physical-page readings. It does not re-evaluate formula-only or font-only mathematics, or force French grammatical defects into artificial English errors. It is not a claim that every French stylistic change has a separately different English sentence.

The classification uses complete annotated French variant blocks, so the position of each changed token is checked in its true TeX context. The formula-context scanner includes multline and TikZ environments. Every primary prose candidate and every mapped numeric-reference candidate has an explicit reviewed disposition; no candidate is silently accepted by a vocabulary-frequency heuristic.
