# SGA 5: six readers, French and English, each in three forms, and the 32 places of Exposé XV

You are GPT-6 Astra in ChatGPT Pro mode, with a Python and LaTeX environment. Three ZIP files are attached:
`SGA5_FR.zip`, `SGA5_EN.zip` and `SGA5_SCANS.zip`. Each has `MANIFEST.tsv` at its root (path in the zip, source,
bytes, SHA-256, role); read the manifests first.

## What this is for

SGA 5 (*Cohomologie ℓ-adique et fonctions L*, Séminaire de Géométrie Algébrique du Bois-Marie 1965–66, directed by
A. Grothendieck, edited by L. Illusie; Lecture Notes in Mathematics 589, 1977) has been transcribed into LaTeX,
checked page by page against the scan, and translated into English. The checking produced two French layers
(diplomatic and corrected), fix logs, a register of the misprints of the print, hand replays, a conventions pass, and a
port of the corrections into the English. All of that is attached. What is missing is the readers: finished books,
one per form and language, that carry every fix and say exactly what was changed. You build them, by script and diff.
The text is far too long to read whole, so work from the records, and look at the text wherever a decision needs it.

## The six readers

Build one book per form and language, each a single LaTeX file that compiles with a standard TeX Live:

| File | Language | Form |
|---|---|---|
| `SGA5_FR_diplomatique.tex` | French | **Diplomatic**: the text exactly as printed, misprints kept, with the page of the print marked at every page break |
| `SGA5_FR_corrige_apparat.tex` | French | **Corrected, with apparatus**: misprints corrected; every place where it differs from the print is marked, and the apparatus gives the printed reading, the correction and its source |
| `SGA5_FR_texte_de_lecture.tex` | French | **Plain reading text**: the corrected text with no apparatus at all; the printed page numbers in the margin only; a short preface says what was left out |
| `SGA5_EN_diplomatic.tex` | English | **Diplomatic**: the English that follows the print; where a misprint changes the English, its printed reading is kept; the print's page breaks are marked |
| `SGA5_EN_corrected_apparatus.tex` | English | **Corrected, with apparatus**: as for the French |
| `SGA5_EN_reading.tex` | English | **Plain reading text**: as for the French |

**Build all three forms of a language from one annotated master.** Mark every place where the print and the
corrected text differ, for instance with a macro `\var{printed reading}{corrected reading}{source id}`, and generate
the three forms from that master by switching the macro. The forms then cannot drift apart. A misprint that does not
change the English (a French spelling, for example) gets no variant in the English.

## The 32 places of Exposé XV (GLOBAL_ISSUES 98)

Between the folio-457 and folio-475 markers both layers have 32 occurrences of `\RGamma` or `\R\Gamma`, in the same
order. The corrected layer prints a plain upright R there (`\R` is `\mathrm{R}`); the diplomatic layer prints a
double-struck ℝ (`\R` is `\mathbb{R}` from line 47, redefined at lines 1940 and 6861). For each of the 32 places, look
at the scan (the strips of its page, the estimated strip, and the crops named in `PLACES_98.tsv`) and decide what the
print has: a plain R, a bold R, a double-struck ℝ, or something else. Record in `PLACES_98_DECIDED.tsv`: id, folio,
what the print has, the image and region you read it from, the diplomatic reading, the corrected reading, and the
reason. Then make both layers and all six readers agree with your decisions (the diplomatic layer follows the print;
the corrected layer follows the edition's convention for derived functors, recorded in GLOBAL_ISSUES 98).

## When it is done

**Each item is done when:**
- **A reader** compiles without errors; it carries every applied fix (check this by script against the fix logs and
  report the counts); and its page markers agree with the folios of the French layers.
- **The apparatus** has one entry for each difference between the diplomatic and the corrected forms, and none for
  anything else.
- **Each of the 32 places** has a reading from the scan, and both layers and all six readers agree with it.
- **Each open item** has a decision and a reason.

**Not acceptable:**
- a reader that drops, summarizes or paraphrases any part of the text;
- a fix applied in one form and missing from another;
- an apparatus entry without a source;
- a silent change of notation;
- a decision without a reason;
- local paths or e-mail addresses in any output.

**The task is finished** when all six readers, the 32 decided places, the change logs, the glossary, the decisions
and the README are in the three ZIPs.
