# English content preparation

The final English content consists of the complete ported English source, including all ten published exposés and both indexes, followed by the individually recorded source repairs. The title/credits/contents material inherited from the ported file is retained here only as input to the final reader wrapper, which replaces it with the edition’s required front matter and records that replacement separately.

## Final files and schemas

`EN_corrected_flat.tex` and `EN_diplomatic_flat.tex` are the paired complete sources. `EN_variants.json` records 875 exact differences. Every row has `id`, `page`, `expose`, `before` (printed), `after` (corrected), `source`, `kind`, and corrected-source character offsets `start_corrected` / `end_corrected`. The older `start` / `end` fields refer to the common source immediately before canonical glyph expansion. Use the corrected-source offsets for final wrapping. Applying all `before` replacements to the corrected source, from the last offset to the first, reproduces the diplomatic source byte for byte.

`EN_common_corrections.json` records 398 operations applied before branch selection, including the 64 final English open-item operations, exact scoped Hom repairs, restored source clauses, and the five glossary spelling operations. Each operation carries its source, reason, and asserted occurrence count. The 32 scanned RΓ places retain their individual `R98-01` through `R98-32` IDs in the variant catalogue. The separately plain RΓ on p. 461 is outside that set.

`EN_ALIGNMENT.json` and `EN_ALIGNMENT.tsv` contain 793 exact folio anchors covering 479 of the 483 supplied French folios. JSON positions are `position_corrected` and `position_diplomatic`. All English headings have a source correspondence; there are no reversed folios or markers inside a variant. Folios 94, 399, 411 and 413 occur within grouped mathematical material without a safe exact insertion boundary in the English source. They are not assigned interpolated markers. The source has no folio-435 marker because the print repeats the preceding folio number; the final reader wrapper explains that jump.

`EN_INTERNAL_HOM_AUDIT.tsv` and its JSON counterpart give all 433 one-to-one French/English derived-Hom occurrences in Exposés III and III B. Each row includes exact current and diplomatic French contexts, the English context, the French macro token and its explicit expansion. The two ordinary derived-Hom factors on pp. 201–202 are retained. The audit supplies 321 individual rendering repairs, including one occurrence in the restored clause after (5.2.6); 110 existing internal-Hom tokens receive the documented local expansion, and the two plain factors remain plain.

`EN_GLYPH_VARIANTS.json` retains the formula-level evidence for the scoped blackboard-R / upright-R projection. It is an evidence catalogue; occurrences inside a larger exact variant are merged into that variant so the final source never contains overlapping replacements. `EN_GLYPH_REVIEW.json` is empty. The p. 441 GLOBAL-98b glyph is explicitly the recorded printed single-underlined double-struck F in both branches, with the upright alternative handled as a “needs the scan” unresolved note by the final wrapper.

`EN_PROJECTION_REVIEW_DECIDED.tsv` disposes of all 28 raw matcher diagnostics. The full manual expressions supersede those fragments. One French parenthetical punctuation correction changes no English wording. GLOBAL-98b follows its explicit open-item decision. `EN_ENCODING_REPAIRS.json` documents four control-word boundaries that must be delimited to preserve the recorded source glyphs: the p. 39 overline, p. 52 μ followed by n, p. 116 multiplication sign and p. 381 check accent. Full grouped variants additionally retain the source’s nested subscript on p. 97 and its y-prime subscripts on p. 385. These are TeX encodings of the recorded printed readings.

## Glossary and port audit

`GLOSSARY_SGA5_FR_EN.tsv` has 320 rows: 212 observed English phrase forms in 131 body vocabulary families, plus all 108 paired index entries (56 terminology and 52 notation entries). Counts have an explicit scope in each row. Body counts exclude TeX command names, comments, front matter, indexes and the inherited editorial footnote relocated to the apparatus; index rows state that they count the exact complete TeX label. The source text itself is not passed through the search view. `GLOSSARY_OCCURRENCES.json` supplies every English occurrence offset. `GLOSSARY_INCONSISTENCIES.md` records all 65 multiple-form families in this audited vocabulary, the five spelling repairs (13 replacements in total), and the source-based translation repairs. First-place folio ranges are bounds from exact anchors, not estimates. `GLOSSARY_COVERAGE_AUDIT.json` verifies every index pair, phrase count and occurrence offset. Its additions are supported by exact paired contexts in `GLOSSARY_ADDITIONS.json` and `GLOSSARY_COORDINATED_CATEGORY_EVIDENCE.json`.

The frozen source retains one inherited English editorial footnote at I 4.6, folio 43. `editorial_note_relocation.py` moves that exact note at the final reader-wrapper stage into the corrected-only unresolved appendix, preserving its entire payload and an exact reversible extraction ledger. The stable decision is EN-INHERITED-0046-P43, with source record SGA5-READER-PROSE-002 and MISPRINTS_5:0054/0055. All 879 final annotation calls and 793 folio markers are unchanged. `EN_PORT_AUDIT.tsv` and its JSON counterpart account for every one of the 1,856 final port records: 707 “apply”, 138 “already”, and 1,011 “not applicable”. The audit traces exact source intervals through the complete build edit map, follows the 42 explicit “covered by” chains, and records later source repairs instead of requiring a superseded surface spelling to survive. ## Reproduction order

The scripts use the attached archives extracted beneath `work/sources/SGA5_FR`, `work/sources/SGA5_EN` and `work/sources/SGA5_SCANS`. The final decisions catalogue is `work/contributions/decisions/open_item_patches.json`. The French preparation must first create the two expanded French bodies in `work/build/prepared`; those literal scoped expansions supply the R-glyph evidence. The fixed `manual_prose_variants.json` is an editorial input catalogue retained with these scripts. The decisions contribution supplies the verified index pairs and body terminology inventory.

Run `python work/contributions/english/prepare_english.py --complete` from the build root. It runs `finalize_english.py`, `alignment_english.py`, `collect_glossary_additions.py`, `build_glossary.py` and `audit_english_port.py` in dependency order. Without `--complete`, `prepare_english.py` regenerates only the initial raw formula-alignment diagnostics. The exact bilingual manual catalogue is generated by `build_manual_prose.py` from the prepared corrected English and retained as reviewed input; it is not inferred from the released English. The English reader wrapper automatically applies the exact inherited-note relocation and writes its ledger. After both languages are wrapped, `audit_glossary_and_notes.py` regenerates the final glossary and note-coverage audit outputs without editing any body.

`EN_FLAT_COMPILE_SUMMARY.json` records a complete nonstop pdfLaTeX compilation of the final diplomatic flat source: 311 pages, zero errors, zero undefined control sequences, nine overfull boxes, three LaTeX warnings and seven package warnings. These measurements precede the final reader front matter and folio/apparatus wrappers. The final reader compile reports supersede them for delivery. Temporary TeX logs and the flat test PDF are working outputs, not final reader deliverables.
