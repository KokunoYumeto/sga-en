\section*{Credits, provenance and rights}
\begingroup\small
\noindent\textbf{Original work.} \textit{Cohomologie $\ell$-adique et fonctions $L$. S\'eminaire de G\'eom\'etrie Alg\'ebrique du Bois-Marie 1965--66 (SGA~5)}, directed by A.~Grothendieck, with the collaboration of I.~Bucur, C.~Houzel, L.~Illusie, J.-P.~Jouanolou and J.-P.~Serre; edited by L.~Illusie. Lecture Notes in Mathematics~589, Springer-Verlag, Berlin, Heidelberg, New York, 1977.

The published texts represented here are Expos\'es I, III, III~B, V, VI, VII, VIII, X, XII and XV, together with the terminology and notation indexes. Expos\'es I and III are by A.~Grothendieck, written up by L.~Illusie; the appendix to I and Expos\'e III~B are by L.~Illusie; Expos\'es V, VI and VII are by J.-P.~Jouanolou; Expos\'es VIII, X and XII are by A.~Grothendieck, written up by I.~Bucur; Expos\'e XV is by C.~Houzel. The individual author and reference credits remain in the body.

\noindent\textbf{This edition.} AI-transcribed French text; AI-translated English text; AI-produced corrections and typesetting. The French source of record is the final pair \texttt{fr/sga5/diplomatic/s5.tex} and \texttt{fr/sga5/current/s5.tex} in \texttt{SGA5\_FR.zip}. The August 2026 Codex corpus records describe the separate diplomatic and corrected layers and preserve their correction and reversal ledgers. The subsequent scan check, manual replay and conventions pass are recorded in \texttt{fixes/}, \texttt{reports/} and \texttt{conventions/} of that archive. The original scan is identified there by 496 physical pages, 62,025,563 bytes and SHA-256 \texttt{B256EBD072A8C68209518412A263C928}\allowbreak\texttt{9C6F1854A346733D86F885930D5FE6CA}.

The English base is \texttt{en/ported\_source/sources/native/SGA5/} in \texttt{SGA5\_EN.zip}, the source following the merge and French correction port recorded at sga-en commit \texttt{d11a0c6}. The port report credits Claude Opus~5.5 (scripts, merge and manual check) and GPT-6.1 Sol, effort high (decisions and proposed edits). The French hand replay and conventions reports credit Claude Opus~5.5. The English repository records credit OpenAI Codex, GPT-5.6 Sol for translation and GPT-6 Astra for corrections and assembly in September--October 2026; they also record the other volume-specific AI work below.

\noindent\textbf{This reader build.} GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI, ChatGPT Pro mode). The six readers are generated from one annotated master per language. The records accompanying this build identify the source of each variant and preserve the decisions on unresolved readings.

\noindent\textbf{Human contributions preserved from the collection records.} The following collection-wide attributions are retained with their volume scope, as recorded in \texttt{en/repo/README.md} and \texttt{en/repo/ATTRIBUTION.md}. The original SGA collection includes work of Alexander Grothendieck, Mich\`ele Raynaud, Michel Raynaud, Pierre Deligne, Nicholas Katz, Michel Demazure, Michael Artin, Jean-Louis Verdier, Luc Illusie and their collaborators.

SGA~1 retains the French retypesetting associated with Bas Edixhoven's volunteer project, the Soci\'et\'e math\'ematique de France and Michel Raynaud's notes (arXiv \texttt{math/0206203v2}). SGA~2 is AI-translated from the corrected French TeX of the re-edition edited by Yves Laszlo, \textit{Cohomologie locale des faisceaux coh\'erents et th\'eor\`emes de Lefschetz locaux et globaux}, Documents Math\'ematiques~4, Soci\'et\'e math\'ematique de France, 2005 (arXiv \texttt{math/0511279v1}). Most of its initial French typesetting was done by volunteers of Bas Edixhoven's project: L.~Bayle, N.~Borne, O.~Brinon, J.~Buresi, M.~Chardin, F.~Ducrot, P.~Graftiaux, F.~Han, P.~Karwasz, L.~Koelblen, D.~Madore, S.~Morel, D.~Naie, B.~Osserman, J.~Riou and V.~S\'echerre; C.~Sabbah adapted it to the SMF format.

The SGA~2 and SGA~3 English sources acknowledge Jacob C.~Reinhold's English translation, snapshot \texttt{e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e}, used for comparison and drafting. His stated CC~BY~4.0 notice applies to that contribution and is preserved. Inherited English wording remains attributed to its human source. The SGA~3 re-edition by Philippe Gille and Patrick Polo is credited for its added lemmas, proofs and corrections, which the English gives in Translator's notes and Additions.

SGA~4 is AI-translated from the French LaTeX edition distributed by Fabrice Orgogozo, source snapshot at commit \texttt{71766d9}, and uses its TeX configuration files. Its notes headed ``N.D.E.'' are that edition's editorial notes, translated. SGA~$4\frac12$ preserves Daniel Miller's LaTeX typesetting and his original typesetter's note. SGA~7~I follows the published volume and a recomposed PDF; \texttt{en/repo/SOURCE\_AUTHORITY.md} records its published expos\'e numbering. The repository also credits Claude Opus~5.5 (Anthropic) for the SGA~2 and SGA~3 sources of 26 October 2026 and the SGA~3 translation revised against the 1970 printing in November 2026, and GPT-6.1 Sol (OpenAI API) for proposed edits carrying the French scan-check corrections into SGA~4.
