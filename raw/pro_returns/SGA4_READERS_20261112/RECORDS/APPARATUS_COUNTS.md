# Apparatus counts by exposé and form

Each variant entry is counted once in the total. A single entry may cite an agreed misprint, an accepted one-model item, and a human record together; the three source columns therefore overlap. Human dossiers preserve the source pairs independently of the correction apparatus. The reading forms omit every apparatus and editorial-note category.

Disputes and possible misprints are counted by supplied item ID. They report unresolved readings and introduce no correction variant. Printed-page marginal markers are not apparatus entries.

The original-source supplement preserves 44 French and 315 English documentary pairs from Orgogozo’s untouched source, plus the original V bis p.138 proof/rewrite in each language. These counts are separate from the transmitted D and released-English dossiers. The eight diplomatic editorial-reference entries support citations in retained human notes; their full text and original ordinal labels are recorded separately.

Both documentary forms also preserve all 129 exact original French N.D.E. arguments in a separately attributed source dossier. The visible body-note counts include the single English inline note at IV p.465 and exclude the two archive-only release-generated notices in VIII. Eight human notes occur only in corrected-body branches: seven inside original paired readings and one inside the V bis rewrite; their original texts remain accessible in both documentary forms through the dossiers.
