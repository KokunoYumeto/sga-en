\chapter*{About this edition}
This is an English translation of SGA~3, \emph{Sch\'emas en groupes}, the seminar on group schemes directed by Michel Demazure and Alexandre Grothendieck at the IH\'ES in 1962--64, as published in Lecture Notes in Mathematics 151, 152 and 153 (Springer, 1970).

The translation is AI-translated. OpenAI Codex models translated it from the French TeX of the re-edition by Philippe Gille and Patrick Polo (Documents Math\'ematiques 7 and 8, Soci\'et\'e math\'ematique de France, 2011; for Tome~II, Expos\'es VIII--XVIII, their drafts of 2008--2009), with Jacob C. Reinhold's \href{https://github.com/jcreinhold/sga/tree/e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e}{English translation} used for comparison and drafting; GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI Codex) checked, corrected and assembled it in September and October 2026. In November 2026 Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) read every expos\'e against the 1970 printing. The English follows the 1970 text: passages that the re-edition inserted or reworded are translated from the original, and the re-editors' notes are taken out. The mathematics that the re-edition adds is given in Translator's notes and Additions, written independently and credited to the re-edition. Misprints of the original are corrected with a note giving the printed reading; where a printed statement needs an extra hypothesis or is false as printed, a note keeps the 1970 statement and gives the reason or a counterexample.

English wording taken from Reinhold's translation remains his, and his CC~BY~4.0 notice applies to his contribution.

The rights in the original work remain with its rights holders. Anything in this edition that the publisher holds rights to is dedicated to the public domain under CC0~1.0. Rights holders who object to the availability of this material can open an issue at \url{https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/sga-en/issues}; the material will be taken down.
