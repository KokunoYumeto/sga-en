\SGASection{4}{Roots and vector group schemes}
\SGARefTarget{sga3:xix:section:4}

\par\medskip\noindent
\textbf{\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:xix:paragraph:4-1}4.1.}---
Let \(S\) be a scheme and let \(F\) be a finite locally free
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module.  The \(S\)-scheme \(\mathbb W(F)\) is smooth
over \(S\).  Its Lie algebra is canonically isomorphic to \(F\).
Indeed, there is a canonical isomorphism
\[
  \mathbb W(F)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \operatorname{Lie}(\mathbb W(F)/S)
  =
  \mathbb W\bigl(\operatorname{Lie}(\mathbb W(F)/S)\bigr)
\]
\textup{(Exposé II, \SGARefLink{sga3:ii:corollary:4-4-1}{Corollary to Definition 4.4}, and the
\SGARefLink{sga3:ii:examples:4-4-2}{Examples preceding 4.5})}.  We shall always identify
\(F\) with \(\operatorname{Lie}(\mathbb W(F)/S)\).
\SGARefTarget{sga3:xix:paragraph:4:1}

\par\medskip\noindent
\textbf{\textit{\SGARefLink{sga3:xix:lemma:4:2}{Lemma 4.2}.}}---
\textit{Let \(S\) be a scheme and let \(V\) be a vector bundle over
\(S\), smooth over \(S\).  There is then a unique isomorphism of
\(\mathcal O_S\)-modules}
\[
  \exp\colon
  \mathbb W\bigl(\operatorname{Lie}(V/S)\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ } V
\]
\textit{which induces the identity on Lie algebras.}
\SGARefTarget{sga3:xix:lemma:4:2}

\SGAProof{Proof}
We have \(V=\mathbb V(H)\) for some quasi-coherent
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module \(H\).  There is then a canonical isomorphism
\[
  \mathbb W\bigl(\operatorname{Lie}(V/S)\bigr)
  =
  \operatorname{Lie}(V/S)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  V
\]
(Exposé II, loc.\ cit.).  Then \(H\simeq\operatorname{Lie}(V/S)^\vee\) is
finite locally free, so \(V\simeq\mathbb W(H^\vee)\), and the uniqueness of
\(\exp\) follows at once because \(\mathbb W\) is fully faithful.
\hfill\(\square\)

\par\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~XIX; our
formulation).  The re-edition gives a more detailed proof, which runs as
follows.  Write \(V=\mathbb V(H)=\operatorname{Spec}(\operatorname{Sym}H)\),
with projection \(\pi\colon V\to S\) and zero section \(\epsilon\).  As
\(\operatorname{Sym}H\) is generated by \(H\), one has
\(\Omega^1_{V/S}\simeq\pi^*H\); hence \(\epsilon^*\Omega^1_{V/S}\simeq H\)
and \(\operatorname{Lie}(V/S)\simeq H^\vee\).  Since \(V\) is smooth over
\(S\), \(\Omega^1_{V/S}\) is finite locally free, and so is
\(H\simeq\epsilon^*\Omega^1_{V/S}\).  For such an \(H\), a point of \(V\)
with values in an \(S\)-scheme \(S'\) is an \(\mathcal O_{S'}\)-linear map
\(H\otimes_{\mathcal O_S}\mathcal O_{S'}\to\mathcal O_{S'}\), that is, a
section of \(H^\vee\otimes_{\mathcal O_S}\mathcal O_{S'}\); this gives an
isomorphism of \(\mathcal O_S\)-modules
\(\theta\colon\mathbb W(H^\vee)\xrightarrow{\sim}V\).  By
\SGARefLink{sga3:xix:paragraph:4-1}{4.1}, \(\operatorname{Lie}(\theta)\) is an isomorphism
\(u\colon H^\vee\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Lie}(V/S)\), and
\(\exp=\theta\circ\mathbb W(u)^{-1}\) induces the identity on Lie algebras,
the identification of 4.1 being compatible with the morphisms
\(\mathbb W(w)\).  If \(\exp'\) is another such isomorphism, then
\(\exp^{-1}\circ\exp'\) is an automorphism of the \(\mathcal O_S\)-module
\(\mathbb W(\operatorname{Lie}(V/S))\), hence of the form \(\mathbb W(v)\),
since \(\mathbb W\) is fully faithful; by 4.1,
\(v=\operatorname{Lie}(\mathbb W(v))=\operatorname{id}\), so
\(\exp'=\exp\).  The re-edition also observes that the argument applies, by
transport of structure, to an \(S\)-scheme with a section that is isomorphic
to such a \(V\) compatibly with the sections.
