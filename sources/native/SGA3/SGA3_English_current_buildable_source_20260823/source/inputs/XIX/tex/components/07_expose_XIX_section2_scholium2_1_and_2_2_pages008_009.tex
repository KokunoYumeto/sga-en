\SGASection{2}{Reductive group schemes: definitions and first properties}
\SGARefTarget{sga3:xix:section:2}

\par\medskip\noindent
\textbf{2.1.}\quad
If \(G\) is a group scheme over \(S\), the following properties are
equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:xix:section:2:item:i}(i)] \(G\) is affine and smooth over \(S\), with connected
  fibers.
\item[\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:xix:section:2:item:ii}(ii)] \(G\) is affine and flat over \(S\), of finite presentation
  over \(S\), with geometrically integral fibers.
\end{enumerate}
These properties are stable under base change and are local for the
fpqc topology.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~XIX, adds a proof. In our
formulation: under (i), \(G\) is of finite presentation over \(S\), being
smooth and affine over \(S\), and its fibres, smooth connected group schemes,
are geometrically integral (Exposé~VI\textsubscript{A}, \SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIA-2.4}{2.4}). Under (ii), the
fibres are geometrically reduced group schemes, hence smooth
(Exposé~VI\textsubscript{A}, \SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIA-1.3.1}{1.3.1}), and connected; being flat and of finite
presentation with smooth fibres, \(G\) is smooth over \(S\)
(EGA~IV\textsubscript{4}, 17.5.1). Each property in (ii) is stable under base
change and descends along faithfully flat quasi-compact morphisms
(EGA~IV\textsubscript{2}, 2.5.1 and 2.7.1; the condition on geometric fibres
can be checked at points above each point of \(S\)); by the equivalence, the
same holds for (i).}
\SGARefTarget{sga3:xix:scholium:2:1}

\par\medskip\noindent\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:xix:paragraph:2-2}\textbf{2.2.}\quad
If the \(S\)-group scheme \(G\) satisfies the preceding conditions, and if
\(Q\) is a torus \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:ix:definition:1:3}{Exposé IX, 1.3})} of \(G\), then
\(\underline{\operatorname{Centr}}_{G}(Q)\) is represented by a closed
subgroup scheme of \(G\) which also satisfies these conditions
(\SGARefLink{sga3:xix:paragraph:1-3}{1.3} and \SGARefLink{sga3:xi:corollary:6:11}{Exposé XI, 6.11}).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~XIX, details
this: \(\underline{\operatorname{Centr}}_G(Q)\) is represented by a closed
subscheme of \(G\) of finite presentation (\SGARefLink{sga3:xi:corollary:6:11:item:a}{Exposé~XI, 6.11(a)}), hence is
affine over \(S\); it is smooth over \(S\) by \SGARefLink{sga3:xi:corollary:2:4}{Exposé~XI, 2.4}; and its
geometric fibres \(\underline{\operatorname{Centr}}_{G_{\bar s}}(Q_{\bar s})\)
are connected by \SGARefLink{sga3:xix:paragraph:1-3}{1.3}.}
\SGARefTarget{sga3:xix:section:2:2}

We shall systematically use the following notation: if \(S\) is a scheme
and \(s\) is a point of \(S\), then \(\bar s\) denotes the spectrum of an
algebraic closure \(\overline{\kappa(s)}\) of \(\kappa(s)\).
