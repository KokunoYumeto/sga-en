\phantomsection\label{cor:sga3-VIB-5.5}%
\noindent\textbf{Corollary 5.5.---}
\emph{Let \(S\) be a scheme, and let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group
scheme, locally of finite presentation, with connected fibers, and
universally open over \(S\). Then \(G\) is separated and of finite
presentation over \(S\).}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, mentions here a result of M.~Raynaud, \emph{Faisceaux amples sur les sch\'emas en
groupes et les espaces homog\`enes}, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 119, Springer, 1970, VI, 2.5: if \(S\) is
normal and \(G\) is smooth over \(S\) with connected fibres, every homogeneous space \(X\) under \(G\) (that is,
\(X\to S\) and \((g,x)\mapsto(gx,x)\colon G\times_SX\to X\times_SX\) are coverings for the fppf topology) which
is locally of finite type over \(S\) is locally quasi-projective over \(S\); in particular \(G\) is
quasi-projective over \(S\).}

Indeed, we have just seen that \(G\) is separated over \(S\), hence
quasi-separated. Moreover, \(G\) is quasi-compact over \(S\) by
\SGARefLink{cor:sga3-VIB-3.6}{3.6}.

\noindent\textbf{5.6.---}
\emph{Proof of \hyperref[thm:sga3-VIB-5.3]{5.3}.}
Before proving \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIB-5.3}{5.3}, let us establish a few lemmas.
The following lemma advantageously replaces EGA~IV\(_3\), 14.5.10,\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text
refers to EGA~IV 19.5.10. The statement meant, the existence, over a noetherian base, of quasi-sections of a
universally open, surjective morphism locally of finite type, is EGA~IV\(_3\), 14.5.10; the Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, corrects the reference.} in that it is independent of
noetherian hypotheses.
