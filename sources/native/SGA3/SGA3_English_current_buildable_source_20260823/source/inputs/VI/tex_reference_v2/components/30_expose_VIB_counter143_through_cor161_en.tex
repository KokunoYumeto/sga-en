\phantomsection\label{ctr:sga3-VIB-1.4.3}%
\noindent\textbf{Counter\SGARefLink{ctr:sga3-VIB-1.4.3}{example 1.4.3}.---}
\emph{Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic zero, let \(G\) be
the constant \(k\)-group \(\mathbb Z\), and let \(H\) be the
additive \(k\)-group \(\mathbb G_{a,k}\). Let \(u:G\to H\) be a
morphism of \(k\)-groups. If \(u\ne0\), then \(\ker u=0\), but
\(u\) is not a closed immersion (\hyperref[ctr:sga3-VIB-1.2.2]{1.2.2}).}

We shall use below the following two results, which should have
appeared in Expos\'e~VI\(_A\).

\medskip
\phantomsection\label{lem:sga3-VIB-1.5}%
\noindent\textbf{Lemma 1.5.---}
\emph{Let \(k\) be a field and let \(G\) be a \(k\)-group locally
of finite type. Every irreducible component of \(G\) has the same dimension
as \(G\); in other words, for every \(g\in G\), one has
\(\dim_gG=\dim G\).}

The two assertions are equivalent (EGA~IV\(_3\), 10.6.3 and 10.7.1). Let
\(C\) be an irreducible component of \(G\). If \(\bar k\) denotes the
algebraic closure of \(k\), then \(C\otimes_k\bar k\) is a union of
irreducible components of \(G\otimes_k\bar k\) (EGA~IV\(_2\), 4.4.1); we
are therefore reduced to the case in which \(k\) is algebraically closed
(EGA~IV\(_2\), 4.1.4). In this case \(C\) is deduced from \(G^0\) by a
translation, so that \(\dim C=\dim G^0=\dim G\).

\medskip
\phantomsection\label{prop:sga3-VIB-1.6}%
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 1.6.---}
\emph{Let \(S\) be a scheme of characteristic zero, and let \(G\)
be an \(S\)-group scheme locally of finite presentation along the
identity section \(\epsilon\). For \(G\) to be smooth over \(S\) along the
identity section, it is necessary and sufficient that the
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module}
\[
  \omega_{G/S}
    =\epsilon^*\bigl(\Omega^1_{G/S}\bigr)
\]
\emph{\textup(called the conormal module of the identity section of \(G\)\textup)
be locally free.}

Recall that a scheme \(S\) is said to be of characteristic zero if
the field \(\kappa(x)\) has characteristic zero for every closed
point \(x\in S\).

Recall also (\SGARefLink{sga3:ii:subsection:4-11}{Expos\'e~II, 4.11}) that, if
\(\pi:G\to S\) is the structural morphism, then
\[
  \Omega^1_{G/S}=\pi^*(\omega_{G/S}).
\]
Thus it is equivalent to say that the \(\mathcal O_S\)-module
\(\omega_{G/S}\) is locally free or that the
\(\mathcal O_G\)-module \(\Omega^1_{G/S}\) is locally free.

The proposition is then an immediate consequence of
EGA~IV\(_4\), 16.12.2 and 17.12.5.\footnote{Translator's note: in detail (after the Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, in our formulation): if \(G\) is smooth over \(S\) along
\(\epsilon(S)\), then \(\Omega^1_{G/S}\) is locally free of finite type on an open neighborhood \(U\) of
\(\epsilon(S)\) (EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.2.3), hence \(\omega_{G/S}=\epsilon^*(\Omega^1_{U/S})\) is locally free.
Conversely, if \(\omega_{G/S}\) is locally free, so is \(\Omega^1_{G/S}=\pi^*(\omega_{G/S})\); on an open
neighborhood \(U\) of \(\epsilon(S)\) on which \(G\) is locally of finite presentation over \(S\), the
morphism \(U\to S\) is then differentially smooth (16.12.2), and 17.12.5, applied to the section
\(\epsilon\), shows that it is smooth at the points of \(\epsilon(S)\).}

\medskip
\phantomsection\label{cor:sga3-VIB-1.6.1}%
\noindent\textbf{Corollary 1.6.1 (Cartier).---}
\emph{Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic zero. Every
\(k\)-group locally of finite type over \(k\) is smooth over
\(k\).}

Indeed, the \(k\)-module \(\omega_{G/k}\) is then plainly free, so
(\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-1.6}{1.6})\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text refers to 1.7, which does not
exist; 1.6 is meant, as in the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011).} \(G\) is smooth over \(k\) at the
identity point \(e\), hence smooth over \(k\) (\hyperref[cor:sga3-VIB-1.3.1]{1.3.1}).
