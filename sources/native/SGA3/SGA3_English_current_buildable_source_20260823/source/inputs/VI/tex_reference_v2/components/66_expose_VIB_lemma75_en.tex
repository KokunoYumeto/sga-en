
\medskip
\phantomsection\label{lem:sga3-VIB-7.5}%
\noindent\textbf{Lemma 7.5.---}
\emph{Let \(S\) be a scheme, let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group
scheme, let \(X\) be an \(S\)-prescheme, and let
\(f:X\to G\) be an \(S\)-morphism. Suppose that \(X\) and \(G\)
are locally of finite presentation over \(S\), and that, for
every \(s\in S\) and every maximal point \(g\) of \(G_s\),
there exists a point \(x\) of \(X\) such that \(f(x)=g\) and
\(f\) is flat at \(x\). Then the morphism}
\phantomsection\label{form:sga3-VIB-7.5-covering-morphism}%
\[
  \mu\circ(f\times_S f):
  X\times_S X\longrightarrow G
\]
\emph{is a covering for the \((\mathrm{fppf})\) topology.}

Note that the set \(V\) of points of \(X\) at which \(f\) is
flat is open, and that \(f|_V\) is an open morphism
(EGA~IV\(_3\), 11.3.1); so, replacing \(X\) by \(V\), we may
suppose \(f\) flat. Let
\[
  U=f(X),
\]
which is an open subset of \(G\). Then the morphism
\(X\times_SX\to G\) is the composite
\phantomsection\label{form:sga3-VIB-7.5-factorization}%
\[
  X\times_S X
  \longrightarrow
  U\times_S U
  \longrightarrow
  G,
\]
where the first morphism is faithfully flat and locally of
finite presentation, since \(X\to U\) is. It will therefore be
enough to prove the same for \(U\times_SU\to G\). Now, since
\(G\to S\) is locally of finite presentation and flat, so is
\[
  \mu:G\times_S G\longrightarrow G
\]
(which is isomorphic to a morphism deduced from the preceding
\(G\to S\) by a base change \(G\to S\), cf.\
\hyperref[sga3:VIA:0.1]{VI\(_A\), 0.1}), and hence so is the
induced morphism
\[
  U\times_S U\longrightarrow G.
\]
To prove that it is surjective, it is enough to look fiber by
fiber, where it follows from
\hyperref[sga3:VIA:0.5]{VI\(_A\), 0.5}.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VI\textsubscript{B}, makes two points explicit. By hypothesis \(U\) contains the maximal points of
each fiber \(G_s\), so \(U\cap G_s\) is a dense open subset of \(G_s\), as VI\textsubscript{A}, 0.5
requires; and a faithfully flat morphism locally of finite presentation is a covering for the
\((\mathrm{fppf})\) topology (Exposé~IV, \SGARefLink{sga3:iv:proposition:6:3:1:item:iv:01}{6.3.1~(iv)}).}
