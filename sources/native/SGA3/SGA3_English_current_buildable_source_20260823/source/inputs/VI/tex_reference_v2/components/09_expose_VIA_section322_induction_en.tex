\phantomsection\label{par:sga3-VIA-3.2.2}%
\noindent\textbf{3.2.2.---}
For every algebra \(A'\) over \(A\) (of the type considered in \hyperref[thm:sga3-VIA-3.2]{3.2}),\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text has ``du type considéré en
4.1''; the algebras meant are the local, finite and free \(A\)-algebras of the proof of 3.2, as the
Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~VI\textsubscript{A}, makes explicit.}
let us now denote by \(U(A')\) the set of points of \(G\otimes_AA'\) having an
open saturated neighborhood \(W\) such that the groupoid induced by
\(G_*\otimes_AA'\) on \(W\) has a quasi-section. It is clear that
\(U(A')\) is saturated under the operations of
\(G(\operatorname{Spec}A')\) on \(G\otimes_AA'\). We shall show that,
when \(A'\) is sufficiently large, \(U(A')\) is equal to \(G\otimes_AA'\).

The proof proceeds by induction on \(\dim\bigl(G-U(A)\bigr)\): let
\(g_1,\ldots,g_n\) be closed points belonging to the various irreducible
components of \(G-U(A)\); if \(A'\) is sufficiently large, the points
\(g'_1,\ldots,g'_p\) of \(G\otimes_AA'\) mapping to \(g_1,\ldots,g_n\) are
strictly rational over \(A'\); likewise, \(U(A)\otimes_AA'\) contains a strictly
rational point \(x\) (\(U(A)\) is not empty by
\SGARefLink{thm:sga3-V-8.1}{Expos\'e~V, 8.1}). Consequently, \(U(A')\) contains
\[
  \bigl(U(A)\otimes_AA'\bigr)\cdot x^{-1}g'_i
  \qquad\text{for every }i;
\]
hence \(U(A')\) contains \(g'_1,\ldots,g'_p\), and
\[
  \dim\bigl(G\otimes_AA'-U(A')\bigr)
  <
  \dim\bigl(G-U(A)\bigr).
\]
The induction hypothesis then implies the existence of an algebra \(A''\)
over \(A'\) such that
\[
  U(A'')
  =
  (G\otimes_AA')\otimes_{A'}A''
  =
  G\otimes_AA''.
\]
