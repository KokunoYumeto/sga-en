\phantomsection\label{lem:sga3-VIB-11.8}%
\noindent\textbf{Lemma 11.8.---}
\emph{Let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group, quasi-separated and
quasi-compact over \(S\), such that \(\mathcal A(G)\) is a free
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module \(\mathcal A\) with basis
\((\varphi_i)\), and let \(\mathcal E\) be a quasi-coherent
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module. Suppose either that \(G\) is affine
over \(S\), or that \(G\) and \(\mathcal E\) are flat over
\(S\). Let \(\rho:\mathcal E\to\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal E\) be a
homomorphism defining an operation of \(G\) on \(\mathcal E\),
and let \(x\in\Gamma(S,\mathcal E)\). Put}
\[
  \rho(x)=\sum\varphi_i\otimes x_i,
\]
\emph{where the \(x_i\in\Gamma(S,\mathcal E)\) are uniquely
determined by this relation. Then the submodule
\(\mathcal F\) of \(\mathcal E\) generated by the \(x_i\) is
stable under \(\rho\), quasi-coherent and of finite type over
\(\mathcal O_S\), and \(x\in\Gamma(S,\mathcal F)\).}

It is clear that \(\mathcal F\) is quasi-coherent and of finite
type. Axiom \hyperref[cond:sga3-VIB-11.6-CM1]{\textup{(CM 1)}}
shows that
\(\sum\Delta(\varphi_i)\otimes x_i=\sum\varphi_i\otimes\rho(x_i)\);
on the other hand,
\(\Delta(\varphi_i)=\sum_j\varphi_j\otimes a_{ij}\), whence
\(\sum\varphi_j\otimes a_{ij}\otimes x_i
=\sum\varphi_i\otimes\rho(x_i)\), whence, since the
\(\varphi_i\) form a basis of \(\mathcal A\),
\(\rho(x_i)=\sum_ja_{ji}\otimes x_j\),\footnote{Translator's
note: the 1970 text has \(\rho(x_i)=\sum a_{ji}\otimes x_i\).}
where \(a_{ji}\in\Gamma(S,\mathcal A)\); this shows that
\(\rho(\mathcal F)\subset\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal F\), so that
\(\mathcal F\) is stable under \(\rho\). Finally, axiom
\hyperref[cond:sga3-VIB-11.6-CM2]{\textup{(CM 2)}}
shows that \(x=\sum_i\eta(\varphi_i)x_i\in\Gamma(S,\mathcal F)\).

\par\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}; our formulation).
Only the coalgebra structure of \(\mathcal A(G)\) enters 11.8 and 11.9, and the re-edition states them, and adds
three results, for comodules over a coalgebra. An \(\mathcal O_S\)-\emph{coalgebra} is an \(\mathcal O_S\)-module
\(\mathcal C\) with morphisms \(\Delta\colon\mathcal C\to\mathcal C\otimes\mathcal C\) and
\(\varepsilon\colon\mathcal C\to\mathcal O_S\) satisfying the coassociativity and counit identities (axioms (HA~1)
and (HA~2) of I, \SGARefLink{sga3:i:subsection:4-2}{4.2}); a (right) \(\mathcal C\)-\emph{comodule} is an
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module \(\mathcal E\) with a morphism \(\mu\colon\mathcal E\to\mathcal E\otimes\mathcal C\) satisfying
the analogues of (CM~1) and (CM~2). Let \(C\) be a coalgebra over a ring \(A\), \(V\) a \(C\)-comodule and \(M\) a
submodule of \(V\); put \(C^\vee=\operatorname{Hom}_A(C,A)\), and let \(c(M)\) be the submodule of \(V\) generated
by the \((\mathrm{id}_V\otimes\varphi)(\mu(x))\), for \(x\in M\) and \(\varphi\in C^\vee\).
(a) \emph{If \(C\) is a projective \(A\)-module} (the generalization of 11.8 due to J.-P.~Serre, see
11.10.1)\emph{, then \(c(M)\) is the smallest subcomodule of \(V\) containing \(M\); it is finitely generated if
\(M\) is; and, for every ring homomorphism \(A\to A'\), if \(M'\) is the image of \(M\otimes_AA'\) in
\(V'=V\otimes_AA'\), then \(c(M')\) is the image of \(c(M)\otimes_AA'\).} Indeed, \(M\subset c(M)\) (take
\(\varphi=\varepsilon\)), and \(c(M)\) is contained in every subcomodule \(N\) containing \(M\), since
\(\mu(M)\subset N\otimes C\), which is a submodule of \(V\otimes C\) because \(C\) is flat. Let \(C\) be a direct
summand of a free module \(L\) with basis \((e_\ell)\), \(r\colon L\to C\) the projection, and \(\pi_\ell\in C^\vee\)
the restriction of the \(\ell\)-th coordinate form. For \(x\in M\), \(\mu(x)=\sum_\ell x_\ell\otimes r(e_\ell)\)
(a finite sum) with \(x_\ell=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\pi_\ell)\mu(x)\in c(M)\); so \(c(M)\) is generated by these
\(x_\ell\), which gives the finiteness. For \(y=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\varphi)\mu(x)\), coassociativity gives
\(\mu(y)=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\psi)\mu(x)=\sum_\ell x_\ell\otimes\psi(r(e_\ell))\), where
\(\psi=(\mathrm{id}_C\otimes\varphi)\circ\Delta\); hence \(\mu(c(M))\subset c(M)\otimes C\). The same formula, with
\(\varphi\) replaced by an element of \(\operatorname{Hom}_{A'}(C\otimes_AA',A')=\operatorname{Hom}_A(C,A')\), shows
that \(c(M')\) is generated over \(A'\) by the images of the \(x_\ell\), hence is the image of \(c(M)\otimes_AA'\).
(b) \emph{If \(A\) is noetherian and \(C\) is flat, every finitely generated submodule \(M\) of \(V\) is contained in a
finitely generated subcomodule} (J.-P.~Serre, \emph{Groupes de Grothendieck des sch\'emas en groupes r\'eductifs
d\'eploy\'es}, Publ.\ Math.\ IH\'ES \textbf{34} (1968), 37--52, \S1.5, Prop.~2). Indeed, \(\mu(M)\subset M'\otimes C\)
for some finitely generated submodule \(M'\) of \(V\). Let \(W\) be the kernel of
\(V\xrightarrow{\mu}V\otimes C\to(V/M')\otimes C\). Then \(M\subset W\), and \(W\subset M'\) because
\(v=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\varepsilon)\mu(v)\), so that \(W\) is finitely generated. As \(C\) is flat, \(W\otimes C\) is
the kernel of the map \(V\otimes C\to(V/M')\otimes C\otimes C\) deduced from the previous one, and by
coassociativity this map sends \(\mu(w)\), for \(w\in W\), to the image of \((\mathrm{id}\otimes\Delta)\mu(w)\), which
is zero since \(\mu(w)\in M'\otimes C\). Hence \(\mu(W)\subset W\otimes C\).
(c) \emph{If \(A\to A'\) is faithfully flat and \(C\otimes_AA'\) is a projective \(A'\)-module, then \(C\) is a
projective \(A\)-module}, so that (a) applies: faithfully flat homomorphisms descend projectivity (M.~Raynaud and
L.~Gruson, \emph{Crit\`eres de platitude et de projectivit\'e}, Invent.\ Math.\ \textbf{13} (1971), 1--89, II, 3.1.3,
through the descent of the Mittag-Leffler condition, loc.\ cit., II, 2.5.1; the more general assertion II, 2.5.2 of
loc.\ cit.\ was corrected by L.~Gruson, \emph{Dimension homologique des modules plats sur un anneau
noeth\'erien}, Symposia Mathematica XI (1973), 243--254, which does not affect the faithfully flat case). The
re-edition also notes that the existence of a smallest subcomodule containing \(M\) in this situation can be seen
without this later result: by (a) over \(A'\), the subcomodule \(c(M\otimes_AA')\) of \(V\otimes_AA'\) has the same
two images in \(V\otimes_AA'\otimes_AA'\), so it descends to a submodule of \(V\), which is the smallest subcomodule
containing \(M\), of finite type if \(M\) is, and compatible with base change.

\phantomsection\label{prop:sga3-VIB-11.9}%
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 11.9.---}
\emph{Let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group, quasi-compact and
quasi-separated over \(S\), such that
\(\mathcal A=\mathcal A(G)\) becomes locally free after a
faithfully flat quasi-compact extension of the base \(S\)
\textup{(}which is the case, for example, when \(G\) is a
reductive group, as we shall see in XXV\footnote{Translator's note: the
Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, refers here to XXII,
\SGARefLink{sga3:xxii:cor:5.7.8}{5.7.8}, where a reductive \(S\)-group is shown to be essentially free over
\(S\) in the sense of VIII, 6.1.}\textup{)}, and let
\(\mathcal E\) be a quasi-coherent \(\mathcal O_S\)-module.
Suppose either that \(G\) is affine over \(S\), or that \(G\)
and \(\mathcal E\) are flat over \(S\). Let
\(\rho:\mathcal E\to\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal E\) be a
homomorphism defining an operation of \(G\) on \(\mathcal E\).
Then, for every quasi-coherent sub-\(\mathcal O_S\)-module
\(\mathcal F\) of \(\mathcal E\), there is a smallest
quasi-coherent sub-\(\mathcal O_S\)-module
\(\widetilde{\mathcal F}\) of \(\mathcal E\) containing
\(\mathcal F\) that is stable under \(\rho\). Its formation
commutes with every extension of the base \(S'\to S\). When
\(\mathcal F\) is of finite type, so is
\(\widetilde{\mathcal F}\).}

One reduces easily, by faithfully flat descent, to the case in
which \(\mathcal A\) is a free \(\mathcal O_S\)-module, in which
case the proposition is a consequence of Lemma~\hyperref[lem:sga3-VIB-11.8]{11.8}.

\par\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}; our formulation).
The re-edition states 11.9 for a quasi-coherent \(\mathcal O_S\)-coalgebra \(\mathcal C\), a quasi-coherent
\(\mathcal C\)-comodule \(\mathcal E\) and a quasi-coherent submodule \(\mathcal F\) of \(\mathcal E\), under the
hypothesis that \(\mathcal C\) is \emph{locally projective}, that is, \(\Gamma(U,\mathcal C)\) is a projective
\(\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_S)\)-module for every affine open subset \(U\) of \(S\) (the terminology of Raynaud and Gruson,
loc.\ cit., p.~82). By (c) of the preceding Addition, and because a module over a ring \(A\) is projective as soon as
its restrictions to the members of a finite affine open cover of \(\operatorname{Spec}A\) are (the product of their
rings being faithfully flat over \(A\)), it suffices that \(S\) be covered by affine open subsets \(U_\alpha\) for which
there are faithfully flat homomorphisms \(\Gamma(U_\alpha,\mathcal O_S)\to A'_\alpha\) with
\(\Gamma(U_\alpha,\mathcal C)\otimes A'_\alpha\) projective over \(A'_\alpha\); this covers the hypothesis of 11.9 and
that of J.-P.~Serre in 11.10.1. \emph{Then there is a smallest quasi-coherent subcomodule \(t(\mathcal F)\) of
\(\mathcal E\) containing \(\mathcal F\); it is of finite type if \(\mathcal F\) is, and its formation commutes with
every base change \(S'\to S\).} Indeed, for \(U=\operatorname{Spec}A\) affine, (a) of the preceding Addition gives the
smallest subcomodule \(c(\Gamma(U,\mathcal F))\) of \(\Gamma(U,\mathcal E)\) containing \(\Gamma(U,\mathcal F)\); by its
compatibility with the flat homomorphisms \(A\to\Gamma(U',\mathcal O_S)\), \(U'\subset U\) affine, these modules are
the sections of a quasi-coherent subcomodule of \(\mathcal E\), which is \(t(\mathcal F)\), minimality, finiteness and
compatibility with base change being local questions. The re-edition accordingly states 11.10 with the hypothesis
that \(\mathcal A(G)\) be locally projective.

\phantomsection\label{cor:sga3-VIB-11.10}%
\noindent\textbf{Corollary 11.10.---}
\emph{Let \(S\) be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated scheme,
let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group, quasi-separated and quasi-compact
over \(S\), such that \(\mathcal A(G)\) becomes locally free
after a faithfully flat quasi-compact extension of the base
\(S\), and let \(\mathcal E\) be a quasi-coherent
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module. Suppose either that \(G\) is affine
over \(S\), or that \(G\) and \(\mathcal E\) are flat over
\(S\). Let an operation of \(G\) on \(\mathcal E\) be given.
Then \(\mathcal E\) is the direct limit of an increasing
filtered family of quasi-coherent sub-\(\mathcal O_S\)-modules
of finite type of \(\mathcal E\) stable under \(G\).}

This is an immediate consequence of
\hyperref[prop:sga3-VIB-11.9]{11.9}, since \(\mathcal E\) is the
direct limit of its quasi-coherent submodules of finite type
\textup{(EGA~I, 9.4.9, and EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.7.7)}.
