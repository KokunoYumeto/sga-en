
\medskip
\phantomsection\label{par:sga3-VIA-5.4}%
\noindent\textbf{5.4.---}
Let \(u:F\to G\) be a quasi-compact homomorphism of groups locally of
finite type and flat over \(A\); suppose that the kernel of \(u\) is also
flat over \(A\). In this case, the quotient group
\(\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F\) exists by Theorem~\hyperref[thm:sga3-VIA-3.2]{3.2} and
\hyperref[par:sga3-VIA-5.2]{5.2}; moreover, \(u\) induces a monomorphism
\(v:\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F\to G\)
(\SGARefLink{sga3:iv:remark:5:2:6}{IV, 5.2.6}), and \(\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F\) is flat
over \(A\) by \SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIA-3.2-iv}{3.2~(iv)}. On the other hand,
\(\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F\) is identified with the quotient sheaf of
\(\widehat F\) by \(\operatorname{Ker}\widehat u\) for the \((\mathrm{fpqc})\)
topology of \((\mathbf{Sch}_{/A})\) (cf.\ 5.2); it follows that
\(F\backslash G\) is identified with
\((\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F)\backslash G\), since the quotient sheaf
\(\widehat F\backslash\widehat G\) for the canonical topology is identified with
the quotient sheaf \((\operatorname{Ker}\widehat u\backslash\widehat F)\backslash\widehat G\).
A fortiori \(v\) induces an isomorphism of \(\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F\)
onto the inverse image in \(G\) of the distinguished point of
\(F\backslash G\) (so that \(v\) is a closed immersion; cf.\
VI\textsubscript{B}). When \(F\) and \(G\) are commutative \(A\)-groups,
this inverse image is the image of \(u\) in the category of abelian
\(A\)-groups, whereas \(\operatorname{Ker}u\backslash F\) is the coimage
of \(u\). Hence:

\medskip
\phantomsection\label{thm:sga3-VIA-5.4.2}%
\noindent\textbf{Theorem.---}
\emph{The category of commutative groups of finite type over a field is
abelian.}

Indeed, when \(A\) is a field, \(\operatorname{Ker}u\) is flat over \(A\),
whatever \(u\) may be.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~VI\textsubscript{A}; our
formulation). The re-edition writes 5.4 for a quasi-compact morphism \(u\colon G\to H\) of
\(A\)-groups locally of finite type whose kernel \(N\) is flat over \(A\), without assuming \(G\)
and \(H\) flat, and adds two statements.

\phantomsection\label{prop:sga3-VIA-5.4.1}%
(a) (Its Proposition~5.4.1.) \emph{Such a \(u\) factors as
\(G\xrightarrow{\;p\;}N\backslash G\xrightarrow{\;i\;}H\), where \(p\) is faithfully flat and
locally of finite presentation and \(i\) is a closed immersion.} Indeed, \(N\) is a closed subgroup
of \(G\), the unit section of \(H\) being a closed immersion (0.3); so \(N\backslash G\) exists, and
\(p\) is faithfully flat and locally of finite presentation, by (b) of the Addition at the end of
\S~3. The morphism \(u\) induces a monomorphism \(i\colon N\backslash G\to H\)
(\SGARefLink{sga3:iv:remark:5:2:6}{IV, 5.2.6}), which is quasi-compact because \(u\) is quasi-compact
and \(p\) is surjective; so \(i\) is a closed immersion by (c) of the first Addition after 2.4.1.

(b) (Its Corollary~5.4.3, with the remarks around it.) \emph{Over a field \(k\), the category of
commutative affine algebraic \(k\)-groups is abelian.} It is a full subcategory of the abelian
category of the Theorem above, stable under kernels and cokernels: a closed subgroup of an affine
group is affine, and the quotient of an affine algebraic \(k\)-group by a closed normal subgroup is
affine, by a theorem of Chevalley (Exposé~VI\textsubscript{B}, \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIB-11.17}{11.17}).
It is also stable under extensions: if \(N\) and \(G/N\) are affine, so is \(G\), the projection
\(G\to G/N\) being an \(N\)-torsor (Exposé~VI\textsubscript{B}, \SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIB-9.2-viii}{9.2~(viii)}).
The re-edition adds that the category of all commutative affine \(k\)-groups, not necessarily of
finite type, is abelian (Exposé~VI\textsubscript{B}, \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIB-11.17}{11.17} and
\SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIB-11.18.2}{11.18.2}; M.~Demazure, P.~Gabriel, \emph{Groupes algébriques}, I,
Masson and North-Holland, 1970, III, \S\,3, 7.4), and refers to Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B},
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.4.2}{2.4.2}, for a proof using formal groups.
