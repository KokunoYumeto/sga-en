\noindent\textbf{3.2.4.---}
To complete the proof of Theorem~\hyperref[thm:sga3-VIA-3.2]{3.2} in the case where \(F\) and \(G\) are of finite type over \(A\),
it remains to study the quotient \(F\backslash G\). By
\SGARefLink{lem:sga3-V-6.1}{Expos\'e~V, 6.1}, assertions \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIA-3.2:item:ii}{(ii)}, \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIA-3.2:item:iii}{(iii)}, and \SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIA-3.2-iv}{(iv)} ``become true''
after the base change
\[
  f:\operatorname{Spec}A'\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spec}A.
\]
By EGA~IV\textsubscript{2}, 2.6.1, 2.6.2, and 2.7.1, these assertions
were therefore true before the base change. Finally, to prove the
second assertion of (i), it suffices to refer to \SGARefLink{par:sga3-V-6-c}{Expos\'e~V, \S~6~c)}.
