\phantomsection\label{prop:sga3-VIB-2.5}%
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 2.5.---}
\emph{Let \(u:G\to H\) be a morphism of \(S\)-groups.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}]\phantomsection\label{concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-i} \emph{Suppose that \(G\) is flat and of finite
  presentation over \(S\) at the points of its identity section, and that
  \(H\) is of finite presentation over \(S\) at the points of its identity
  section. Then the set \(U\) of points \(s\in S\) such that \(u_s\) is
  flat (respectively, smooth, \'etale) is open in \(S\).}\footnote{Translator's note: the
Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, requires of \(H\) only that it be of finite
type over \(S\) at the points of its identity section, here and in 2.6. In our formulation: take for
\(Y\) the open set of points at which \(\pi_H\) is locally of finite type, and \(X\) as in the proof
below. Then \(u_X\) is locally of finite presentation, since \(\pi_X=\pi_Y\circ u_X\) is and \(\pi_Y\) is
locally of finite type (EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.4.3~(v)); so the sets of points of \(X\) at which \(u_X\) is flat
(respectively, smooth, \'etale) are open, and as \(X\) is flat and locally of finite presentation over
\(S\) and \(Y\) locally of finite type over \(S\), the fibrewise flatness criterion still applies (The
Stacks Project, Tag 05VK; with EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.5.1 and 17.6.1, for the smooth and \'etale cases).}

  \emph{If, in particular, \(G\) is assumed flat over \(S\), and \(G\) and
  \(H\) locally of finite presentation over \(S\), then the set \(V\) of
  points of \(G\) at which \(u\) is flat (respectively, smooth, \'etale) is
  equal to \(G|_U\).}

\item[\textup{(ii)}]\phantomsection\label{concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-ii} \emph{Suppose that \(u\) is locally of finite
  presentation (respectively, locally of finite type) at the points of the
  identity section of \(G\), and that, for every \(s\in S\), \(G_s\) is
  locally of finite type over \(\kappa(s)\) \textup(a condition satisfied if
  \(G\) is of finite type over \(S\) at the points of the identity section
  \textup{(\SGARefLink{lem:sga3-VIB-1.3.1.1}{1.3.1.1})}\textup). Then the set \(U\) of \(s\in S\) such that
  \(u_s\) is unramified (respectively, locally quasi-finite) is open in
  \(S\).}

  \emph{If, moreover, \(u\) is assumed locally of finite presentation
  (respectively, locally of finite type), then the set \(V\) of points of
  \(G\) at which \(u\) is unramified (respectively, quasi-finite) is equal
  to \(G|_U\).}
\end{enumerate}

We prove \textup{\SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-i}{(i)}}. Note first (\SGARefLink{lem:sga3-VIB-1.3.1.1}{1.3.1.1}) that, for every
\(s\in S\), \(G_s\) is locally of finite type over \(\kappa(s)\). Let \(Y\)
be the set of points of \(H\) at which \(\pi_H\) is of finite
presentation, and let \(X\) be the set of points of \(u^{-1}(Y)\) at which
\(\pi_G\) is flat and of finite presentation. Recall (EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.4.2,
and EGA~IV\(_3\), 11.3.1) that \(X\) (respectively, \(Y\)) is open in \(G\)
(respectively, \(H\)). Let \(u_X:X\to Y\) be the morphism induced by
\(u\). Since \(Y\) (respectively, \(X\)) contains the identity section of
\(H\) (respectively, \(G\)), the restriction \(\pi_X\) of \(\pi_G\) to
\(X\) is surjective, and the morphism \(S\to X\) induced by
\(\epsilon_G\) is an \(S\)-section of \(X\). Let \(V_X\) be the set of
points of \(X\) at which \(u_X\) is flat (respectively, smooth, \'etale).
Let \(x\in X\) and put \(s=\pi_X(x)\); then, by EGA~IV\(_3\), 11.3.10
(respectively, 11.3.10 and EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.5.1, respectively, 11.3.10 and
17.6.1), \(x\) belongs to \(V_X\) if and only if \(u_s\) is flat
(respectively, smooth, \'etale) at the point \(x\), or, what amounts to the
same (\hyperref[prop:sga3-VIB-1.3]{1.3}), if and only if \(u_s\) is flat (respectively, smooth,
\'etale). Consequently,
\[
  U=\epsilon_G^{-1}(V_X)
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  V_X=\pi_X^{-1}(U).
\]
Recall (EGA~IV\(_3\), 11.3.1 (respectively, EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.3.7)) that
\(V_X\) is open in \(X\), hence in \(G\), so that \(U\) is open in \(S\).

The second assertion follows from what we have just seen, for then
\(X=G\), \(Y=H\), \(V_X=V\), so that \(V=\pi_G^{-1}(U)\).

Assertion \textup{\SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-ii}{(ii)}} is proved in the same way, using \hyperref[prop:sga3-VIB-1.3]{1.3} and the
fact that, for an \(S\)-morphism \(u:X\to Y\) locally of finite
presentation (respectively, locally of finite type) to be unramified
(respectively, quasi-finite) at a point \(x\in X\), it is necessary and
sufficient that, if \(s\) denotes the projection of \(x\) on \(S\), \(u_s\)
be unramified (respectively, quasi-finite) at the point \(x\)
(EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.4.2, and Err\(_{\mathrm{III}}\)~20).

\phantomsection\label{cor:sga3-VIB-2.6}%
\noindent\textbf{Corollary 2.6.---}
\emph{Let \(u:G\to H\) be a morphism of \(S\)-groups that is a
radicial morphism (as is the case if \(u\) is a monomorphism
\textup(EGA~I, 3.5.4\textup)). Suppose that \(G\) and \(H\) are locally
of finite presentation over \(S\) and that \(G\) is flat over \(S\).
Then the set \(U\) of \(s\in S\) such that
\(u_s\) is an open immersion is open in \(S\), and the restriction
of \(u\) over \(U\) is an open immersion.}

By \hyperref[concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-i]{2.5}\textup{\SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-i}{(i)}}, the set \(U'\) of points \(s\in S\) such that
\(u_s\) is \'etale is open in \(S\). Since \(u\) is radicial, so
is \(u_s\), for every \(s\in S\); hence EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.9.1,
gives
\[
  U=U',
\]
which shows that \(U\) is open. Finally, by \hyperref[concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-i]{2.5}\textup{\SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIB-2.5-i}{(i)}}, the
restriction of \(u\) over \(U\) is \'etale; since \(u\) is
radicial, this restriction is an open immersion
(EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.9.1).

\phantomsection\label{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7}%
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 2.7.---}
\emph{Let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group. The following conditions are
equivalent:}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{\SGAInlineRefTarget{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:i}(i)}] \emph{\(G\) is unramified over \(S\) at the
  points of the identity section;}
\item[\textup{\SGAInlineRefTarget{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:ii}(ii)}] \emph{the identity section is an open
  immersion;}
\item[\textup{\SGAInlineRefTarget{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:iii}(iii)}] \emph{\(G\) is of finite presentation over
  \(S\) at the points of the identity section, and, for every
  \(s\in S\), \(G_s\) is unramified over \(\kappa(s)\).}
\end{enumerate}
\emph{If, moreover, \(G\) is locally of finite presentation over
\(S\), then each of the three preceding conditions is equivalent
to the following:}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(iv)}] \emph{\(G\) is unramified over \(S\).}
\end{enumerate}

The equivalence of assertions \textup{\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:i}{(i)}} and \textup{\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:ii}{(ii)}}
follows from the more general \hyperref[lem:sga3-VIB-2.7.1]{Lemma~2.7.1} below. Note (\SGARefLink{lem:sga3-VIB-1.3.1.1}{1.3.1.1})
that either condition \textup{\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:i}{(i)}} or condition \textup{\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:iii}{(iii)}}
implies that, for every \(s\in S\), \(G_s\) is locally of finite
type over \(\kappa(s)\). Thus, by EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.4.1, assertion
\textup{(i)} is equivalent to the assertion that, for every
\(s\in S\), \(G_s\) is unramified over \(\kappa(s)\) at the point
\(e_s\), the identity of \(G_s\), or, equivalently by \hyperref[cor:sga3-VIB-1.3.1]{1.3.1}, to
the assertion that \(G_s\) is unramified over \(\kappa(s)\).
Consequently, assertions \textup{\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:i}{(i)}} and \textup{\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-2.7:item:iii}{(iii)}} are
equivalent. Finally, if \(G\) is locally of finite presentation
over \(S\), assertions \textup{(iii)} and \textup{(iv)} are
equivalent (EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.4.1).

\phantomsection\label{lem:sga3-VIB-2.7.1}%
\noindent\textbf{Lemma 2.7.1.---}
\emph{Let \(G\) be an \(S\)-scheme equipped with a section
\(\epsilon\). In order that \(G\) be unramified over \(S\) at the
points of this section, it is necessary and sufficient that
\(\epsilon\) be an open immersion.}

We already know that the condition is necessary (EGA~IV\(_4\), 17.4.1,
\textup{a)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{b\(^{\prime\prime}\)})).
Conversely, if \(\epsilon\) is an open immersion, then the
restriction to \(\epsilon(S)\) of the structural morphism
\[
  G\longrightarrow S
\]
is an isomorphism; hence \(G\) is unramified over \(S\) at the
points of \(\epsilon(S)\).
