\noindent\textbf{4.5.---}
We have therefore constructed \(F\backslash G\). The other
assertions of \hyperref[thm:sga3-VIA-3.2]{Theorem~3.2} reduce directly to assertions about the
groupoids \(\mathscr S(G^\alpha_*)\). As in Expos\'e~V, \S~6, it suffices
to prove \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIA-3.2:item:ii}{(ii)}, \SGARefLink{thm:sga3-VIA-3.2:item:iii}{(iii)} and \SGARefLink{concl:sga3-VIA-3.2-iv}{(iv)}; by SGA~1, VIII, it suffices to verify the
corresponding assertions for the groupoid
\(\mathscr S(G^\alpha_*)\otimes_AA'\), which is isomorphic to the direct sum of finitely many copies of
\(\mathscr S(G^0_*)\otimes_AA'\) (cf.\ \hyperref[par:sga3-VIA-4.4]{4.4}), so that we are reduced to the
groupoid \(\mathscr S(G^0_*)\). For this last groupoid we continue to
follow the proof given in Expos\'e~V, \S~6, as we began to do in \SGARefLink{par:sga3-VIA-4.3}{4.3}.

\phantomsection\label{par:sga3-VIA-4.6}%
\noindent\textbf{4.6.---}
Let us conclude this section with some remarks concerning \SGARefLink{lem:sga3-V-6.1}{Lemma~6.1}
and \S~9~a) of Expos\'e~V: under the hypotheses and with the notation
of Expos\'e~V, \S~9~a), we seek a condition under which formation of
the cokernel of the \((\mathrm{Sch}/S)\)-groupoid \(X_*\) commutes
with a base extension
\[
  \pi:S'\longrightarrow S.
\]
The cokernels of \(X_*\) and \(X'_*\) are identified with the
cokernels of the groupoids \(U_*\) and \(U'_*\) induced by \(X_*\)
and \(X'_*\) on the quasi-sections \(U\) and \(U'\), respectively.
We are therefore reduced to the case of a
\((\mathrm{Sch}/S)\)-groupoid satisfying the hypotheses of
\SGARefLink{thm:sga3-V-4.1}{Theorem~V, 4.1}. If \(g:U_0\to Y\) is the canonical morphism of
\(U_0=U\) onto the cokernel \(Y\) of \(U_*\), we want the sequence of
\(\mathcal O_{Y'}\)-modules
\[
  \tag{*}\label{eq:sga3-VIA-4.6-star}
  \mathcal O_{Y'}
    \longrightarrow g'_*(\mathcal O_{U'_0})
    \rightrightarrows
  (gu_1)'_*(\mathcal O_{U'_1})
    =
  (gu_0)'_*(\mathcal O_{U'_1})
\]
to be exact. \emph{If \(S\) is locally Noetherian and \(X_0\) of finite type
over \(S\), or if \((d_0,d_1)\) is an equivalence pair}, it follows from
EGA~III\textsubscript{1}, 1.4.15, that the sequence \eqref{eq:sga3-VIA-4.6-star} is identified with
the inverse image of the sequence
\[
  \tag{**}\label{eq:sga3-VIA-4.6-double-star}
  \mathcal O_Y
    \longrightarrow g_*(\mathcal O_{U_0})
    \rightrightarrows
  g_*u_{1*}(\mathcal O_{U_1})
    =
  g_*u_{0*}(\mathcal O_{U_1})
\]
whenever \(S'\) is flat over \(S\). Since \eqref{eq:sga3-VIA-4.6-double-star} is an exact sequence,
we see that \emph{the construction of the cokernel of \(X_*\) commutes with
flat extensions of the base under the hypotheses mentioned}.\footnote{Translator's note: the printing cites ``EGA III 1.4.5''; flat base
change is EGA~III\textsubscript{1}, 1.4.15. The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~VI\textsubscript{A},
observes that the extra hypotheses are not needed: under those of Theorem~V, 4.1, \(u_0\) and \(u_1\)
are finite and locally free and \(g\) is integral (V, 4.1~(ii)), so that \(g\), \(gu_0\) and \(gu_1\)
are affine; their direct images of structure sheaves commute with every base change, and the inverse
image of the exact sequence (**) under the flat morphism \(Y\times_SS'\to Y\) is exact. (The re-edition
also uses that the cokernel of \(U'_*\) maps homeomorphically onto \(Y\times_SS'\), V, \S~9~a).) It
concludes that the formation of the cokernel of \(X_*\) commutes with flat base change whenever
\(X_*\) has quasi-sections ``locally'' (cf.\ the proof of Theorem~V, 7.1).}
