\phantomsection\label{prop:sga3-VIB-3.7}%
\noindent\textbf{Proposition 3.7.---}
\emph{Let \(G\) be an \(S\)-group locally of finite presentation; then
\(\underline{G^0}\) is ind-constructible in \(G\). If \(G\) is assumed
quasi-separated over \(S\), and \(S\) quasi-compact and quasi-separated,
then \(\underline{G^0}\) is constructible. Consequently, if \(G\) is
quasi-separated over \(S\), \(\underline{G^0}\) is locally
constructible.}

We first prove the first assertion. Since \(\pi\) is locally of finite
presentation, given \(s\in S\), there are an open subset \(U\) of \(G\)
containing \(\epsilon(s)\) and an open subset \(V\) of \(S\) containing
\(s\) such that \(\pi(U)\subset V\) and the morphism \(\pi':U\to V\)
induced by \(\pi\) is of finite presentation. Put
\[
  T=\epsilon^{-1}(U)
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  W=\pi'^{-1}(T).
\]
Then the morphism \(\pi'':W\to T\) induced by \(\pi'\) is of finite
presentation, and admits as a section the morphism
\(\epsilon'':T\to W\) induced by \(\epsilon\). For every \(t\in T\),
\(\underline{G_t^0}\) being irreducible (Expos\'e~VI\(_A\), \SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIA-2.4}{2.4}),
\(W\cap\underline{G_t^0}\) is dense in \(\underline{G_t^0}\), hence
irreducible, hence connected: it is the connected component of
\(\pi''^{-1}(t)\) containing \(\epsilon''(t)\). The union \(W^0\) of the
\(W\cap\underline{G_t^0}\), for \(t\in T\), is locally constructible in
\(W\), by EGA~IV\(_3\), 9.7.12. But it follows from
Expos\'e~VI\(_A\), \SGARefLink{sga3:VIA:0.5}{0.5}, that \(W^0\cdot W^0\) is equal to
\(\underline{G^0}\cap\pi^{-1}(T)\). Now, since \(\pi\) is locally of finite
presentation, so is \(\mu\) (Expos\'e~VI\(_A\), \SGARefLink{sga3:VIA:0.1}{0.1}), and hence so is
the morphism
\[
  \mu'':W\times_T W\longrightarrow\pi^{-1}(T)
\]
induced by \(\mu\). Consequently
\[
  \underline{G^0}\cap\pi^{-1}(T)=\mu''(W^0\times_T W^0)
\]
is ind-constructible in \(\pi^{-1}(T)\) (EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.9.5(viii)), since,
\(W^0\) being locally constructible in \(W\), it is clear that
\(W^0\times_T W^0\) is locally constructible in \(W\times_T W\). This
shows that \(\underline{G^0}\) is ind-constructible in \(G\).

Suppose now that \(S\) is quasi-compact and quasi-separated and that
\(G\) is quasi-separated over \(S\); then (\hyperref[prop:sga3-VIB-3.5]{3.5}) there is a
quasi-compact open subset \(U\) of \(G\) containing
\(\underline{G^0}\). Since
\(G\) is quasi-separated over \(S\), \(G\) is quasi-separated;
hence the open subset \(U\) is retrocompact (EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.2.7),
and it suffices to show that \(\underline{G^0}\) is constructible
in \(U\)
(EGA~0\(_{\mathrm{III}}\), 9.1.8).

Moreover, \(U\) is quasi-compact, hence quasi-compact over \(S\)
(EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.2.4), and is quasi-separated over \(S\).
Therefore the restriction of \(\pi\) to \(U\) is of finite
presentation. By EGA~IV\(_3\), 9.7.12, \(\underline{G^0}\) is locally
constructible in \(U\), hence constructible in \(U\), since \(U\)
is quasi-compact and quasi-separated (EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.8.1).\footnote{Translator's note: the last
assertion of 3.7 follows from the second, applied to the restrictions of \(G\) over the affine open
subsets of \(S\). The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, also obtains it from the
first part of the proof: if \(G\) is quasi-separated over \(S\), the morphism \(\mu''\) is quasi-compact
(EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.2.4) and quasi-separated, hence of finite presentation, so that by Chevalley's theorem
(EGA~IV\(_1\), 1.8.4) the image \(\underline{G^0}\cap\pi^{-1}(T)\) of the locally constructible set
\(W^0\times_TW^0\) is locally constructible.}

\phantomsection\label{cor:sga3-VIB-3.8}%
\noindent\textbf{Corollary 3.8.---}
\emph{Let \(S_0\) be a quasi-compact and quasi-separated scheme,
\(I\) an increasing filtered preordered set,
\((\mathcal A_i)_{i\in I}\) a direct system of commutative
quasi-coherent \(\mathcal O_{S_0}\)-algebras,}
\[
  \mathcal A=\varinjlim_i\mathcal A_i,\qquad
  S_i=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A_i\quad(i\in I),\qquad
  S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathcal A
\]
\emph{\textup{(cf.\ EGA~II, 1.3.1)}. Let \(G\) be an
\(S_0\)-group scheme locally of finite presentation. Then the
canonical map}
\[
  \varinjlim_i G^0(S_i)\longrightarrow G^0(S)
\]
\emph{is bijective.}

Since \(G\) is locally of finite presentation over \(S_0\),%
\begingroup
\renewcommand{\thefootnote}{\fnsymbol{footnote}}%
\footnote[1]{\label{note:sga3-VIB-3.8-base}Translator's note: the
French source prints ``over \(S\)'' here. The scheme \(G\) was
defined over \(S_0\), while \(S\) and the \(S_i\) are the test
schemes of the direct system; the required base is therefore
\(S_0\).}%
\endgroup{}
the canonical map
\[
  \varinjlim_i G(S_i)\longrightarrow G(S)
\]
is bijective (EGA~IV\(_3\), 8.14.2~c)). It follows immediately
that the canonical map
\[
  \varinjlim_i G^0(S_i)\longrightarrow G^0(S)
\]
is injective. We prove that it is surjective. Let
\[
  g\in G^0(S)\subset G(S).
\]
There is an \(i\in I\) such that \(g\) factors, by means of an
element \(g_i\in G(S_i)\), through \(S\to S_i\). By hypothesis,
\[
  g^{-1}(\underline{G^0})=S.
\]
But \(\underline{G^0}\) is ind-constructible in \(G\), by \hyperref[prop:sga3-VIB-3.7]{3.7}, so
\[
  g_i^{-1}(\underline{G^0})
\]
is ind-constructible in \(S_i\). It then follows from EGA~IV\(_3\),
8.3.4, that there is an index \(j\geq i\) such that
\[
  g_j^{-1}(\underline{G^0})=S_j,
\]
where \(g_j\) is the map induced by \(g_i\) under the base change
\(S_j\to S_i\). Thus \(g_j\in G^0(S_j)\), and hence \(g\) comes
from an element of
\[
  \varinjlim_i G^0(S_i).
\]
