
\subsection*{2.5}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.5}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{2.5. Étale formal groups}

A formal \(k\)-group is said to be \emph{étale} if its underlying
formal variety is étale \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.6}{1.6})}; these formal groups have a very
simple structure. \emph{Suppose} indeed that \(k\) is \emph{local}; let
\(\kappa\) be the residue field of \(k\), \(\kappa_s\) a separable
closure of \(\kappa\), and \(\Gamma\) the topological Galois group of
\(\kappa_s\) over \(\kappa\). We call \emph{\(\Gamma\)-set} the datum of a
set \(E\) and of a continuous action of \(\Gamma\) on \(E\) \textup{(}the
isotropy group of an element \(x\in E\) is then an open subgroup of
\(\Gamma\)\textup{)}. Finally, with every formal variety \(X\) over \(k\)
we associate the inductive limit
\[
  X(\kappa_s)
  =
  \varinjlim_{\ell}X(\ell)
\]
of the sets \(X(\ell)\), \(\ell\) ranging over the finite extensions of
\(\kappa\) contained in \(\kappa_s\). It is clear that \(\Gamma\) acts
continuously on \(X(\kappa_s)\), and it follows from SGA~1, I~8.3, that
the functor \(X\mapsto X(\kappa_s)\) is an equivalence of the category
of étale formal varieties over \(k\) with that of \(\Gamma\)-sets.\footnote{Translator's
note: in more detail (the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, writes this out; our
formulation). An element of \(X(\kappa_s)\) comes from some \(X(\ell)\) with \(\ell\) finite and Galois over
\(\kappa\), on which \(\Gamma\) acts through the finite group \(\operatorname{Gal}(\ell/\kappa)\); hence the
continuity. A morphism \(\operatorname{Spf}\ell\to X\) factors uniquely through
\(X_\kappa=X\widehat\otimes_k\kappa\), so that \(X(\ell)=X_\kappa(\ell)\). By SGA~1, I~8.3, applied modulo
each open ideal of \(k\), \(X\mapsto X_\kappa\) is an equivalence between the étale formal varieties over
\(k\) and over \(\kappa\); the latter are the sums of spectra of finite separable extensions of
\(\kappa\), that is, the étale \(\kappa\)-schemes, which Galois theory identifies with the
\(\Gamma\)-sets by \(Y\mapsto Y(\kappa_s)\) (SGA~1, V, \S\S~7--8).}

\medskip
\noindent
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.5:proposition}
{\itshape This functor \(X\mapsto X(\kappa_s)\) therefore induces an
equivalence of the category of étale formal groups over \(k\)
\textup{(}local\textup{)} with that of \(\Gamma\)-groups,} that is, of the
groups of the category of \(\Gamma\)-sets \textup{(}such a
\(\Gamma\)-group thus consists of an abstract group \(G\) together with
a continuous action of \(\Gamma\) on \(G\) by group automorphisms\textup{)}.

\par\medskip\noindent\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:VIIB:2.5.A}%
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}; our formulation). Let \(k\) be a field,
\(k_s\) a separable closure of \(k\), \(\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(k_s/k)\),
and let \(G\) be an étale formal \(k\)-group, with \(M=G(k_s)\). The
\(\Gamma\)-orbits in \(M\) correspond to the points of \(G\): to the
orbit of \(x\), whose stabilizer \(\Gamma_x\) is open, corresponds a point
with residue field \(L_x=k_s^{\Gamma_x}\), of degree \([\Gamma:\Gamma_x]\)
over \(k\). Thus the affine algebra \(\mathcal A(G)\) is the product, with
the product topology, of these fields, one for each orbit, and
\(\mathbf H(G)=\mathcal A(G)^\dagger\) is the direct sum of their duals,
which are its simple subcoalgebras. Hence \(\mathbf H(G)\) is
\emph{pointed}, that is, all its simple subcoalgebras have dimension
\(1\), if and only if \(\Gamma\) acts trivially on \(M\), or again if all
residue fields of \(G\) are equal to \(k\). In that case
\(\mathcal A(G)=k^M\), \(\mathbf H(G)\) is the group algebra \(kM\) of
the group \(M=G(k)\), and \(M\) is the set of the \(x\in\mathbf H(G)\)
such that \(\varepsilon(x)=1\) and \(\Delta(x)=x\otimes x\).
