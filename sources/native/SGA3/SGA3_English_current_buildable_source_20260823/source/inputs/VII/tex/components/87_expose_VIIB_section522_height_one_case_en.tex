
\subsubsection*{5.2.2}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:5.2.2}

Suppose first that \(B\) has height at most \(1\), that is, that
\(x^p=0\) if \(x\in I\). It then follows from \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:5.1.4}{5.1.4} that the
graded algebra \(\operatorname{Gr}_I(B)\) associated with \(B\) by the
filtration
\[
  I\supset\overline{I^2}\supset\overline{I^3}\supset\cdots
\]
is endowed with a cogroup structure of the category
\(\mathbf{Alpg}/k\). If one puts
\[
  C=\prod_{n\geq0}\operatorname{Gr}_I(B)_n,
\]
the structural morphism of \(\operatorname{Gr}_I(B)\) into
\(\operatorname{Gr}_I(B)\widehat\otimes_k\operatorname{Gr}_I(B)\)
induces a diagonal morphism \(\Delta_C:C\to C\widehat\otimes_kC\) which
makes \(C\) the affine algebra of a formal group \(K\) over \(k\). It
is clear that \(\omega_{K/k}=I/\overline{I^2}\) and that \(K\) is
radicial of height at most \(1\). By \SGARefLink{sga3:viib:subsection:4-4}{4.4}, the identity map of
\(\omega_{K/k}\) therefore induces an isomorphism of
\[
  k[[\omega_{K/k}]]
  \big/
  \bigl((x^p)\bigr)_{x\in\omega_{K/k}}
\]
onto \(C\). This implies in particular that the map \(\varphi\) of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:5.2.1:section-proposition}{5.2.1} induces an isomorphism on the graded algebras associated
with \(B'\) and \(B\) when \(B'\) and \(B\) are filtered by the powers
of their maximal ideals. Hence \(\varphi\) is an isomorphism
\textup{([CA], V, \S\,7, Lemma~1)}.
