\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Lemma 5.1.5.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:5.1.5:filtered-groups-lemma}
{\itshape Let \(B\) be an abelian group, and let
\[
  B=B'_0\supset B'_1\supset B'_2\supset\cdots
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  B=B_0\supset B_1\supset B_2\supset\cdots
\]
be two separated filtrations of \(B\) by subgroups such that
\(B'_m\subset B_m\) for every \(m\). If the canonical map of
\(B'_m/B'_{m+1}\) into \(B_m/B_{m+1}\) is surjective for every \(m\),\footnote{Translator's
note: the 1970 text has \(B'_m/B'_{m+2}\) here, which gives the same condition, both maps having image
\((B'_m+B_{m+1})/B_{m+1}\), and, in the proof, ``the inclusion of \(B_m\) in \(B_m\)''.}
and if \(B\) is complete for the topology defined by the filtration
\((B'_m)\), then \(B'_m=B_m\) for every \(m\).}

Apply Lemma~1 of \textup{[CA]}, V, \S\,7, to the inclusion of
\(B'_m\) in \(B_m\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, states this lemma of P.~Gabriel, \emph{Des cat\'egories ab\'eliennes},
Bull.\ Soc.\ Math.\ France \textbf{90} (1962), 323--448; in our words: if \(f:M\to N\) is a homomorphism of
abelian groups filtered by decreasing sequences of subgroups, compatible with the filtrations, \(M\) being
separated and complete and \(N\) separated, and if the induced map of graded groups is surjective (resp.\
injective), then \(f\) is surjective (resp.\ injective); see also N.~Bourbaki, \emph{Alg\`ebre commutative},
chap.~III, \S\,2, no.~8. Here \(M=B'_m\), filtered by the \(B'_{m+i}\), is complete, being open in \(B\),
and \(N=B_m\) is filtered by the \(B_{m+i}\).}

\medskip
\noindent\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, 5.1--5.1.5; our formulation). The
re-edition proves Proposition 5.1 for an arbitrary pseudocompact ring
\(k\), assuming that \(A\) and the quotients
\(\overline{J^n}/\overline{J^{n+1}}\) \((n\geq0)\), where
\(J=\overline{AI_B}\), are topologically flat, that is, projective
pseudocompact \(k\)-modules (0.3.7). The proof of 5.1.1--5.1.5 applies
in this case, once the lemma of 5.1.2 is known in the following form:
\emph{let \(U\) and \(V\) be pseudocompact \(k\)-modules filtered by
decreasing sequences of closed submodules, with \(U_0=U\) and
\(V_0=V\); if every \(U_i/U_{i+1}\) is topologically flat, then
\(W_n/W_{n+1}\simeq\bigoplus_{i+j=n}(U_i/U_{i+1})\widehat\otimes_k
(V_j/V_{j+1})\).} Indeed, the \(U_i/U_{i+1}\) being projective, the
exact sequences \(0\to U_{i+1}\to U_i\to U_i/U_{i+1}\to0\) split, and
the argument given in the note to that lemma goes through, the
complements \(S_i\simeq U_i/U_{i+1}\) being topologically flat. Then
\((C,J)\mapsto\operatorname{Gr}_J(C)\) commutes with finite direct sums
on the pairs for which \(C\) and the \(\overline{J^n}/\overline{J^{n+1}}\)
are topologically flat; 2.7 applies to \(\operatorname{Spf}(A/J)\),
\(A/J\) being topologically flat; the quotient \(F\backslash G\) exists
(2.4); and 5.1.3, 5.1.4 and 5.1.5 use only the decompositions
\(A_n\simeq A_0\widehat\otimes_kA'_n\), reduction modulo the augmentation
ideal of \(A_0\), and lemmas on filtered abelian groups.
