
\subsubsection*{2.4.2}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.4.2}

Suppose now that \(H\) and \(G\) are topologically flat over \(k\).
Since \(G\) is topologically flat over \(k\) and over \(G/H\), \(G/H\)
is topologically flat over \(k\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.3}{1.3.3})}; on the other hand, it
is clear that
\[
  \operatorname{Coker}(f)
  =
  \operatorname{Coker}(f');
\]
since \(f'\) is a monomorphism, the canonical projection \(q\) of \(K\)
onto \(\operatorname{Coker}(f')\) is topologically flat, and \(f'\) is
an isomorphism of \(G/H\) onto \(\ker(q)\).\footnote{Translator's note: this is
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.4}{2.4} applied to the monomorphism \(f'\colon G/H\to K\), whose source is topologically
flat over \(k\) by what precedes; the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, gives
this reference.} If \(\ker(f)\) and \(G\) are
topologically flat over \(k\), we therefore see that
\[
  \operatorname{Coim}(f)=\operatorname{Im}(f);
\]
whence:

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Theorem.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.4.2:theorem}
{\itshape The commutative formal groups over a field form an abelian
category.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Corollary.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.4.2:corollary}
{\itshape The commutative affine group schemes over a field form an
abelian category.}\footnote{Translator's note: compare Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{A},
\SGARefLink{par:sga3-VIA-5.4}{5.4}, where the commutative groups of finite type over a field are shown to form an
abelian category, and the Addition that follows it there, on commutative affine groups; the Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, refers here to the remarks after its VI\textsubscript{A}, 5.4.3.}

This follows from \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.2}{2.2.2}.
