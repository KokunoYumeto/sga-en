
\subsection*{\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:viib:subsection:4-4}4.4 Theorem}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:4.4}

\noindent\textbf{Theorem 4.4.}
{\itshape Let \(p\) be a prime number, \(k\) a pseudocompact ring of
characteristic \(p\), and \(G\) a formal group over \(k\). The following
assertions are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textup{(i)}] There exists a \(p\)-Lie algebra \(\mathbf L\),
flat over \(\mathcal O_k\), such that \(G\) is isomorphic to
\(\mathscr G_p(\mathbf L)\).

\item[\textup{(ii)}] There exists a projective pseudocompact
\(k\)-module \(\omega\) such that the affine algebra of \(G\) is
isomorphic to the quotient of \(k[[\omega]]\) by the closed ideal
generated by the elements \(x^p\), \(x\in\omega\).

\item[\textup{\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:viib:subsection:4-4:item:iii}(iii)}] The group \(G\) has height at most \(1\), and
\(\omega_{G/k}\) is a projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module.

\item[\textup{(iv)}] The group \(G\) has height at most \(1\), and
is topologically flat over \(k\).
\end{enumerate}}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B},
adds that in (i) \(\mathbf L\) is then isomorphic to \(\operatorname{Lie}(G)\) (4.2.2), and that in (ii)
\(\omega\) is isomorphic to \(\omega_{G/k}\): the ideal generated by the \(x^p\) lies in the closure of the
square of the augmentation ideal of \(k[[\omega]]\), so that the augmentation ideal of the quotient has
\(\omega\) as its quotient by the closure of its square.}

\noindent\textit{Proof of \textup{(i)} \(\boldsymbol{\Rightarrow}\)
\textup{(ii)}.}
Let \(\omega\) be the pseudocompact \(k\)-module dual to \(\mathbf L\)
\textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}{1.2.3})}, and let \(A\) be the quotient of
\(k[[\omega]]\) by the closed ideal generated by the elements
\(x^p\), \(x\in\omega\). For every profinite \(k\)-algebra of finite
length \(C\), a continuous homomorphism
\[
  h:A\longrightarrow C
\]
is determined by its restriction \(h'\) to \(\omega\); this
restriction \(h'\) maps \(\omega\) into \(\sqrt[p]{0_C}\). One thus
obtains a canonical bijection from
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Alp}/k}(A,C)
\]
onto the set
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{c}\bigl(\omega,\sqrt[p]{0_C}\bigr)
\]
of continuous \(k\)-linear maps from \(\omega\) into
\(\sqrt[p]{0_C}\). The latter set is canonically isomorphic to
\[
  \mathbf L(C)\otimes_C\sqrt[p]{0_C}
\]
\textup{(see the proof of \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:3.3:cartier-theorem}{3.3})}. The implication
\textup{(i)} \(\Rightarrow\) \textup{(ii)} therefore follows from
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:4.3.1}{4.3.1}.

For the remaining implications, consult the analogous proofs of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:3.3:cartier-theorem}{Theorems~3.3} and VIIA~\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:7.4.1}{7.4.1}.


\subsubsection*{4.4.1 Corollary}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:4.4.1}

\noindent\textbf{Corollary 4.4.1.}
{\itshape If \(k\) is a field of characteristic \(p>0\), the functor
\[
  \mathbf L\longmapsto\mathscr G_p(\mathbf L)
\]
is an equivalence from the category of \(p\)-Lie algebras over
\(k\) to the category of formal \(k\)-groups of height at most
\(1\).}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, adds
that if \(k\) is an Artinian ring of characteristic \(p>0\), the same proof gives an equivalence between the
category of \(p\)-Lie algebras flat over \(k\) and that of formal \(k\)-groups of height at most \(1\) which
are topologically flat over \(k\) (by 4.4, (i) \(\Leftrightarrow\) (iv), and 4.2.2).}

Indeed, as \(G\) ranges over the formal groups of height at most
\(1\), the functor
\[
  G\longmapsto\mathscr G_p\bigl(\operatorname{Lie}G\bigr)
\]
is isomorphic to the identity functor, by \SGARefLink{sga3:viib:subsection:4-4}{Theorem~4.4}; likewise, we
have seen that the functor
\[
  \mathbf L\longmapsto
  \operatorname{Lie}\mathscr G_p(\mathbf L)
\]
is isomorphic to the identity functor \textup{(confer VII\textsubscript{A},
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:7.3}{7.3})}.\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text has ``VII\textsubscript{A} 7.5'', a
number that does not exist in Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{A}. The statement meant is VII\textsubscript{A}, 7.3,
for \(\mathbf L\) finite-dimensional, and in general its extension in the
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:5.5.3}{Addition after VII\textsubscript{A}, 5.4.1}, (b), to which the Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, refers (as its VII\textsubscript{A}, 5.5.3).}
