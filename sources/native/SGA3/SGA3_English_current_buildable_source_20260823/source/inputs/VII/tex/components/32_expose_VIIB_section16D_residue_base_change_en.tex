\noindent\textbf{(c)}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.6.E}
\emph{The conditions of the text.} The following conditions on \(X\)
are equivalent:
\textup{(i)} \(A\) is formally étale over \(k\);
\textup{(ii)} \(X\) is étale, that is, topologically flat over \(k\),
with \(\Delta_X\) a local isomorphism;
\textup{(iii)} for every \(x\in X\), \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\) is isomorphic
to \(k_s[T]/(F)\) for some monic separable \(F\in k_s[T]\);
\textup{(iv)} \(X\) is topologically flat over \(k\), and every
\(\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(s)\) is a finite separable
extension of \(\kappa(s)\);
\textup{(v)} for every open ideal \(\mathfrak l\) of \(k\), the local
components of \(A\widehat\otimes_k(k/\mathfrak l)\) are finite étale
\((k/\mathfrak l)\)-algebras.

The local components of \(A\widehat\otimes_k(k/\mathfrak l)\) are the
nonzero \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_k(k/\mathfrak l)\), algebras
over the local factor \(k_s/\mathfrak lk_s\) of \(k/\mathfrak l\); so
\textup{(b)} gives \textup{(i)}\(\Leftrightarrow\)\textup{(v)}.

\textup{(v)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{(iii)}. Fix \(x\). For every open
ideal \(\mathfrak l\neq k_s\) of \(k_s\), the algebra
\(\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_{k_s}(k_s/\mathfrak l)\) is local,
finite étale over \(k_s/\mathfrak l\), with residue field
\(\kappa(x)\); for \(\mathfrak l=\mathfrak n_s\) this shows that
\(\kappa(x)\) is separable over \(\kappa(s)\). Let \(F\in k_s[T]\) be
monic, lifting the minimal polynomial of a primitive element of
\(\kappa(x)\) over \(\kappa(s)\). By the end of \textup{(b)}, there are
isomorphisms
\((k_s/\mathfrak l)[T]/(F)\simeq
\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_{k_s}(k_s/\mathfrak l)\) inducing the
identity of \(\kappa(x)\); they are unique, by \textup{(a)}, hence
compatible with one another, and in the limit they give
\(k_s[T]/(F)\simeq\mathcal O_{X,x}\). Indeed, every pseudocompact
\(k_s\)-module \(M\) is the projective limit of the
\(M\widehat\otimes_{k_s}(k_s/\mathfrak l)\), since every quotient of
\(M\) of finite length is annihilated by an open ideal of \(k_s\).

\textup{(iii)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{(v)} follows from \textup{(a)}, applied
over the rings \(k_s/\mathfrak lk_s\).

\textup{(iii)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{(ii)}. Each \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\) is
free of finite rank over \(k_s\), a direct factor of \(k\); so \(A\),
the product of the \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\), is a projective pseudocompact
\(k\)-module \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.2.1}{0.2.1})}, that is, \(X\) is topologically flat
over \(k\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.5}{1.3.5})}. Put \(E=\mathcal O_{X,x}\). The local ring of
\(X\times X\) at \(\Delta_X(x)\) is the local component of
\(E\widehat\otimes_kE=E\otimes_{k_s}E\) at the kernel of
\(E\otimes_{k_s}E\to E\to\kappa(x)\). Since \(E\) is étale over
\(k_s\), the diagonal of \(\operatorname{Spec}E\) is an open immersion
\textup{(EGA~\(\mathrm{IV}_4\), 17.4.2)}, as well as a closed one; so the
multiplication \(E\otimes_{k_s}E\to E\) is the projection onto a direct
factor, which is local. Hence that local component is \(E\), and
\(\Delta_X\) is a local isomorphism.

\textup{(ii)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{(iv)}. Fix \(x\), put
\(B_0=\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(s)\), and let \(K\) and
the local components \(B'\) of \(B_0\otimes_{\kappa(s)}K\) be as in
\textup{(b)}, with \(\kappa=\kappa(s)\) and \(L=\kappa(x)\). The
condition on \(\Delta_X\) is preserved by the base changes
\(k\to\kappa(s)\to K\), localization commuting with them; so the
multiplication \(B'\widehat\otimes_KB'\to B'\) becomes an isomorphism
after localization at the diagonal point. But
\(B'\widehat\otimes_KB'\) is local: its quotient by the closed ideal
generated by \(\mathfrak n'\widehat\otimes B'\) and
\(B'\widehat\otimes\mathfrak n'\), an ideal of topologically nilpotent
elements, is \(K\otimes_KK=K\). So the multiplication
\(B'\widehat\otimes_KB'\to B'\) is injective. For
\(a\in\mathfrak n'\), it maps \(a\widehat\otimes1\) and
\(1\widehat\otimes a\), which lie in the distinct summands
\(\mathfrak n'\widehat\otimes K\) and \(K\widehat\otimes\mathfrak n'\)
of \((K\oplus\mathfrak n')\widehat\otimes_K(K\oplus\mathfrak n')\), to
the same element; hence \(\mathfrak n'=0\), and one concludes as in
\textup{(b)} that \(B_0=\kappa(x)\), separable over \(\kappa(s)\).

\textup{(iv)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{(iii)}. Fix \(x\). Then
\(\mathcal O_{X,x}\), a direct factor of \(A\), is a projective
pseudocompact \(k_s\)-module, and
\(\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(s)=\kappa(x)\) is separable
over \(\kappa(s)\). With \(F\) as in
\textup{(v)}\(\Rightarrow\)\textup{(iii)}, the residue map
\(k_s[T]/(F)\to\kappa(x)\) lifts, by \textup{(a)}, to a continuous
morphism \(u:k_s[T]/(F)\to\mathcal O_{X,x}\), the maximal ideals of the
quotients of finite length of \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\) being nilpotent; and
\(u\) is an isomorphism by \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.3.4}{0.3.4}, since \(u\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(s)\)
is one.

We write \(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\) for the full
subcategory of \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\) formed by the étale formal
varieties, as in the text below. The re-edition defines these varieties
by condition \textup{(i)}.
