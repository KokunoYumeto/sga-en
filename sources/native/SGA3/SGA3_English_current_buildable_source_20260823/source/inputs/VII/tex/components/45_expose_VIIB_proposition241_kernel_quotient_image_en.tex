
\subsubsection*{2.4.1}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.4.1}

Let \(f:G\to K\) be a homomorphism of formal \(k\)-groups, \(H\) the
kernel of \(f\), and \(u\) the inclusion of \(H\) in \(G\). If \(H\) is
topologically flat over \(k\), the homomorphism \(f':G/H\to K\) induced
by \(f\) is a monomorphism: this is a consequence of the results of
Exposé~IV;\footnote{Translator's note: see Exposé~IV,
\SGARefLink{sga3:iv:remark:5:2:6}{5.2.6}; the reference is added in the Gille--Polo re-edition
(2011), Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}.} we nevertheless give a direct proof. Let \(T\) be a formal
variety of finite length over \(k\) and \(t\) an element of
\((G/H)(T)\) such that \(f'\circ t\) is the unit element of \(K(T)\).
We must show that \(t\) is the unit element of \((G/H)(T)\). Since the
canonical projection \(\operatorname{pr}_1\) of \(T\times_{G/H}G\) onto
\(T\) is surjective and topologically flat \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.4}{2.4})}, it
suffices to show \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.1}{1.3.1})} that \(t\circ\operatorname{pr}_1\) is
the unit element of \((G/H)(T\times_{G/H}G)\). Since
\(t\circ\operatorname{pr}_1=p\circ\operatorname{pr}_2\), where
\(\operatorname{pr}_2\) is the canonical projection of
\(T\times_{G/H}G\) onto \(G\), the equality
\[
  1=f'\circ(p\circ\operatorname{pr}_2)=f\circ\operatorname{pr}_2
\]
and the exact sequence

show that \(\operatorname{pr}_2\) factors through \(H\), hence that
\(p\circ\operatorname{pr}_2\) is null.

\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.4.1:corollary}%
If \(B\), \(C\) and \(D\) are the affine algebras of \(G\), \(K\) and
\(G/H\), then \(p:G\to G/H\) therefore induces an injection of \(D\)
into \(B\), and \(f'\) a surjection of \(C\) onto \(D\)
\textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.prop}{1.3})}. Consequently, \(D\) is the image of \(C\) in \(B\).
