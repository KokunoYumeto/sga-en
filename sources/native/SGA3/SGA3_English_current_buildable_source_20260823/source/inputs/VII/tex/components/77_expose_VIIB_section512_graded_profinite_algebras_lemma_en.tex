
\subsubsection*{5.1.2}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:5.1.2}

Denote by \(\mathbf{Alpg}/k\) the category of ``graded profinite
\(k\)-algebras'': an object of this category consists of a sequence
\[
  A_0,A_1,\ldots,A_n,\ldots
\]
of linearly compact vector spaces over \(k\) and of a profinite algebra
structure on the product
\[
  \prod_{n\geq0}A_n
\]
such that
\[
  A_n\cdot A_m\subset A_{m+n}
\]
\textup{(\(A_n\) is identified with a part of \(\prod_{i\geq0}A_i\) by
means of the canonical injection)}; a morphism
\[
  \psi:(A_n)\longrightarrow(B_n)
\]
is a sequence of continuous linear maps
\[
  \psi_n:A_n\longrightarrow B_n
\]
such that
\[
  \psi_{m+n}(a\cdot a')=\psi_m(a)\cdot\psi_n(a')
  \qquad\text{if }a\in A_m\text{ and }a'\in A_n.
\]

It is clear that two graded profinite \(k\)-algebras \((A_n)\) and
\((B_n)\) have a \emph{direct sum in \(\mathbf{Alpg}/k\)} and that this
direct sum has as \(n\)-th component the product
\[
  \prod_{i=0}^{n}
  A_i\widehat\otimes_k B_{n-i}
\]
of the linearly compact \(k\)-vector spaces
\(A_i\widehat\otimes_k B_{n-i}\). This direct sum will be denoted by
\[
  (A_n)\widehat\otimes_k(B_n).
\]

One can associate with every object \((C,J)\) of \(\mathcal C\) the
graded profinite algebra \(\operatorname{Gr}_J(C)\) of \(C\) for the
filtration defined by the closures \(\overline{J^n}\) of the powers of
\(J\); one thus has
\[
  \operatorname{Gr}_J(C)_n
  =
  \overline{J^n}/\overline{J^{n+1}},
\]
and the multiplication of \(\operatorname{Gr}_J(C)\) is induced by that of
\(C\). Moreover, it follows from the lemma below that the functor
\((C,J)\mapsto\operatorname{Gr}_J(C)\) commutes with finite direct sums;
it therefore transforms a cogroup of \(\mathcal C\) into a cogroup of
\(\mathbf{Alpg}/k\). One sees in particular that, with the notation of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:5.1.1}{5.1.1}, the graded profinite algebra \(\operatorname{Gr}_I(A)\) is
endowed with a cogroup structure in \(\mathbf{Alpg}/k\):

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Lemma.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:5.1.2:filtration-lemma}
{\itshape Let \(U_0\supset U_1\supset\cdots\) and
\(V_0\supset V_1\supset\cdots\) be two separated filtered linearly
compact \(k\)-vector spaces. Filter the completed tensor product
\(W=U\widehat\otimes_kV\) by means of the closed subspaces
\[
  W_n
  =
  U_n\widehat\otimes_kV_0
  +
  U_{n-1}\widehat\otimes_kV_1
  +\cdots+
  U_0\widehat\otimes_kV_n.
\]
For every \(n\), the canonical maps of
\((U_i/U_{i+1})\widehat\otimes_k(V_j/V_{j+1})\) into \(W_n/W_{n+1}\)
\((i+j=n)\) define an isomorphism
\[
  \bigoplus_{i+j=n}
  (U_i/U_{i+1})\widehat\otimes_k(V_j/V_{j+1})
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  W_n/W_{n+1}.
\]}\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text leaves the proof to the reader (and prints
\(U_j/U_{j+1}\) for \(V_j/V_{j+1}\) in the last display). Here is one. Choose closed complements \(S_i\) of
\(U_{i+1}\) in \(U_i\) for \(i\leq n\), which exist by duality (0.2.2). Then
\(U=S_0\oplus\cdots\oplus S_n\oplus U_{n+1}\) and \(U_i=S_i\oplus\cdots\oplus S_n\oplus U_{n+1}\); as
\(\widehat\otimes_k\) is exact and commutes with finite direct sums,
\(W_n=\bigoplus_{i\leq n}S_i\widehat\otimes_kV_{n-i}\oplus U_{n+1}\widehat\otimes_kV\) and
\(W_{n+1}=\bigoplus_{i\leq n}S_i\widehat\otimes_kV_{n+1-i}\oplus U_{n+1}\widehat\otimes_kV\), whence
\(W_n/W_{n+1}\simeq\bigoplus_{i\leq n}S_i\widehat\otimes_k(V_{n-i}/V_{n+1-i})\), with \(S_i\simeq
U_i/U_{i+1}\). The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, proves the lemma over a
pseudocompact ring (see the Addition after 5.1.5).}
