
\subsubsection*{1.6.1}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.6.1}

Let \(Y\) be an étale formal \(k\)-variety and let
\(f:X\to Y\) be a morphism of \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\). The graph
morphism \(\Gamma_f:X\to X\times Y\), with components
\(\operatorname{id}_X\) and \(f\), is then the inverse image of the
diagonal morphism \(\Delta_Y:Y\to Y\times Y\) under the base change
\(f\times\operatorname{id}_Y:X\times Y\to Y\times Y\). It follows that
\(\Gamma_f\) is a local isomorphism, hence that
\[
  f=\operatorname{pr}_2\circ\Gamma_f
\]
is topologically flat if \(\operatorname{pr}_2\) is, for example if
\(X\) is topologically flat over \(k\). Conversely, since \(Y\) is
topologically flat, \(X\) is topologically flat over \(k\) if \(X\) is
so over \(Y\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.3}{1.3.3})}. Taking in particular for \(f\) the
canonical morphism \(p:X\to X_e\) of \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.6}{1.6}, one sees that \(X\) is
topologically flat over \(X_e\) if and only if \(X\) is so over \(k\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}, moves here, as a Remark~1.6.2, the first part of
2.5.2 below: when \(k\) is a perfect field, \(X\mapsto X_e\) is also right
adjoint to the inclusion of \(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\) in
\(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\). It adds two observations. Being a right adjoint,
\(X\mapsto X_e\) then commutes with the projective limits that exist in
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\), among them the finite ones
\textup{(Addition to 1.6, (f))}. For finite products this can also be seen
directly: as \(k\) is perfect, \(\mathcal O_{X_e,x}=\kappa(x)\), the affine
algebra of \(X_e\) is the quotient of that of \(X\) by its radical, and, for
points \(x\) of \(X\) and \(x'\) of another formal \(k\)-variety \(X'\), the
quotient of \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_k\mathcal O_{X',x'}\) by its
radical is \(\kappa(x)\otimes_k\kappa(x')\), which is reduced because
\(\kappa(x)\) is separable over \(k\).}
