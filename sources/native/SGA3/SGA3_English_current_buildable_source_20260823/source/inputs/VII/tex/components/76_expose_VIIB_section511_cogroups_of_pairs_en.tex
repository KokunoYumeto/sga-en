
\subsubsection*{5.1.1}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:5.1.1}

Consider first the following category \(\mathcal C\): an object of
\(\mathcal C\) is a pair \((C,J)\) formed by a profinite \(k\)-algebra
\(C\) and a closed ideal \(J\) of \(C\); a morphism
\[
  \psi:(C,I)\longrightarrow(D,J)
\]
of \(\mathcal C\) is a continuous homomorphism of the algebra \(C\) into
the algebra \(D\) which maps \(I\) into \(J\). If one associates with
\((C,I)\) the pair
\[
  \bigl(\operatorname{Spf}(C/I),\operatorname{Spf}C\bigr),
\]
one obviously obtains an antiequivalence of \(\mathcal C\) onto the
category of pairs \((Z,Y)\) formed by a formal \(k\)-variety \(Y\) and a
formal subvariety \(Z\).

A cogroup structure on an object \((C,J)\) of \(\mathcal C\) consists
of a formal group structure on \(\operatorname{Spf}C\) such that the
following conditions are satisfied \textup{(notation of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.1}{2.1})}:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  \textup{(1)}\quad&
  \Delta_CJ
  \subset
  J\widehat\otimes_kC
  +
  C\widehat\otimes_kJ;
  \\
  \textup{(2)}\quad&
  \varepsilon_C(J)=0;
  \\
  \textup{(3)}\quad&
  c_C(J)\subset J.
\end{aligned}
\]
These conditions also mean that \(\operatorname{Spf}(C/J)\) is a formal
subgroup of \(\operatorname{Spf}C\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, adds that a morphism of cogroups \((C',J')\to(C,J)\) then amounts to a
homomorphism of formal groups \(\operatorname{Spf}C\to\operatorname{Spf}C'\) mapping
\(\operatorname{Spf}(C/J)\) into \(\operatorname{Spf}(C'/J')\).}

It follows from \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.7:prop}{2.7} that conditions (2) and (3) are a
consequence of (1) when \(C\) is local, that is, when
\(\operatorname{Spf}C\) is an infinitesimal group, and \(J\neq C\).\footnote{Translator's
note: the 1970 text does not state the condition \(J\neq C\); for \(J=C\), condition (1) holds and (2)
does not. When \(J\neq C\), \(J\) lies in the maximal ideal of \(C\), which is the kernel of
\(\varepsilon_C\), whence (2); then (1) and (2) make \(\operatorname{Spf}(C/J)\) an infinitesimal formal
submonoid, which is a group by 2.7, whence (3). The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, adds the condition and this argument. In the application below, \(I\) is
contained in \(I_A\).} In
particular, if one puts \(I=\overline{AI_B}\), condition \textup{(i)} of
Proposition 5.1 entails the relation
\[
  \Delta_AI_B
  \subset
  I\widehat\otimes_kA+A\widehat\otimes_kI,
\]
whence
\[
  \Delta_AI
  \subset
  I\widehat\otimes_kA+A\widehat\otimes_kI,
\]
so that \(\Delta_A\) induces a diagonal morphism on \(A/I\) which makes
\(A/I\) the affine algebra of a formal subgroup \(F\) of \(G\).
