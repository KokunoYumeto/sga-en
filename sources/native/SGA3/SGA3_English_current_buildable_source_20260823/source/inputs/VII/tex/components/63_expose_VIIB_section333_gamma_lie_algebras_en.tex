
\subsubsection*{3.3.3}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:3.3.3}

Continue to suppose that \(k\) is a field of characteristic \(0\).
Let \(\overline k\) be the algebraic closure of \(k\), and let
\(\Gamma\) be the topological Galois group of \(\overline k\) over
\(k\).

For every formal \(k\)-group \(G\), denote by
\(\mathbf{Aut}_k(G)\) the \(k\)-group functor assigning to every
finite-dimensional commutative \(k\)-algebra \(C\) the group of
automorphisms of the formal \(C\)-group
\[
  G\widehat\otimes_k C.
\]
This \(k\)-functor is manifestly left exact \textup{(\(G\) is topologically
flat over \(k\)!)}, so that we can treat it as a formal group over
\(k\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, details
this; in our words: let \(H\) be the covariant bialgebra of \(G\) (2.2.1). For \(C\) in
\(\operatorname{Alf}/k\), an endomorphism of the formal \(C\)-group \(G\widehat\otimes_kC\) amounts to a
\(k\)-linear map \(H\to H\otimes_kC\) satisfying the identities that express compatibility with the
multiplications, units, comultiplications and counits. As \(H\) is flat over \(k\), the functor
\(C\mapsto H\otimes_kC\) commutes with finite projective limits, hence so does the functor
\(\mathbf{End}_k(G)\), which is therefore a formal \(k\)-variety (1.1 and 0.4.2). Then \(\mathbf{Aut}_k(G)\)
is the fiber product of the morphism \((u,v)\mapsto(u\circ v,v\circ u)\) of
\(\mathbf{End}_k(G)\times\mathbf{End}_k(G)\) into itself with the unit section (cf.\ Expos\'e~I,
\SGARefLink{sga3:i:remark:1-7-3}{1.7.3}), hence a formal \(k\)-group.}
If \(L\) is a \(k\)-Lie algebra and
\(G=\mathscr G(L)\), \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:3.3:cartier-theorem}{Theorem~3.3} shows that
\(\mathbf{Aut}_k(G)\) is isomorphic to the \(k\)-group functor
\(\mathbf{Aut}_k(L)\) which assigns to each finite-dimensional
\(k\)-algebra \(C\) the group of automorphisms of the
\(C\)-Lie algebra
\[
  C\otimes_k L.
\]

If \(G\) is an arbitrary formal \(k\)-group, we saw in \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.2:proposition}{2.5.2}
that \(G\) decomposes canonically as a semidirect product of an
étale formal group \(G_e\) and an infinitesimal formal group
\(G_0\). This semidirect product is determined by the data of
\(L=\operatorname{Lie}(G)\), of the \(\Gamma\)-group
\(G_e(\overline k)\), and of a homomorphism
\[
  \Phi:
  G_e\longrightarrow
  \mathbf{Aut}_k(G_0)
  \simeq
  \mathbf{Aut}_k(L).
\]
Such a homomorphism sends \(G_e\) into the ``étale part''
\(\mathbf{Aut}_k(L)_e\) of \(\mathbf{Aut}_k(L)\)
\textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.1:corollary}{2.5.1})}. It is therefore determined by the data of a
homomorphism of \(\Gamma\)-groups
\[
  \Phi(\overline k):
  G(\overline k)\longrightarrow
  \bigl(\mathbf{Aut}_kL\bigr)(\overline k).
\]

If one lets \(\Gamma\) act on \(L\otimes_k\overline k\) by means of the
formula \(\gamma(\ell\otimes t)=\ell\otimes\gamma(t)\), the homomorphism
\(\Phi(\overline k)\) makes \(G(\overline k)\) act by automorphisms on
the \(\overline k\)-Lie algebra \(L\otimes_k\overline k\) in such a way
that
\[
  \gamma(g)(\ell)
  =
  \gamma\bigl(g(\gamma^{-1}\ell)\bigr)
\]
if \(g\in G(\overline k)\), \(\gamma\in\Gamma\), and
\(\ell\in L\otimes_k\overline k\). We express this last condition by
saying that \(G(\overline k)\) acts on \(L\otimes_k\overline k\)
\emph{compatibly with \(\Gamma\)}.

The situation can be summarized in a ``highbrow'' statement. Call a
\emph{\(\Gamma\)-Lie algebra over \(k\)} the data of a triple
\[
  (L,M,\phi)
\]
consisting of a Lie algebra \(L\) over \(k\), a \(\Gamma\)-group \(M\),
and an action \(\phi\) of \(M\) on \(L\otimes_k\overline k\) that is
``compatible with the action of \(\Gamma\) on \(M\) and
\(L\otimes_k\overline k\).'' If \((L,M,\phi)\) and
\((L',M',\phi')\) are two such \(\Gamma\)-Lie algebras, a homomorphism
from the first to the second is a pair \((f,\theta)\) consisting of a
morphism \(f:L\to L'\) of Lie algebras and a morphism
\(\theta:M\to M'\) of \(\Gamma\)-groups such that
\[
  (f\otimes_k\overline k)(m\cdot x)
  =
  \theta(m)\cdot(f\otimes_k\overline k)(x)
\]
if \(x\in L\otimes_k\overline k\) and \(m\in M\). Then the functor
\[
  G\longmapsto
  \bigl(
    \operatorname{Lie}G,
    G(\overline k),
    \Phi(\overline k)
  \bigr)
\]
is an equivalence from the category of formal groups over \(k\)
\textup{(\(k\) a field of characteristic~\(0\))} to the category of
\(\Gamma\)-Lie algebras.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, remarks that for \(k\) algebraically closed, \(\Gamma\) being then trivial, this
gives the Cartier--Gabriel--Kostant theorem: a cocommutative Hopf algebra \(H\) over \(k\) is the semidirect
(smash) product of the enveloping algebra of the Lie algebra of its primitive elements and of the group
algebra of the group of its group-like elements.}
