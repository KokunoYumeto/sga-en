
\medskip
\noindent\SGAInlineRefTarget{sga3:vii:paragraph:1-2-4}\textbf{1.2.4.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.4}
For every pseudocompact \(k\)-module \(E\), the \(\mathbf O_k\)-module
\(\mathbf V^f_k(E)\) is naturally isomorphic to the functor
\[
  C\longmapsto\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Alp}/k}\bigl(\widehat S_k(E),C\bigr),
\]
where \(\widehat S_k(E)\) denotes the \emph{completed symmetric algebra} of
\(E\), which can be constructed as follows. Let \(T_k(E)\) be the direct
sum of the abelian groups
\[
  \widehat{\bigotimes}_{k}^{\,n}E
  =
  E\widehat{\otimes}_{k}\cdots\widehat{\otimes}_{k}E
  \qquad
  \left(
    n\geq0;\quad
    \widehat{\bigotimes}_{k}^{\,0}E=k
  \right);
\]
one makes \(T_k(E)\) a graded \(k\)-algebra by defining the
multiplication in the usual way. Let then \(S_k(E)\) be the quotient of
\(T_k(E)\) by the homogeneous ideal whose \(n\)-th component is the
closed \(k\)-submodule of \(\widehat{\bigotimes}_{k}^{\,n}E\) generated by
the elements
\[
\begin{aligned}
&x_1\widehat{\otimes}\cdots\widehat{\otimes}x_i
 \widehat{\otimes}x_{i+1}\widehat{\otimes}\cdots
 \widehat{\otimes}x_n
\\[-0.1em]
&\qquad{}-
x_1\widehat{\otimes}\cdots\widehat{\otimes}x_{i+1}
 \widehat{\otimes}x_i\widehat{\otimes}\cdots
 \widehat{\otimes}x_n.
\end{aligned}
\]
If \(S_k^n(E)\) is the quotient of \(\widehat{\bigotimes}_{k}^{\,n}E\) by
this submodule, \(S_k(E)\) is plainly a graded \(k\)-algebra whose
\(n\)-th component is \(S_k^n(E)\); the profinite algebra
\(\widehat S_k(E)\) is the separated completion of \(S_k(E)\) for the
linear topology defined by the ideals \(\mathfrak l\) such that
\(S_k(E)/\mathfrak l\) is a \(k\)-module of finite length and
\(S_k^n(E)\cap\mathfrak l\) is an open submodule of \(S_k^n(E)\) for
every \(n\).\footnote{Translator's note: for example, let \(k\) be a field. Then \(E\) is the dual
\(V^*\) of the vector space \(V=E^\dagger\) (0.2.2), and \(\widehat S_k(E)\) is the product, over the
closed points \(x\) of the union of the affine spaces \(\operatorname{Spec}S_k(W^*)\) attached to the
finite-dimensional subspaces \(W\) of \(V\), of the projective limits over \(W\) of the completed local
rings of these affine spaces at \(x\). If \(k\) is algebraically closed and \((e_i)\) is a pseudobasis of
\(E\), each factor is the ring of formal power series in the \(e_i\), with the topology defined by the
ideals of finite codimension. The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, gives this
description.}

In all that follows, we identify the formal spectrum of
\(\widehat S_k(E)\) with the \(k\)-functor \(\mathbf V^f_k(E)\)
(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}{1.2.3}).

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{1.2.5.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.5}
By \SGARefLink{sga3:vii:paragraph:1-2-4}{1.2.4}, the zero morphism from \(E\) to \(k\) corresponds to a
morphism of profinite algebras
\[
  \pi:\widehat S_k(E)\longrightarrow k.
\]
The morphism \(\pi\) induces the zero map on \(S_k^n(E)\) for
\(n\geq1\), and defines a section of the structure morphism
\[
  \mathbf V^f_k(E)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Spf}(k).
\]

We denote by \(\mathbf V^{f,0}_k(E)\) the formal variety whose points
are the images \(x\) of the points \(s\) of \(\operatorname{Spf}(k)\) under
the section \(\operatorname{Spf}(\pi)\), and which has the same local
algebras as \(\mathbf V^f_k(E)\) at these points \(x\). It is clear that
the affine algebra of \(\mathbf V^{f,0}_k(E)\) is the infinite product
\[
  k[[E]]
  =
  k\times E\times S_k^2(E)\times S_k^3(E)\times\cdots.
\]
The multiplication of \(k[[E]]\) is induced by that of \(S_k(E)\), and
there is a natural isomorphism
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Alp}/k}(k[[E]],C)
  \simeq
  \operatorname{Hom}_c\bigl(E,r(C)\bigr)
\]
when \(C\) ranges over the \(k\)-algebras of finite length and \(r(C)\)
denotes the radical of \(C\).\footnote{Translator's note: a continuous homomorphism
\(\phi\colon\widehat S_k(E)\to C\) with \(C\) of finite length corresponds to \(u=\phi|_E\in
\operatorname{Hom}_c(E,C)\); it factors through \(k[[E]]\) if and only if \(\phi\) vanishes on
\(S^n_k(E)\) for large \(n\), that is, if and only if \(u(E)\) consists of nilpotent elements, i.e.\ lies
in \(r(C)\), whose powers vanish. The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, spells
this out.}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{1.2.6.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.6}
A formal \(k\)-variety \(V\) is said to be \emph{of finite length} if
its affine algebra is. Similarly, if \(S\) is a scheme, an \(S\)-scheme
\(X\) is said to be \emph{of finite length} if \(X\) is finite over \(S\)
and the direct image of \(\mathcal O_X\) on \(S\) is an
\(\mathcal O_S\)-module of finite length.\footnote{Translator's note: such an \(X\) thus amounts to finitely
many closed points \(s_1,\ldots,s_n\) of \(S\) together with an \(\mathcal O_{S,s_i}\)-algebra of finite
length for each \(i\); and the formal scheme \(\widehat S\) below is the formal spectrum (1.1) of the
pseudocompact ring \(\mathcal A(\widehat S)=\prod_s\widehat{\mathcal O}_{S,s}\), with the product topology.
The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, adds these descriptions.} The
\(S\)-schemes of finite length identify with the formal varieties of
finite length over the following formal scheme \(\widehat S\): the
underlying topological space of \(\widehat S\) is the set of closed
points of \(S\), endowed with the discrete topology; if \(s\) is such a
closed point, the structure sheaf \(\mathcal O_{\widehat S}\) has as
stalk \(\mathcal O_{\widehat S,s}\) at \(s\) the separated completion
\(\widehat{\mathcal O}_{S,s}\) of \(\mathcal O_{S,s}\) for the linear
topology defined by the ideals of finite colength.

Let \(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\ell f}/\widehat S\) be the category of
formal varieties of finite length over \(\widehat S\) (identified with
the category of \(S\)-schemes of finite length). By \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.1}{1.1} and
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.1}{1.2.1}, the category \(\operatorname{Vaf}/\widehat S\) of formal
varieties over \(\widehat S\) is equivalent to the category of
left-exact contravariant functors from
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\ell f}/\widehat S\) to \((\mathbf{Sets})\). For
example, if \(X\) is an \(S\)-scheme, the functor
\[
  T\longmapsto
  \operatorname{Hom}_{(\operatorname{Sch}/S)}(T,X)
\]
is such a left exact functor; it is therefore naturally isomorphic to a
functor
\(T\mapsto\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vaf}/\widehat S}(T,\widehat X/\widehat S)\),
where \(\widehat X/\widehat S\) is a formal variety over \(\widehat S\)
which can be described as follows: the underlying set consists of the
points \(x\) of \(X\) such that the residue field \(\kappa(x)\) of \(X\)
at \(x\) is a finite extension of the residue field \(\kappa(s)\) of the
image \(s\) of \(x\) in \(S\); the stalk of
\(\mathcal O_{\widehat X/\widehat S}\) at \(x\) is the separated
completion of the local ring \(\mathcal O_{X,x}\) for the linear topology
defined by the ideals of finite colength.
