
\medskip
\noindent\textbf{1.2.3.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}
\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3.B}%
Let \(N\) be a pseudocompact \(k\)-module. We write
\(\mathbf V_k^f(N)\), or \(\mathbf N^\dagger\), for the
\(\mathbf O_k\)-module which assigns to every
\(C\in\operatorname{Ob}(\operatorname{Alf}/k)\) the \(C\)-module
\[
  (C\widehat{\otimes}_kN)^\dagger
\]
dual to \(C\widehat{\otimes}_kN\) (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.2.2}{0.2.2}); this
\(\mathbf O_k\)-module \(\mathbf V_k^f(N)\) will be called the
\emph{dual} of \(N\). It is clear that \(\mathbf V_k^f(N)\) is flat
(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.1}{1.2.1}) when \(N\) is a projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module.

\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3.D}%
Conversely, if \(\mathbf M\) is an \(\mathbf O_k\)-module such that every
morphism \(C\to D\) of \(\operatorname{Alf}/k\) induces a bijection from
\(D\otimes_C\mathbf M(C)\) onto \(\mathbf M(D)\),\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text defines the
dual (written \(\mathbf M^*\)) for every \(\mathbf O_k\)-module \(\mathbf M\). The condition, added in the
Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, where such modules are called
\emph{admissible}, is needed to define the projective system: for open ideals
\(\mathfrak l'\subset\mathfrak l\) it identifies \(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)^*\) with
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{k/\mathfrak l'}(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l'),k/\mathfrak l)\), which receives
\(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l')^*\) by composition with \(k/\mathfrak l'\to k/\mathfrak l\). Flat
\(\mathbf O_k\)-modules satisfy it.} we call \emph{dual} of \(\mathbf M\),
and denote by \(\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)\), the projective limit of the
pseudocompact modules \(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)^*\) over
\(k/\mathfrak l\) (\(\mathfrak l\) ranging over the open ideals of \(k\)).
\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3.E}%
If \(\mathbf M\) is a flat \(\mathbf O_k\)-module, it is clear that
\(\mathbf M\) is none other than the dual of \(\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)\), so
that the functors \(N\mapsto\mathbf V_k^f(N)\) and
\(\mathbf M\mapsto\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)\) define an anti-equivalence from
the category of projective pseudocompact \(k\)-modules to the category of
flat \(\mathbf O_k\)-modules (cf.\ \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.2.2}{0.2.2}).

\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3.F}%
Moreover, if \(M\) is a topologically free pseudocompact \(k\)-module,
let \((m_i)\) be a pseudobasis of \(M\) (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.2.1}{0.2.1}) and let \(m_i^C\) be
the canonical image of \(m_i\) in \(C\widehat{\otimes}_kM\)
(\(C\in\operatorname{Alf}/k\)). If one defines the element \(n_i^C\) of
\((C\widehat{\otimes}_kM)^\dagger\) by
\[
  n_i^C(m_i^C)=1,
  \qquad
  n_i^C(m_j^C)=0\quad(i\ne j),
\]
the family \((n_i^C)\) is a basis of \(\mathbf V_k^f(M)(C)\); moreover,
for every morphism \(\varphi:C\to D\) of \(\operatorname{Alf}/k\),
\(\mathbf V_k^f(M)(\varphi)\) sends \(n_i^C\) to \(n_i^D\): one can
therefore choose bases of the \(C\)-modules \(\mathbf V_k^f(M)(C)\)
``coherently.''


\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}; our formulation).
\emph{Proof of the anti-equivalence, and its compatibility with base change.}
\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3.A}%
(a) Let \(k\to K\) be a morphism of pseudocompact rings, \(B\) a pseudocompact \(K\)-module and \(M\) a
projective \(k\)-module (without topology). Give \(M^*=\operatorname{Hom}_k(M,k)\) the topology of 0.2.2,
and \(\operatorname{Hom}_k(M,B)\) the topology of pointwise convergence, for which the sets
\(\{f\mid f(x_1),\ldots,f(x_s)\in B'\}\), with \(x_j\in M\) and \(B'\) an open submodule of \(B\), form a
basis of neighborhoods of \(0\); let \(M^*\widehat\otimes_kB\) be the separated completion of
\(M^*\otimes_kB\) for the topology defined as in 0.5, the open submodules of \(B\) taking the place of the
open ideals of \(\ell\). The canonical map \(M^*\widehat\otimes_kB\to\operatorname{Hom}_k(M,B)\),
\(f\otimes b\mapsto(x\mapsto f(x)b)\), is then an isomorphism of pseudocompact \(K\)-modules: it is one
for \(M=k\), and both sides transform direct sums in \(M\) into products.

(b) For a projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module \(N\), the natural map
\(N\to\Gamma^*(\mathbf V_k^f(N))\) is an isomorphism: both sides commute with products, and for a local
component \(N=k_{\mathfrak m}\) and an open ideal \(\mathfrak l\subset\mathfrak m\), the dual over
\(k/\mathfrak l\) of \(k_{\mathfrak m}\widehat\otimes_kk/\mathfrak l=(k/\mathfrak l)_{\mathfrak m}\) is
\((k/\mathfrak l)_{\mathfrak m}\), whose dual is again \((k/\mathfrak l)_{\mathfrak m}\). Conversely, let
\(\mathbf M\) be a flat \(\mathbf O_k\)-module. Each \(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)\) is then a projective
\(k/\mathfrak l\)-module, so that the transition maps of the system \((\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)^*)\) are the
reductions modulo the ideals \(\mathfrak l/\mathfrak l'\). Hence \(\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)\) is projective, by
(i) and (ii) of the Addition in 0.3: for \(P\) of finite length, annihilated by an open ideal
\(\mathfrak l_0\), \(\operatorname{Hom}_c(\Gamma^*(\mathbf M),P)\) is the inductive limit over
\(\mathfrak l\subset\mathfrak l_0\) of the \(\operatorname{Hom}_c(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)^*,P)\), which are
exact in \(P\), each \(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)^*\) being a projective pseudocompact \(k/\mathfrak l\)-module
(0.2.2). Moreover, if \(C\) is in \(\operatorname{Alf}/k\) and \(\mathfrak l_0\) is an open ideal of
\(k\) mapping to \(0\) in \(C\), then \((k/\mathfrak l_0)\widehat\otimes_k\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)=\mathbf
M(k/\mathfrak l_0)^*\) (0.3.5), and (a) gives
\(C\widehat\otimes_k\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}_{k/\mathfrak l_0}(\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak
l_0),C)=\operatorname{Hom}_C(\mathbf M(C),C)\), whose dual is \(\mathbf M(C)\) (0.2.2, \(C\) being Artinian
and \(\mathbf M(C)\) projective). So \(\mathbf M\simeq\mathbf V_k^f(\Gamma^*(\mathbf M))\).

(c) Let \(k\to K\) be a morphism of pseudocompact rings, \(\mathbf M\) a flat \(\mathbf O_k\)-module, and
\(\mathbf M_K\) the \(\mathbf O_K\)-module \(C\mapsto\mathbf M(k/\mathfrak l)\otimes_{k/\mathfrak l}C\),
where \(\mathfrak l\) is any open ideal of \(k\) mapping to \(0\) in \(C\). The same computation, with the
\(K/I\) (\(I\) an open ideal of \(K\)) in place of \(C\), gives
\(\Gamma^*_K(\mathbf M_K)\simeq\Gamma^*(\mathbf M)\widehat\otimes_kK\): the anti-equivalence commutes with
base change.
