
\subsection*{2.3.2}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.3.2}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.3.2:prop}{2.3.2}}

Let us now suppose, for simplicity, that the ring \(k\) is Artinian.
When \(G\) is topologically flat over \(k\), the algebra
\[
  \mathbf H(G)=\mathbf H(G)(k)
\]
may be characterized by a universal property \textup{(\textsc{Cartier})}:
let \(U\) be a \(k\)-algebra \textup{(associative, with unit element)}
and \(U_m\) the \(k\)-functor in groups that associates with every
\(k\)-algebra \(C\) of finite length the multiplicative group of the
invertible elements of \(U\otimes_kC\); identify \(G\) with the
\(k\)-functor in groups
\[
  C\longmapsto
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vaf}/k}
    \bigl(\operatorname{Spf}(C),G\bigr),
\]
and denote by
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-}\operatorname{Gr.}}(G,U_m)\) the set of
homomorphisms of \(k\)-functors in groups from \(G\) to \(U_m\). We have
the following.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Proposition.}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.3.2:prop}
{\itshape For every formal group \(G\) topologically flat over an
Artinian ring \(k\), and every \(k\)-algebra \(U\), there is a
canonical isomorphism
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-}\operatorname{Gr.}}(G,U_m)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-}\operatorname{Alg.}}
    \bigl(\mathbf H(G),U\bigr).
\]}

Indeed, let \(X\) be a formal variety over \(k\), \(M\) a
\(k\)-module, and \(\mathbf W(M)\) the \(k\)-functor
\(C\mapsto M\otimes_kC\); moreover, let
\(\operatorname{Hom}_k\bigl(X,\mathbf W(M)\bigr)\) denote the set of
morphisms of \(k\)-functors from \(X\) to \(\mathbf W(M)\). If \(A\) is
the affine algebra of \(X\) and \(\mathfrak l\) runs through the open
ideals of \(A\), there are canonical isomorphisms
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_k\bigl(X,\mathbf W(M)\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \varprojlim_{\mathfrak l}
  \operatorname{Hom}_k\bigl(\operatorname{Spf}(A/\mathfrak l),\mathbf W(M)\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  \varprojlim_{\mathfrak l}M\otimes_k(A/\mathfrak l)
\]
and linear maps
\[
  M\otimes_k(A/\mathfrak l)
  \longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Hom}_k\bigl((A/\mathfrak l)^\dagger,M\bigr)
\]
that send \(u\otimes x\) to the \(k\)-linear map
\(f\mapsto f(x)\cdot u\). Passing to the projective limit, one
therefore obtains maps
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_k\bigl(X,\mathbf W(M)\bigr)
  \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }
  M\widehat\otimes_kA
  \xrightarrow{\ \psi_A\ }
  \operatorname{Hom}_k(A^\dagger,M),
\]
where \(M\widehat\otimes_kA\) denotes the projective limit of the
\(k\)-modules \(M\otimes_k(A/\mathfrak l)\). The map \(\psi_A\) plainly
involves only the structure of pseudocompact \(k\)-module of \(A\);
moreover, it is bijective when \(A\) is equal to \(k\), hence, more
generally, when \(A\) is a projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module
\textup{(the functor \(N\mapsto M\widehat\otimes_kN\) commutes with
infinite products)}.

Suppose now \(X\) equal to \(G\) and \(M\) to \(U\). For every
\(k\)-algebra \(C\) of finite length, multiplication then makes
\(U\otimes_kC\) a monoid with unit element; moreover, it is clear that
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{k\text{-}\operatorname{Gr.}}(G,U_m)\) is the part
of \(\operatorname{Hom}_k\bigl(G,\mathbf W(U)\bigr)\) formed by the
homomorphisms of \(k\)-functors in monoids with unit element. When
\(A\) is a projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module, it follows from the
functorial character of \(\psi_A\) that these homomorphisms correspond
to the \(k\)-linear maps from \(\mathbf H(G)=A^\dagger\) to \(U\) that
are compatible with the multiplications and with the unit elements of
\(\mathbf H(G)\) and \(U\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}, carries out this step. In outline: let
\(\theta:G\to\mathbf W(U)\) correspond to \(\gamma\in U\widehat\otimes_kA\) and
to \(\phi=\psi_A(\gamma):\mathbf H(G)\to U\). The value of \(\theta\) at the
unit point of \(G\) is \(\phi(\varepsilon_A)\), and \(\varepsilon_A\) is the
unit element of \(\mathbf H(G)\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.3}{2.3})}. The morphisms
\((g,g')\mapsto\theta(gg')\) and \((g,g')\mapsto\theta(g)\theta(g')\) from
\(G\times G\) to \(\mathbf W(U)\) correspond to elements of
\(U\widehat\otimes_kA\widehat\otimes_kA\) whose images under
\(\psi_{A\widehat\otimes A}\), composed with
\(\phi_G:\mathbf H(G)\otimes_k\mathbf H(G)\to\mathbf H(G\times G)\), are
\(h\otimes h'\mapsto\phi(hh')\) and \(h\otimes h'\mapsto\phi(h)\phi(h')\). As
\(\psi_{A\widehat\otimes A}\) and \(\phi_G\) are bijective, \(A\) being
projective, \(\theta\) is a homomorphism of monoids with unit element if and
only if \(\phi\) is a homomorphism of unital algebras.}
