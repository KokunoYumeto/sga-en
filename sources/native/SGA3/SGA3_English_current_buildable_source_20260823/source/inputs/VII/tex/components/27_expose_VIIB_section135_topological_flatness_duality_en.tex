
\subsubsection*{1.3.5}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.3.5}

A formal variety \(X\) over \(k\) is said to be \emph{topologically
flat} if its affine algebra \(A\) is a projective pseudocompact
\(k\)-module, that is, if the structure morphism
\(X\to\operatorname{Spf}(k)\) is topologically flat. Such a formal
variety can be described by means of coalgebras: let us denote by
\(\mathbf H(X)\) the \(\mathbf O_k\)-module \(\mathbf V_k^f(A)\) dual to
\(A\) (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}{1.2.3}). Since \(\mathbf V_k^f(A\widehat{\otimes}A)\)
identifies with \(\mathbf V_k^f(A)\otimes_k\mathbf V_k^f(A)\)
(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.2}{1.2.2}) by \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.3.6}{0.3.6}, the multiplication of the algebra
\(A\) induces by transposition a diagonal morphism, which makes
\(\mathbf H(X)\) an \(\mathbf O_k\)-coalgebra (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.2}{1.2.2}). We shall say
that \(\mathbf H(X)\) is the \emph{coalgebra of \(X\) over
\(\mathbf O_k\)}.

\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.3.5.C}%
Conversely, every \(\mathbf O_k\)-coalgebra \(\mathbf H\) defines a
\(k\)-functor (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.1}{1.2.1}) \(\operatorname{Spf}^*\mathbf H\): for every
object \(C\) of \(\operatorname{Alf}/k\),
\((\operatorname{Spf}^*\mathbf H)(C)\) is the set of elements \(x\) of
\(\mathbf H(C)\) such that \(\varepsilon_{\mathbf H(C)}(x)=1\) and
\(\Delta_{\mathbf H(C)}(x)=x\otimes x\) (notation of
VII\textsubscript{A}, \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:3.1}{3.1}). When the \(\mathbf O_k\)-module
underlying \(\mathbf H\) is flat, \(\operatorname{Spf}^*\mathbf H\)
identifies with the formal spectrum of an algebra
\(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\in\operatorname{Alp}/k\), whose underlying
topological \(k\)-module is the dual of \(\mathbf H\) (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}{1.2.3}), the
multiplication of \(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\) being induced by the diagonal
morphisms of the coalgebras \(\mathbf H(C)\) (cf.\ VII\textsubscript{A},
3.1.2).
\phantomsection\label{sga3:VIIB:1.3.5.D}%
One thus sees that the functors \(X\mapsto\mathbf H(X)\) and
\(\mathbf H\mapsto\operatorname{Spf}^*\mathbf H\) define an equivalence
between the category of topologically flat formal \(k\)-varieties and
the category of flat \(\mathbf O_k\)-coalgebras.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}; our formulation).
(a) When \(k\) is Artinian, \(A\mapsto A^\dagger\) is an anti-equivalence from the category of
topologically flat profinite \(k\)-algebras to the category of flat \(k\)-coalgebras, with quasi-inverse
\(H\mapsto H^*\) (0.2.2): by 0.3.6, \(A^\dagger\otimes_kA^\dagger\simeq(A\widehat\otimes_kA)^\dagger\) when
\(A\) is projective, so the multiplication and the unit of \(A\) transpose to a diagonal morphism and a
counit of \(A^\dagger\), and conversely. This is used implicitly above. (b) For a flat
\(\mathbf O_k\)-coalgebra \(\mathbf H\) and \(C\) in \(\operatorname{Alf}/k\), the identification of
\((\operatorname{Spf}^*\mathbf H)(C)\) with
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Alp}/k}(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H),C)
=\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Alp}/C}(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\widehat\otimes_kC,C)\) comes from
\(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\widehat\otimes_kC\simeq\mathbf H(C)^*\) (Addition in 1.2.3, (b)) and from (a) over the
Artinian ring \(C\), which identifies the algebra homomorphisms \(\mathbf H(C)^*\to C\) with the elements
\(x\) of \(\mathbf H(C)\) such that \(\varepsilon(x)=1\) and \(\Delta(x)=x\otimes x\). (c) By (c) of the
Addition in 1.2.3, the equivalence commutes with base change: if \(k\to K\) is a morphism of pseudocompact
rings, \(X\widehat\otimes_kK\) corresponds to \(\mathbf H(X)\otimes_kK\).
