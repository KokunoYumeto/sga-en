
\subsubsection*{4.1.1}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:4.1.1}

Consider now a scheme \(S\) over the prime field \(\mathbb F_p\), an
\(S\)-scheme \(X\), and an \(S\)-scheme \(q:T\to S\) of finite length
\textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.6}{1.2.6})}. Let \(\operatorname{fr}(S)\) be the Frobenius
morphism of \(S\) relative to \(\mathbb F_p\) \textup{(this morphism
induces the identity on the underlying topological space and sends a
section \(s\) of the structure sheaf to \(s^p\);
VII\textsubscript{A}, \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:4.1}{4.1})}. If we put
\[
  X^{(p)}=X\times_{\operatorname{fr}(S)}S
\]
\textup{(VII\textsubscript{A}, loc.\ cit.)}, the morphisms of formal
varieties over \(\widehat S\)
\[
  f:T\longrightarrow\widehat{X^{(p)}}/\widehat S
\]
correspond bijectively to the \(S\)-morphisms of \(T\) into
\(X^{(p)}\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.6}{1.2.6})}, that is, to the commutative squares
\[
  \begin{tikzcd}[row sep=2.4em,column sep=3.2em]
    T \arrow[r,"f'"] \arrow[d,"q"'] & X \arrow[d] \\
    S \arrow[r,"\operatorname{fr}(S)"] & S\rlap{\,.}
  \end{tikzcd}
\]
To such a square there corresponds an \(S\)-morphism of \(T\) into
\((\widehat X/\widehat S)^{(p)}\) if and only if \(f'\) factors
through a subscheme \(X_0\) of \(X\) which is of finite length over
\(S\). One sees in this way that
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vf}/\widehat S}
    \bigl(T,(\widehat X/\widehat S)^{(p)}\bigr)
\]
can be identified with a subset of
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vf}/\widehat S}
    \bigl(T,\widehat{X^{(p)}}/\widehat S\bigr),
\]
which defines \((\widehat X/\widehat S)^{(p)}\) as \emph{a formal
subvariety of} \(\widehat{X^{(p)}}/\widehat S\). One has the equality
\[
  (\widehat X/\widehat S)^{(p)}=\widehat{X^{(p)}}/\widehat S
\]
if \(\operatorname{fr}(S)\circ q\) is an \(S\)-scheme of finite
length whenever \(q\) is one. This is the case, for example, when \(S\)
is locally of finite type over a field \(\kappa\) such that
\([\kappa:\kappa^p]<+\infty\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, rewrites this
paragraph, takes \(\operatorname{fr}(S)\circ\eta\) as the structure morphism of \(X^{(p)}\) (\(\eta\) being that
of \(X\)), states the reverse inclusion \(\widehat{X^{(p)}}/\widehat S\subset(\widehat X/\widehat S)^{(p)}\)
as a correction, and notes that the paragraph is not used below. With
\(X^{(p)}=X\times_{\operatorname{fr}(S)}S\), whose structure morphism is the second projection, the inclusion of
the text is the right one, and it can be strict: if \(S=\operatorname{Spec}\kappa\) with
\([\kappa:\kappa^p]=\infty\) and \(X=\operatorname{Spec}\kappa^{1/p}\), then \(\widehat X/\widehat S\) is empty
since \([\kappa^{1/p}:\kappa]=\infty\), whereas the homomorphism
\(\kappa^{1/p}\otimes_{\kappa,\operatorname{fr}}\kappa\to\kappa\), \(a\otimes b\mapsto a^pb\), defines an
\(S\)-morphism \(S\to X^{(p)}\), that is, a point of \(\widehat{X^{(p)}}/\widehat S\) with values in
\(T=S\).}
