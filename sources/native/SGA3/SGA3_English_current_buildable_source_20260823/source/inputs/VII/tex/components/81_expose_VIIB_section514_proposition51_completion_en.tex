
Now denote by \(\operatorname{Gr}(B)\) and \(\operatorname{Gr}'(B)\) the
graded algebras associated with \(B\) for the filtration induced by
that of \(A\) and for the filtration defined by the closures
\(\overline{I_B^n}\) of the powers of \(I_B\). Since the diagram
\[
  \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=3.4em]
    B \arrow[r,"\mathrm{incl.}"]
    & A
      \arrow[r,shift left=0.7ex,"\mathrm{in}_2"]
      \arrow[r,shift right=0.7ex,"(q\widehat\otimes_kA)\Delta_A"']
    & (A/\overline{AI_B})\widehat\otimes_kA
  \end{tikzcd}
\]
is commutative by condition \textup{(i)}, one sees that
\(\operatorname{Gr}(B)_n\subset A'_n\) for every \(n\). Now
\[
  I/\overline{I^2}\simeq(A/I)\widehat\otimes_kA'_1
\]
is generated, as a pseudocompact \((A/I)\)-module, by the image of
\(\operatorname{Gr}'(B)_1\), so that
\(\operatorname{Gr}(B)_1\simeq A'_1\), whence
\(\operatorname{Gr}(B)=(A'_n)\); moreover, the canonical map of
\(\operatorname{Gr}'(B)\) into \(\operatorname{Gr}(B)\) is surjective,
since the graded algebra \(\operatorname{Gr}(B)=(A'_n)\) is generated
by its terms of degree \(1\). Lemma \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:5.1.5}{5.1.5} therefore shows that
\(\operatorname{Gr}'(B)=\operatorname{Gr}(B)\).

Consider finally the kernel \(N\) of the pair
\((\mathrm{in}_2,(q\widehat\otimes A)\Delta_A)\) above, and endow
\(N\) with the filtration induced by that of \(A\). One then has
\[
  (A'_n)
  \subset\operatorname{Gr}(B)
  \subset\operatorname{Gr}(N)
  \subset(A'_n).
\]
Since all the filtrations considered are separated, one deduces that
\(N=B\) \textup{([CA], V, \S\,7, Lemma~1)}.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, details two steps; in our words: (a) as
\(A_0=k\cdot1\oplus I_0\), \(I_0\) being the augmentation ideal of \(A_0\), one has
\(A_1\simeq A'_1\oplus(I_0\widehat\otimes_kA'_1)\), and the \(A_0\)-submodule generated by a closed subspace
\(S\) of \(A'_1\) is \(S\oplus(I_0\widehat\otimes_kS)\); so it is \(A_1\) only if \(S=A'_1\), which gives
\(\operatorname{Gr}(B)_1=A'_1\); (b) the lemma of [CA] also requires \(B\) to be complete for the induced
filtration: it is, being closed in \(A\), which is complete for the filtration by the \(\overline{I^n}\),
these ideals tending to \(0\) since \(G\) is infinitesimal (0.1.2). The re-edition also obtains
\(\overline{I_B^n}=B\cap\overline{I^n}\) from the topological flatness of \(A\) over \(B\) (2.4): both ideals
generate \(\overline{I^n}\) in \(A\), and \(A\) is faithfully flat over \(B\).}
