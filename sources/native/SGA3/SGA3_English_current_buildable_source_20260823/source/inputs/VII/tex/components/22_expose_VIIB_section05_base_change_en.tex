
\section*{0.5. Base Change}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:0.5}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.5}{0.5}. Base Change}

Let \(k\) and \(\ell\) be pseudocompact rings and
\[
  \phi:k\longrightarrow\ell
\]
a homomorphism (\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.1.3}{0.1.3}). For every pseudocompact \(k\)-module
\(M\), we denote by \(M\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\) the separated completion
of \(M\otimes_k\ell\) for the linear topology defined by the sums of the
images of \(M'\otimes_k\ell\) and \(M\otimes_k\ell'\) in
\(M\otimes_k\ell\), where \((M',\ell')\) ranges over the pairs formed by
an open submodule of \(M\) and an open ideal of \(\ell\); likewise, if
\(m\in M\) and \(x\in\ell\), we denote by \(m\widehat{\otimes}_kx\) the
canonical image of \(m\otimes_kx\) in \(M\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\). It is
clear that \(M\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\) is a pseudocompact
\(\ell\)-module.

To every pseudocompact \(\ell\)-module \(N\) and every morphism
\(f:M\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\to N\), associate the map
\[
  f':M\longrightarrow N,
  \qquad
  m\longmapsto f(m\widehat{\otimes}_k1).
\]
It is clear that one obtains in this way a bijection from
\(\operatorname{Hom}_c(M\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell,N)\) onto the set of
continuous \(k\)-linear maps from \(M\) to the \(k\)-module deduced from
\(N\) by restriction of scalars. One deduces from this, as in
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.3.2}{0.3.2} and \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.3.5}{0.3.5},\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text has ``comme en 0.3.2
et 0.3.4''; the commutation with products comes from 0.3.5 and 0.3.6. The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, corrects the reference.} that the functor
\(M\mapsto M\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\) is right exact and that it commutes
with infinite products and with the completed tensor product.

\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}; our formulation).
(a) Here \(\ell\) need not be a profinite \(k\)-algebra; for instance, \(k\) may be a field and \(\ell\)
an arbitrary extension field of \(k\). When the \(k\)-module underlying \(\ell\) is pseudocompact, an
open \(k\)-submodule of a pseudocompact \(\ell\)-module contains an open \(\ell\)-submodule (0.4), and
\(M\widehat\otimes_k\ell\) is the completed tensor product (0.3) of the pseudocompact \(k\)-modules \(M\)
and \(\ell\). (b) The bijection above rests on the following fact: if \(u\colon M\to N\) is continuous and
\(k\)-linear and \(N'\) is an open \(\ell\)-submodule of \(N\), there are an open submodule \(M'\) of \(M\)
with \(u(M')\subset N'\), and an open ideal \(\ell'\) of \(\ell\) with \(\ell'u(m_j)\subset N'\) for
finitely many \(m_j\) generating \(M\) modulo \(M'\); then \((m,\lambda)\mapsto\lambda u(m)\) maps
\(M'\times\ell\) and \(M\times\ell'\) into \(N'\), so that \(m\otimes\lambda\mapsto\lambda u(m)\) extends
continuously to \(M\widehat\otimes_k\ell\). As in 0.3.5, it follows that \(M\mapsto M\widehat\otimes_k\ell\)
commutes with filtered projective limits; and the commutation with the completed tensor product is a
canonical isomorphism \((M\widehat\otimes_kN)\widehat\otimes_k\ell\simeq(M\widehat\otimes_k\ell)
\widehat\otimes_\ell(N\widehat\otimes_k\ell)\), both sides representing, on pseudocompact
\(\ell\)-modules \(P\) of finite length, the continuous \(k\)-bilinear maps from \(M\times N\) to \(P\).
(c) When \(\ell\) is a profinite \(k\)-algebra, every pseudocompact \(\ell\)-module is pseudocompact over
\(k\) (note to 0.4), and the bijection above says that \(M\mapsto M\widehat\otimes_k\ell\) is left adjoint
to the restriction of scalars from pseudocompact \(\ell\)-modules to pseudocompact \(k\)-modules.

Finally, if \(A\) is a profinite \(k\)-algebra, there is a unique
profinite \(\ell\)-algebra structure on \(A\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\) such
that
\[
  (m\widehat{\otimes}_kx)(n\widehat{\otimes}_ky)
  =
  (mn)\widehat{\otimes}_k(xy)
\]
for \(m,n\in A\) and \(x,y\in\ell\). One says that
\(A\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\) is the algebra deduced from \(A\) by the
extension of scalars \(\phi\). The functor
\(A\mapsto A\widehat{\otimes}_k\ell\) is right exact.
