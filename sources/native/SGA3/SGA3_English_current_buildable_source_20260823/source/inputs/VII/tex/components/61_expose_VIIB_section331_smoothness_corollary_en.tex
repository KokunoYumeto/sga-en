
\subsubsection*{3.3.1. Corollary}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:3.3.1}

{\itshape Let \(S\) be a locally Noetherian scheme over \(\mathbb Q\),
and \(G\) an \(S\)-group scheme locally of finite type. If
\(\omega_{G/S}\) is a locally free \(\mathcal O_S\)-module, \(G\) is smooth
over \(S\) along the unit section.}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, adds the hypothesis that \(G\) be flat over \(S\), and in the proof applies
(iv)~\(\Rightarrow\)~(ii) of 3.3, which needs \(H\) topologically flat over \(k\). The hypothesis is not
needed: the proof below uses condition (iii) of 3.3 (\(H\) infinitesimal, \(\omega_{H/k}\) projective), and
the proof of (iii)~\(\Rightarrow\)~(i) does not use flatness. It gives
\(\widehat{\mathcal O}_x\simeq k[[t_1,\ldots,t_n]]\), which is flat over \(k=\widehat{\mathcal O}_s\), so that
\(G\) is in fact flat over \(S\) at \(x\). Compare Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B}, \SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-1.6}{1.6},
also stated without flatness.}

Indeed, let \(x\) be a point of the unit section, \(s\) its image in
\(S\), and \(\mathcal O_x\) and \(\mathcal O_s\) the local algebras of \(x\)
and \(s\). It suffices to show that \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_x\) is formally
smooth over \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_s\) \textup{(EGA~IV, 6.8.6, and
EGA~0\textsubscript{IV}, 19.3.6)}.\footnote{Translator's note: \(\mathcal O_x\) and \(\mathcal O_s\) being
Noetherian local rings, their ideals of finite colength are those that contain a power of the maximal
ideal; so \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_x\) and \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_s\) are the completions for the adic topologies, as
in EGA. By EGA~IV, 6.8.6, \(G\) is smooth at \(x\) if \(\mathcal O_x\) is formally smooth over
\(\mathcal O_s\), and by EGA~0\textsubscript{IV}, 19.3.6, this holds if \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_x\) is formally smooth
over \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_s\). Below, \(H\) is a formal group because the formation of
\(\widehat G/\widehat S\) commutes with products, by its definition in \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.6}{1.2.6}. The Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, writes these points out and cites EGA~IV\textsubscript{4},
17.5.3, which gives the smoothness criterion directly.} Now \(\widehat{\mathcal O}_x\) and
\(\widehat{\mathcal O}_s\) are the local rings of \(x\) and \(s\) in
\(\widehat G/\widehat S\) and \(\widehat S\). Putting
\(k=\widehat{\mathcal O}_s\) and \(H=\operatorname{Spf}(\widehat{\mathcal O}_x)\), it
is clear that \(H\) is an infinitesimal formal group over \(k\) and that
\[
  \omega_{H/k}
  =
  (\omega_{G/S})_s\otimes_{\mathcal O_s}\widehat{\mathcal O}_s.
\]
Hence \(\omega_{H/k}\) is the product of a finite number of copies of
\(k\). The corollary therefore follows from \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:3.3:cartier-theorem}{3.3} and from
EGA~0\textsubscript{IV}, 19.3.4.

We thus recover a result obtained elsewhere for group schemes locally
of finite presentation over a scheme \(S\).\footnote{Translator's note: Expos\'e~VI\textsubscript{B},
\SGARefLink{prop:sga3-VIB-1.6}{1.6}; the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, gives this reference.}
