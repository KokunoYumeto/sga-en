
\subsubsection*{2.2.1}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.2.1}

A bialgebra \(\mathbf H\) over \(\mathbf O_k\) is said to be
\emph{flat} if its underlying module is flat
\textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.1}{1.2.1})}. If \(\mathbf H\) is flat, it is clear that the
\(k\)-functor \(\operatorname{Spf}^*(\mathbf H)\) is left exact. It is
therefore isomorphic to
\[
  C\longmapsto
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vaf}/k}
    \bigl(\operatorname{Spf}(C),\mathscr G(\mathbf H)\bigr)
\]
for a certain formal group \(\mathscr G(\mathbf H)\); it follows from
VII\textsubscript{A} \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:3.2.2}{3.2.2} and \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.5}{1.3.5} that
\(\mathscr G(\mathbf H)\) can be described as follows. Let
\(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\) be the pseudocompact \(k\)-module dual to
\(\mathbf H\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}{1.2.3})}. The diagonal morphisms of the
hyperalgebras \(\mathbf H(C)\) induce on \(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\) a
multiplication that makes it a profinite \(k\)-algebra. The
multiplication in the hyperalgebras \(\mathbf H(C)\) induces on
\(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)\) a diagonal morphism satisfying conditions
\textup{\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.1:item:i}{(i)}}, \textup{(ii)} and \textup{\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.1:item:iii}{(iii)}} of \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.1}{2.1}.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}, details this step. For \(C\) of finite length,
\(\mathbf H(C)\) is a projective \(C\)-module, so the canonical map
\(\mathbf H(C)^*\widehat\otimes_C\mathbf H(C)^*\to
\bigl(\mathbf H(C)\otimes_C\mathbf H(C)\bigr)^*\) is bijective (this is clear
for free modules and passes to direct factors). Hence the transposes of the
diagonal morphism, the multiplication, the unit and the inversion of
\(\mathbf H(C)\) give \(\mathbf H(C)^*\) a multiplication, a diagonal
morphism, an augmentation and an antipode satisfying \textup{(i)--(iii)} of
2.1, compatibly with \(C\); in the limit over the \(C=k/\mathfrak l\), they
make \(\Gamma^*(\mathbf H)=\varprojlim_{\mathfrak l}\mathbf H(k/\mathfrak l)^*\)
a cogroup in the category of profinite \(k\)-algebras.} It
remains to put
\[
  \mathscr G(\mathbf H)=\operatorname{Spf}\Gamma^*(\mathbf H).
\]

Conversely, let \(G\) be a topologically flat formal \(k\)-group with
affine algebra \(A\), and write
\[
  \mathbf H(G)=\mathbf V_k^f(A)
\]
for its \(\mathbf O_k\)-coalgebra \textup{(cf.\ \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2.3}{1.2.3})}. The
diagonal morphism
\[
  \Delta_A:A\longrightarrow A\widehat\otimes_kA
\]
then induces, for every finite-length \(k\)-algebra \(C\), a
\(C\)-linear map
\[
  \mathbf V_k^f(A)(C)
  \otimes_C
  \mathbf V_k^f(A)(C)
  \longrightarrow
  \mathbf V_k^f(A)(C),
\]
which makes the coalgebra \(\mathbf V_k^f(A)(C)\) into a
\(C\)-bialgebra. We say that \(\mathbf H(G)\) is the \emph{bialgebra of the formal group}
\(G\). By \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.5}{1.3.5}, the functor \(\mathbf H:G\mapsto\mathbf H(G)\) is an
equivalence of the category of topologically flat formal \(k\)-groups
with that of flat bialgebras over \(\mathbf O_k\). The functor
\(\mathscr G\) is a quasi-inverse (``an inverse up to functorial
isomorphism'') of \(\mathbf H\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}, adds that this equivalence, and its analogue
below for an Artinian ring, commutes with base change along a morphism of
pseudocompact rings \(k\to K\). For Artinian rings this is the equality
\(\mathbf H(G\widehat\otimes_kK)=\mathbf H(G)\otimes_kK\), which follows from
\(\operatorname{Hom}_c(A,K)=\operatorname{Hom}_c(A,k)\otimes_kK\), valid when
\(A\) is a projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module. For \(k\) Artinian, the flat
bialgebras over \(k\) are the Hopf \(k\)-algebras that are flat over \(k\) and
cocommutative.}

When \(k\) is an Artinian ring and \(G\) is a topologically flat
formal \(k\)-group, the functor \(\mathbf H(G)\) is plainly
determined by its value
\[
  \mathbf H(G)=\mathbf H(G)(k)
\]
at \(k\). We also say that \(\mathbf H(G)\) is the \emph{bialgebra} of \(G\). The
functor \(\mathbf H:G\mapsto\mathbf H(G)\) is then an equivalence of the
category of topologically flat formal \(k\)-groups with the category of
flat bialgebras over \(k\).

\subsubsection*{2.2.2}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.2.2}

Suppose, for simplicity, that \(k\) is Artinian, and let \(G\) be a
commutative, topologically flat formal \(k\)-group. In this case, the
bialgebra \(\mathbf H(G)\) has a commutative multiplication, so that
\(\mathbf H(G)\) is a cogroup in the category of commutative
\(k\)-algebras \textup{(VII\textsubscript{A} \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:3.3}{3.3})}.

If \(k\) is Artinian, one therefore sees that the functor
\(G\mapsto\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(G)\) is an antiequivalence of the
category of commutative topologically flat formal \(k\)-groups with that
of commutative group schemes that are affine and flat over \(k\).\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}, makes the quasi-inverse explicit, after
P.~Cartier, \emph{Groupes algébriques et groupes formels}, in
\emph{Colloque sur la théorie des groupes algébriques} (Bruxelles, 1962),
Gauthier-Villars, 1962, pp.~87--111, \S\,14. For a commutative affine flat
\(k\)-group scheme \(T\), the bialgebra \(\mathcal O(T)\), commutative and
cocommutative, is \(\mathbf H(D(T))\) for the formal group
\(D(T)=\operatorname{Spf}^*\mathcal O(T)\) of \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.1}{2.2.1}; then
\(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(D(T))=T\),
\(D(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(G))=G\), and the morphisms \(G\to D(T)\)
correspond to the Hopf algebra homomorphisms \(\mathbf H(G)\to\mathcal O(T)\),
that is, to the morphisms \(T\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(G)\). The
construction commutes with extension of the Artinian base. When
\(\mathbf H(G)\) is finite over \(k\), one recovers the Cartier duality of
finite commutative group schemes \textup{(VII\textsubscript{A}, \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:3.3.1}{3.3.1})}.}

When \(k\) is a field, one thus obtains an antiequivalence of the
category of commutative formal \(k\)-groups with that of affine
commutative \(k\)-group schemes.
