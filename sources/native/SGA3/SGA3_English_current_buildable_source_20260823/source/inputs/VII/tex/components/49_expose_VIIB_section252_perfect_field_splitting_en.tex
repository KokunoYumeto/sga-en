
\subsubsection*{2.5.2}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.5.2}

When \(k\) is a perfect field, the functor \(X\mapsto X_e\) of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.6}{1.6} is also right adjoint to the inclusion of
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\) in \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\):
more precisely, if \(k\) is a perfect field, \(X_e\) has the same points
as \(X\) and has for local algebra at a point \(x\) the residue field of
\(\mathcal O_{X,x}\). The canonical projections of the algebras
\(\mathcal O_{X,x}\) onto their residue fields therefore define a section
\[
  s:X_e\longrightarrow X
\]
of \(p\). This section depends functorially on \(X\); it is therefore a
homomorphism of formal groups when \(X\) is a formal group.

\medskip
\noindent
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:2.5.2:proposition}
{\itshape When \(k\) is a perfect field, we therefore see that a formal
group \(G\) over \(k\) is canonically isomorphic to the semidirect product
of an infinitesimal group \(G_0\) and an étale group \(G_e\) acting on
\(G_0\).}\footnote{Translator's note: the same argument works over any field \(k\) for a formal group
\(G\) all of whose residue fields are equal to \(k\): then \(G_e\) is the constant group defined by
\(M=G(k)\), the residue projections again give a section \(G_e\to G\) which is a homomorphism, and
\(\mathbf H(G)\) is the semidirect product of \(\mathbf H(G_0)\) by the group algebra \(kM\). The
Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, notes this here and develops it at the end of
\S~2; see the Addition there.}

If, moreover, we suppose \(G\) commutative, \(G\) is the product of
\(G_0\) and \(G_e\). This decomposition of the commutative formal groups
over \(k\) corresponds to an analogous decomposition of the affine
commutative \(k\)-group schemes \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.2}{2.2.2})}: let us say that a commutative
group scheme over a field \(k\) is \emph{of multiplicative type}
\textup{(}resp.\ \emph{unipotent}\textup{)} if it is isomorphic to
\(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(G)\), where \(G\) is an étale \textup{(}resp.\
infinitesimal\textup{)} commutative formal \(k\)-group.\footnote{Translator's note: for affine
commutative \(k\)-groups these definitions agree with the usual ones: of multiplicative type in the sense
of Expos\'e~IX, \SGARefLink{sga3:ix:definition:1:1}{1.1}, and unipotent in the sense that every simple comodule is
trivial (for groups of finite type, that of Expos\'e~XVII). See the Additions after 2.5.2 and at the end
of \S~2. The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}, takes the definition of IX, 1.1
here and proves the equivalence.} By
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.1}{2.5.1}, every affine commutative group scheme \(G\) over a field \(k\)
contains a subgroup \(G_m\) of multiplicative type such that \(G/G_m\)
is unipotent. When \(k\) is perfect, \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.2}{2.5.2} moreover implies the
existence of a canonical retraction of \(G\) onto \(G_m\),\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text
has ``2.5.1'' here; the retraction comes from the decomposition \(G'=G'_0\times G'_e\) of 2.5.2, applied to
the commutative formal group \(G'\) such that \(G\simeq\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(G')\).} so that \(G\)
is the product of a unipotent group and a group of multiplicative
type.

\par\medskip\noindent
\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}; our formulation). When \(k\) is a field that is
not perfect, the exact sequence \(1\to G_0\to G\to G_e\to1\) of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.1}{2.5.1} need not split. The re-edition gives the following example,
after M.~Demazure and P.~Gabriel, \emph{Groupes alg\'ebriques}, I, Masson and
North-Holland, 1970, III, \S~6, 8.6. Let \(p>0\) be the characteristic of
\(k\) and \(\lambda\in k\) an element that is not a \(p\)-th power. The
algebra
\[
  A_\lambda=k[x,y]/(x^p-x,\ y^p-\lambda x),
\]
on which \(x\) and \(y\) are primitive, is a commutative and
cocommutative Hopf algebra of dimension \(p^2\): it is the restricted
enveloping algebra of the abelian \(p\)-Lie algebra with basis \(x,y\)
and \(p\)-th power map \(x\mapsto x\), \(y\mapsto\lambda x\)
\textup{(VII\textsubscript{A}, \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIA:5.3}{5.3})}. Let \(G_\lambda\) be the commutative formal
\(k\)-group with affine algebra \(A_\lambda\). The Hopf subalgebra
\(k[x]/(x^p-x)\) is the affine algebra of the constant group
\((\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)_k\), and it is the largest étale subalgebra of
\(A_\lambda\), the other factors \(k[y]/(y^p-\lambda a)\),
\(a\in\mathbb F_p^\times\), of \(A_\lambda\) being purely inseparable field
extensions of \(k\). So \(G_\lambda\to(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)_k\) is the
morphism \(p\) of 2.5.1, and its kernel, defined by \(x=0\), has affine
algebra \(k[y]/(y^p)\): it is \(\alpha_{p,k}\). A section would be a
morphism of Hopf algebras \(A_\lambda\to k[x]/(x^p-x)\) that is the
identity on \(x\); it would send the primitive element \(y\) to a
primitive element \(cx\), \(c\in k\), with \(c^px=\lambda x\), which is
impossible. Thus \(0\to\alpha_{p,k}\to G_\lambda\to(\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z)_k\to0\)
does not split, and by duality \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.2}{2.2.2})} neither does the exact
sequence of commutative affine \(k\)-groups
\(0\to\mu_{p,k}\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf H(G_\lambda)\to\alpha_{p,k}\to0\),
where \(\mu_{p,k}=\operatorname{Spec}k[t]/(t^p-1)\). The re-edition adds,
after Demazure and Gabriel, that all extensions of \(\alpha_{p,k}\) by
\(\mu_{p,k}\) are obtained in this way.
