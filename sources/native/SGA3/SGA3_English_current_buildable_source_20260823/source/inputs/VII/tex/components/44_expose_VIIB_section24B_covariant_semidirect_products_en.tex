\label{sga3:VIIB:2.4.B}%
\textup{(e)} Suppose \(k\) Artinian. Through the isomorphism
\(N\times Q\simeq G\) above, the bialgebras \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.1}{2.2.1})} satisfy
\(\mathbf H(G)=\mathbf H(N)\otimes_k\mathbf H(Q)\) as coalgebras, an
element \(x\otimes h\) corresponding to the product \(xh\) in
\(\mathbf H(G)\), where \(\mathbf H(N)\) and \(\mathbf H(Q)\) are
identified with subalgebras of \(\mathbf H(G)\) through \(j\) and
\(\sigma\). On points,
\((y\,\sigma(q))(y'\sigma(q'))=y\,\alpha(q,y')\,\sigma(qq')\), with
\(\alpha(q,y')=\sigma(q)\,y'\,\sigma(q)^{-1}\), a morphism
\(\alpha:Q\times N\to N\). Let
\(\alpha_*:\mathbf H(Q)\otimes_k\mathbf H(N)\to\mathbf H(N)\) be the map
induced by \(\alpha\); computing in \(\mathbf H(G)\),
\(\alpha_*(h\otimes x')=\sum_ih_i\,x'\,c(h'_i)\) when
\(\Delta(h)=\sum_ih_i\otimes h'_i\), \(c\) being the antipode. The product
of \(\mathbf H(G)\) is then
\[
  (x\otimes h)(x'\otimes h')
  =
  \sum_ix\,\alpha_*(h_i\otimes x')\otimes h'_ih'.
\]
\textup{(The re-edition writes \(x\,\alpha_*(h\otimes x')\otimes hh'\), which
is correct when \(h\) is group-like, as in what follows, but not for a
primitive \(h\).)} In particular, if \(M\) is an abstract group and \(Q\) is
the constant formal group \(M_k\), so that \(\mathbf H(Q)\) is the group
algebra \(kM\), then \(\alpha_*(\gamma\otimes x')=\gamma x'\gamma^{-1}\) for
\(\gamma\in M\): the group \(M\) acts on \(\mathbf H(N)\) by automorphisms of
Hopf algebras, and \(\mathbf H(G)\) is the semidirect product of
\(\mathbf H(N)\) by \(kM\).
