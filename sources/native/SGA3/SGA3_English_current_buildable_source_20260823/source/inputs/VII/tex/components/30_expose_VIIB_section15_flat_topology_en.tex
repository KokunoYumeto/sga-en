
\subsection*{1.5}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.5}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{1.5. The flat topology}

Let \(k\) be a pseudocompact ring and \((f_i)\) a family of morphisms
\[
  f_i:X_i\longrightarrow X
\]
of \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\). We shall say that \((f_i)\) is a
\emph{surjective topologically flat family} if the morphism
\[
  \coprod_i X_i\longrightarrow X
\]
induced by the \(f_i\) is surjective and topologically flat. This
means that every \(f_i\) is topologically flat and that every point of
\(X\) lies in the image of at least one \(X_i\).

It follows from \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.3}{1.3.3} that surjective topologically flat families
define a pretopology on \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\) \textup{(IV,
\SGARefLink{sga3:iv:definition:4:2:5}{4.2.5})}. The corresponding topology will be called the \emph{flat
topology} on \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\).

It is clear that a contravariant functor \(F\) from
\(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\) to \((\mathbf{Sets})\) is a sheaf for the flat
topology if and only if \(F\) carries every
direct sum to a direct product and the sequence

is exact for every surjective topologically flat morphism
\(f:X\to Y\).

\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.3.1}{Proposition~1.3.1} implies that the flat topology is coarser than the
canonical topology. We therefore obtain a functor \(i\) from
\(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\) to the category of sheaves for the flat
topology by associating with every formal variety \(X\) the functor
\[
  iX:T\longmapsto
  \operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vaf}/k}(T,X).
\]
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.4.thm}{Theorem~1.4} implies that, if \(d_0,d_1:X_1\rightrightarrows X\) is an
equivalence relation such that \(d_1\) is topologically flat, formation
of the quotient commutes with \(i\):\footnote{Translator's note: here
\(i(X)/i(X_1)\) is the quotient of the sheaf \(i(X)\) by the equivalence
relation \(i(X_1)\) in the category of sheaves for the flat topology, so the
assertion is that the formal variety \(X/X_1=\operatorname{Coker}(d_0,d_1)\)
of \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:1.2}{1.2} represents this quotient sheaf. The Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Exposé~VII\textsubscript{B}, states it in this form as a theorem, identifies \(X\)
with \(iX\) as in Exposé~I, and refers to Exposé~IV:
\SGARefLink{sga3:iv:corollary:4:3:5}{4.3.5} for the sheaf condition relative to a pretopology,
\SGARefLink{sga3:iv:subsection:4:5}{4.5} for topologies coarser than the canonical one (every
representable functor is then a sheaf), and \SGARefLink{sga3:iv:lemma:4:6:5}{4.6.5}, by which an
equivalence relation that is effective for a suitable class of morphisms has its
quotient sheaf represented by the quotient object.}
\[
  i(X)/i(X_1)\simeq i(X/X_1).
\]
