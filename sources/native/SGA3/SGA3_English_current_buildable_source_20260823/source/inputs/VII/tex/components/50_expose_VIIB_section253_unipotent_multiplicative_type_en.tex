\emph{Addition} (after the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011),
Expos\'e~VII\textsubscript{B}; our formulation). \emph{Comparison with
Expos\'e~IX.} Let \(k\) be a field, \(k_s\) a separable closure of \(k\), and
\(\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(k_s/k)\). In Expos\'e~IX, \SGARefLink{sga3:ix:definition:1:1}{1.1}, a
\(k\)-group scheme \(T\) is of multiplicative type if \(T_S\) is
diagonalizable for some faithfully flat and quasi-compact \(k\)-scheme
\(S\); such a \(T\) is affine and commutative, by descent, and it comes to
the same to ask that \(T_K\simeq D_K(M)\) for a field extension \(K\) of
\(k\) and an abelian group \(M\) (replace \(S\) by one of its points).

(a) \emph{An affine commutative \(k\)-group \(T\) is of multiplicative type
in this sense if and only if its dual \(D(T)\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.2}{2.2.2})} is étale, that
is, if and only if \(T\) is of multiplicative type in the sense of
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.2}{2.5.2}; \(T_{k_s}\) is then diagonalizable.} Let \(\mathcal A\) be the
affine algebra of \(D(T)\), so that \(\mathcal O(T)=\mathcal A^\dagger\), and
let \(\mathcal A_0\) be its local component at the unit point. By
\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.1}{2.5.1}, \(D(T)\) is étale if and only if \(\mathcal A_0=k\). For a field
extension \(K\) of \(k\), \(\mathcal A\widehat\otimes_kK\) is the affine
algebra of \(D(T_K)\), and its local component at the unit point is
\(\mathcal A_0\widehat\otimes_kK\), a projective limit of local Artinian
\(K\)-algebras with residue field \(K\). If \(T_K\simeq D_K(M)\), then
\(\mathcal O(T)\otimes_kK\) is the group algebra \(KM\), so that
\(\mathcal A\widehat\otimes_kK\simeq K^M\) and
\(\mathcal A_0\widehat\otimes_kK=K\), whence \(\mathcal A_0=k\). Conversely,
if \(D(T)\) is étale, so is \(D(T)\widehat\otimes_kk_s=D(T_{k_s})\), which is
therefore the constant group defined by \(M=D(T)(k_s)\), \(k_s\) being
separably closed; by the Addition in \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5.A}{2.5}, \(\mathcal O(T)\otimes_kk_s\) is
the group algebra \(k_sM\), that is, \(T_{k_s}\simeq D_{k_s}(M)\).

(b) Consequently, by \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.2.2}{2.2.2} and \SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:2.5:proposition}{2.5}, \(T\mapsto D(T)(k_s)\) is an
anti-equivalence from the category of the \(k\)-groups of multiplicative
type onto that of the continuous \(\Gamma\)-modules, that is, of the
commutative \(\Gamma\)-groups.

For the comparison of ``unipotent'' with the usual notion, see the
Addition at the end of \S~2.
