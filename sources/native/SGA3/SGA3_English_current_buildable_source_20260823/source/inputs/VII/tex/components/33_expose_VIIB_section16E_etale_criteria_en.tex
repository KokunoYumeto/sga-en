
\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(d)}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.6.C}
\emph{Reduction to the residue fields.} Let \(r(k)\) be the radical of
\(k\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.1.2}{0.1.2})} and put
\(\kappa(k)=k/r(k)=\prod_{s\in\operatorname{Spf}(k)}\kappa(s)\), with
the product topology; let \(S_{\kappa(k)}\) be the scheme that is the
sum of the \(\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\). For a formal
\(k\)-variety \(X\) put \(X_\kappa=X\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(k)\); its
affine algebra is the quotient of \(A\) by the closure of \(r(k)A\), so
\(X_\kappa\) has the same points as \(X\), and
\(\mathcal O_{X_\kappa,x}=\mathcal O_{X,x}\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(s)\).
By \textup{(a)}, \(X_\kappa\) is étale over \(\kappa(k)\) when \(X\) is
étale over \(k\). Every formal \(\kappa(k)\)-variety is topologically
flat, so by \textup{(c)}\,\textup{(iv)} the étale ones are the
\(\operatorname{Spf}\bigl(\prod_{i\in I}L_i\bigr)\), with \(L_i\) a
finite separable extension of some \(\kappa(s_i)\); and a continuous
\(\kappa(k)\)-morphism \(\prod_jL'_j\to L_i\) factors through a single
factor \(L'_j\), since \(L_i\) has no idempotents other than \(0\) and
\(1\). Hence
\(\operatorname{Spf}\bigl(\prod_iL_i\bigr)\mapsto\coprod_i\operatorname{Spec}L_i\)
is an equivalence of \(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/\kappa(k)\)
with the category of étale \(S_{\kappa(k)}\)-schemes.

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(e)}
\phantomsection
\label{sga3:VIIB:1.6.D}
\emph{If \(Y\) is étale over \(k\), the map
\(\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vaf}/k}(X,Y)\to
\operatorname{Hom}_{\operatorname{Vaf}/\kappa(k)}(X_\kappa,Y_\kappa)\)
is bijective.} Let \(B\) be the affine algebra of \(Y\). A morphism
\(X_\kappa\to Y_\kappa\) is a continuous morphism of \(k\)-algebras
\(\phi:B\to A/\overline{r(k)A}\). Every open ideal \(\mathfrak N\) of
\(A\) contains \(\mathfrak lA\) for some open ideal \(\mathfrak l\) of
\(k\), \(A/\mathfrak N\) being of finite length, and
\(r(k)^n\subset\mathfrak l\) for large \(n\) \textup{(\SGARefLink{sga3:VIIB:0.1.2}{0.1.2})}; so the
image of \(r(k)A\) in \(A/\mathfrak N\) is nilpotent, and, \(B\) being
formally étale, the composite of \(\phi\) with the projection onto
\(A/(\mathfrak N+\overline{r(k)A})\) lifts to exactly one continuous
morphism \(\phi_{\mathfrak N}:B\to A/\mathfrak N\). By uniqueness the
\(\phi_{\mathfrak N}\) are compatible, and they define the unique
continuous morphism \(B\to A\) lifting \(\phi\). In particular, the
functor \(X\mapsto X_\kappa\) is fully faithful on
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\).

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(f)}
\emph{The functor \(X\mapsto X_\kappa\) is an equivalence of
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\) with
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/\kappa(k)\), hence, by
\textup{(d)}, with the category of étale \(S_{\kappa(k)}\)-schemes.}
By \textup{(e)} it remains to show that every
\(\operatorname{Spf}\bigl(\prod_iL_i\bigr)\) as in \textup{(d)} is of the
form \(X_\kappa\). Take \(X=\operatorname{Spf}\bigl(\prod_iE_i\bigr)\),
where \(E_i=k_{s_i}[T]/(F_i)\) and \(F_i\) is monic and lifts the
minimal polynomial of a primitive element of \(L_i\) over
\(\kappa(s_i)\); \(X\) is étale by \textup{(c)}\,\textup{(iii)}, and
\(E_i\widehat\otimes_k\kappa(s_i)=L_i\). By \textup{(c)}\,\textup{(iii)},
every profinite \(k\)-algebra formally étale over \(k\) is a product of
free modules of finite rank over local components of \(k\), hence a
projective pseudocompact \(k\)-module. Finally,
\(\operatorname{Vaf}^{\mathrm{\acute{e}t}}/k\) is stable under finite
projective limits in \(\operatorname{Vaf}/k\): \(\operatorname{Spf}(k)\)
is étale, and if \(B\to A\) and \(B\to A'\) are morphisms of profinite
\(k\)-algebras formally étale over \(k\), a continuous morphism
\(A\widehat\otimes_BA'\to C/J\) amounts to two continuous morphisms
\(A\to C/J\) and \(A'\to C/J\) that agree on \(B\); their lifts to
\(C\) agree on \(B\), by uniqueness of the lift of \(B\to C/J\), so
\(A\widehat\otimes_BA'\) is formally étale over \(k\).
