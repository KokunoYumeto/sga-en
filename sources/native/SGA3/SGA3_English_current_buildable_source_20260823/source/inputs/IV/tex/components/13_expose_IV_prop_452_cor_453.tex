\noindent\SGARefTarget{sga3:iv:proposition:4:5:2}\textbf{Proposition 4.5.2.}
\textit{Let \(S\) be a representable sheaf on \(\mathscr C\), and let
\(F\to S\) be a morphism in \(\widehat{\mathscr C}\). The presheaf
\[
  S'\longmapsto \operatorname{Hom}_{S}(S',F)
\]
on \(\mathscr C/S\) is separated (respectively, is a sheaf) if and only if
\(F\) is a separated presheaf (respectively, a sheaf) on \(\mathscr C\).}

For every functor \(P\), one has (I, \SGARefLink{sga3:i:proposition:1:4:1}{1.4.1})
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}(P,F)
  =\coprod_{f\in\operatorname{Hom}(P,S)}
    \operatorname{Hom}_{f}(P,F).
\]
For every \(S'\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathscr C)\) and every covering sieve
\(C'\) of \(S'\), there is an isomorphism
\[
  \operatorname{Hom}(C',S)\xleftarrow{\ \sim\ }\operatorname{Hom}(S',S),
\]
since \(S\) is a sheaf. \SGARefLink{sga3:iv:proposition:4:5:2}{The proposition} follows immediately from these two
formulas.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Expos\'e~IV, spells
this out. In our formulation: by the first formula, for \(P=S'\) and for
\(P=C'\), and since \(f\mapsto f|_{C'}\) is bijective by the second, the
restriction map \(\operatorname{Hom}(S',F)\to\operatorname{Hom}(C',F)\) is the
disjoint union, over \(f\in\operatorname{Hom}(S',S)\), of the maps
\(\operatorname{Hom}_f(S',F)\to\operatorname{Hom}_{f|_{C'}}(C',F)\); it is
injective (bijective) if and only if all of these are, and these are the maps
that the separation (sheaf) condition on \(\mathscr C/S\) involves.}

\medskip
\noindent\SGARefTarget{sga3:iv:corollary:4:5:3}\textbf{Corollary 4.5.3.}
\textit{If a topology on \(\mathscr C\) is coarser than the canonical
topology, then the topology it induces on \(\mathscr C/S\) is coarser than
the canonical topology of \(\mathscr C/S\).}
