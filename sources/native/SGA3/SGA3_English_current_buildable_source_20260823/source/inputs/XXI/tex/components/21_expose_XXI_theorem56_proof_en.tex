\begin{proof}
Put
\[
 M(w)=\operatorname{Card}\bigl(R^+\cap w^{-1}(-R^+)\bigr).
\]
If \(M(w)=0\), then \(w=1\) by Corollary~\ref{sga3:XXI:5.4}, and the
theorem is trivial (\(m=0\), and assertions (iii)--(v) are empty). We
argue by induction on \(M(w)\). If \(M(w)>0\), there exists
\(\beta_0\in\Delta\) with \(w(\beta_0)\in-R^+\). Put
\(\alpha_0=\alpha\), and consider the set
\[
 A=w^{-1}(R^+)\cap R_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}.
\]
It is a system of positive roots of \(R_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\). Hence
there exists \(w_0\in W_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\) with
\[
 w_0^{-1}\bigl(R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\bigr)=A.
\]
Put \(w'=ww_0^{-1}\). By Lemma~\ref{sga3:XXI:3.4.9}, one has at once
\[
 R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}=w_0\bigl(R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\bigr),
\]
whence\footnote{Translator's note: the 1970 text states this as the equality of
\((R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0})\cap w^{-1}(-R^+)\) and
\((R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0})\cap w'^{-1}(-R^+)\), which fails in general; the
Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~XXI, corrects it, but applies \(w_0\) to the second
of these sets instead of the first, which also fails in general (type \(A_3\) gives
examples with \(w_0\) of order \(3\)). The relation above holds because \(w_0\) permutes
\(R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\) and \(w'(w_0(\delta))=w(\delta)\); only the equality of
the cardinals of the two sets is used.}
\[
 \bigl(R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\bigr)\cap w'^{-1}(-R^+)
 =w_0\Bigl(\bigl(R^+-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\bigr)\cap w^{-1}(-R^+)\Bigr).
\]
On the other hand,
\[
 R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\cap w'^{-1}(-R^+)
 =R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\cap w_0(-A)
 =R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\cap\bigl(-R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\bigr)=\varnothing,
\]
whereas
\[
 \beta_0\in R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\cap w^{-1}(-R^+).
\]
We have therefore proved that \(M(w')<M(w)\). Put
\(\alpha_1=w_0(\alpha_0)\); we show that \(\alpha_1\in\Delta\), that is,
\(\alpha_0\in w_0^{-1}(\Delta)\). We know that \(w(\alpha_0)\in\Delta\),
hence that \(\alpha_0\in w^{-1}(\Delta)\), hence also that
\(\alpha_0\in w^{-1}(\Delta)\cap R_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\), hence that
\(\alpha_0\) is a simple root of
\(A=w_0^{-1}(R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0})\), and therefore belongs to
\(w_0^{-1}(\Delta\cap R^+_{\alpha_0,\beta_0})\subset w_0^{-1}(\Delta)\)
(see \ref{sga3:XXI:3.4.8}). Thus \(\alpha_1\) is simple and belongs to
\(R_{\alpha_0,\beta_0}\). If \(\alpha_0\neq\alpha_1\), then
\(\beta_0=\alpha_1\). Finally \(\beta=w'(\alpha_1)\), with
\(M(w')<M(w)\), and one concludes by induction.
\end{proof}
