\begin{corollary}\label{sga3:XXI:4.3}
If \((x,\alpha)\ge0\), \((x,\beta)\ge0\), and one puts
\[
 x-w(x)=a_w\alpha^\ast+b_w\beta^\ast\qquad(w\in W),
\]
then
\[
 a_w+b_w\ge0.
\]
If moreover \((x,\alpha)>0\) (respectively \((x,\beta)>0\)), then
\[
 a_w+b_w>0
\]
for \(w\ne1,s_\beta\) (respectively for \(w\ne1,s_\alpha\)).\footnote{Translator's
note: the 1970 text puts \(w(u)-u=a_w\alpha+b_w\beta\) for \(u\in V^\ast\), with the roots
in place of the coroots and the opposite sign, for which Lemma 4.2 gives
\(a_w+b_w\leq0\), not \(\geq0\). The statement is corrected as above in the Gille--Polo
re-edition (2011), Exposé~XXI, which also writes 4.2 with \(x-w_k(x)\).}
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
This follows at once from Proposition~\ref{sga3:XXI:4.1} and
Lemma~\ref{sga3:XXI:4.2}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{sga3:XXI:4.4}
Let \(\mathcal{R}\) be any root datum, and let \(\Delta\) be a system
of simple roots. Let \(\gamma\) be a positive root, and let \(\alpha,\beta\) be two
simple roots. If
\[
 \operatorname{ord}_\Delta(s_\alpha(\gamma))
 <\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(\gamma),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{ord}_\Delta(s_\beta(\gamma))
 \le\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(\gamma),
\]
then
\(\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(w(\gamma))\le\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(\gamma)\)
for every \(w\in W_{\alpha,\beta}\) (the subgroup of \(W\) generated by
\(s_\alpha\) and \(s_\beta\)), and
\(\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(w(\gamma))<\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(\gamma)\)
for every \(w\in W_{\alpha,\beta}\), \(w\neq1\), \(w\neq s_\beta\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Consider the dual root datum \(\mathcal{R}^\ast\), and then the datum
\[
 (M^\ast,M,R'^\ast,R'),
\]
where \(R'\) is the set of roots that are rational linear
combinations of \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\). Applying
Corollary~\ref{sga3:XXI:4.3} to this datum gives the stated result.\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~XXI,
explains the application. In our formulation: as
\(\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(s_\alpha(\gamma))=\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(\gamma)-(\alpha^\ast,\gamma)\),
the hypotheses say that \((\alpha^\ast,\gamma)>0\) and \((\beta^\ast,\gamma)\geq0\).
Corollary 4.3, applied to the datum \((M^\ast,M,R'^\ast,R')\), in which \(\alpha\) and
\(\beta\) play the part of \(\alpha^\ast\) and \(\beta^\ast\), and to \(x=\gamma\), gives
\(\gamma-w(\gamma)=a_w\alpha+b_w\beta\) with \(a_w+b_w\geq0\), and \(>0\) for
\(w\neq1,s_\beta\); and
\(\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(w(\gamma))=\operatorname{ord}_\Delta(\gamma)-(a_w+b_w)\).}
\end{proof}

\section{The Weyl group: generators and relations}
\label{sga3:XXI:5}
\setcounter{theorem}{0}

Let \(\mathcal{R}\) be a root datum. Since the Weyl group is the same
for this datum and for the corresponding reduced datum, we may assume
it reduced when studying the Weyl group.

Let \(\Delta\) be a system of simple roots, whose elements will be
denoted \(\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\ldots,\alpha_n\), \(n=\rgss(\mathcal{R})\). Let
\(n_{ij}\) be the order of the element \(s_{\alpha_i}s_{\alpha_j}\) of \(W\).
In particular \(n_{ii}=1\), and we have seen in \ref{sga3:XXI:3.4.2} and
\ref{sga3:XXI:3.4.3} that the subgroup \(W_{ij}\) of \(W\)
generated by \(s_{\alpha_i}\) and \(s_{\alpha_j}\) is defined by
\[
 s_{\alpha_i}^{\,2}=s_{\alpha_j}^{\,2}
 =(s_{\alpha_i}s_{\alpha_j})^{n_{ij}}=1.
\]

\begin{theorem}\label{sga3:XXI:5.1}
The group \(W\) is the group generated by the elements
\[
 s_{\alpha_i},\qquad i=1,2,\ldots,n,
\]
subject to the relations
\[
 (s_{\alpha_i}s_{\alpha_j})^{n_{ij}}=1.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
We have already seen that the theorem is true when \(n=2\); we shall
use this fact in the proof. Introduce the group \(\widetilde W\)
generated by elements
\[
 T_i,\qquad i=1,2,\ldots,n,
\]
subject to the relations
\[
 (T_iT_j)^{n_{ij}}=1.
\]
In particular \(n_{ii}=1\), whence \(T_i^2=1\). Let
\[
 p:\widetilde W\longrightarrow W
\]
be the group homomorphism sending \(T_i\) to \(s_{\alpha_i}\). We know
that \(p\) is surjective; we shall prove that it is injective.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}\label{sga3:XXI:5.2}
For every \(\alpha\in\mathscr{P}(\Delta)\), one can define uniquely an
element \(T_\alpha\in\widetilde W\) with the following properties:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \(p(T_\alpha)=s_\alpha\);
\item[(ii)] \(T_{\alpha_i}=T_i\);
\item[(iii)] if \(\beta\) and \(\alpha\) are positive roots such that
\(s_{\alpha_i}(\alpha)=\beta\), then
\[
 T_iT_\alpha T_i=T_\beta.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
