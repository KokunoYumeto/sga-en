affine and \(d_1\) is locally free of constant rank \(n\).}

\]

\hyperref[thm:sga3-V-4.1]{Theorem~4.1} for each \(X_\ast^{(n)}\). We are reduced to the case in
which \(d_1\) is finite locally free of rank \(n\).
