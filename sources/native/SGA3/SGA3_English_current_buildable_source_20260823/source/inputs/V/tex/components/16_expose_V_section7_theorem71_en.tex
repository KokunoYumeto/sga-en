
\section*{7. Quotient by a proper and flat pre-equivalence relation}
\phantomsection\label{sec:sga3-V-7}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{7. Quotient by a proper and flat pre-equivalence relation}

\medskip
\phantomsection\label{thm:sga3-V-7.1}%
\noindent\textbf{Theorem 7.1.}---%
\emph{Let \(S\) be a locally Noetherian scheme and let}
\SGAThreeDiagram[0.56\textwidth]
  {figures/exp5/p274_theorem71_groupoid.png}
  {The groupoid occurring in \hyperref[thm:sga3-V-7.1]{Theorem~7.1}.}
  {fig:sga3-V-7.1-groupoid}
  {Polo--Gille re-edition, Expos\'e V}
\emph{be a \((\mathbf{Sch}/S)\)-groupoid such that \(d_1\) is proper
and flat, \(X_0\) is quasi-projective over \(S\),}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~V, notes
that this hypothesis on \(X_0\) cannot be dropped (see the note on Hironaka's
example in \hyperref[thm:sga3-V-4.1]{4.1}), and mentions the theorem of S.~Keel and S.~Mori
(\emph{Quotients by groupoids}, Ann.\ of Math.\ \textbf{145} (1997), 193--213),
which it states thus: if \(X\) is an algebraic space of finite type over a
locally noetherian base \(S\) and \(j\colon R\to X\times_SX\) is a flat groupoid
whose stabilizer \(j^{-1}(\Delta_X)\) is finite over \(X\), there is an
algebraic space that is a geometric quotient of \(X\) by \(R\) and a uniform
categorical quotient, separated if \(j\) is; in particular a flat \(S\)-group
scheme acting properly on \(X\) with finite stabilizers has a geometric
quotient \(X\to X/G\), a result due, for reductive \(G\), to J.~Kollár
(\emph{Quotient spaces modulo algebraic groups}, Ann.\ of Math.\ \textbf{145}
(1997), 33--79).}
\emph{and the morphism \(X_1\to X_0\times_S X_0\) with components \(d_0\) and
\(d_1\) is quasi-finite. Then:}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)]\phantomsection\label{thm:sga3-V-7.1-i}%
  \emph{There exists a cokernel \((Y,p)\) of \((d_0,d_1)\) in
  \((\mathbf{Sch}/S)\). Moreover, every such \((Y,p)\) is a cokernel
  of \((d_0,d_1)\) in the category of all ringed spaces.}
\item[(ii)]\phantomsection\label{thm:sga3-V-7.1-ii}%
  \emph{\(p\) is surjective, open, and proper, and \(Y\) is of finite type
  over \(S\).}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~V, states
  (ii) in the form: \(p\) is surjective, open, proper and of finite
  presentation, and \(Y\) is separated and of finite presentation over \(S\).
  This follows from (ii) as printed: \(S\) being locally noetherian, \(Y\to S\)
  and \(p\) are of finite presentation; and the diagonal of \(Y\) over \(S\) is
  closed, being the image of the diagonal of the separated \(S\)-scheme
  \(X_0\) under the proper surjective morphism \(p\times_Sp\). The
  re-edition also notes that TDTE~III, Theorem~6.1, asserts that \(Y\) is
  quasi-projective over \(S\) when \(S\) is noetherian, and that it does not
  see how to deduce this from the local existence of quasi-sections (for
  finite flat relations, see the Addition at the end of 5~b)).}
\item[(iii)]\phantomsection\label{thm:sga3-V-7.1-iii}%
  \emph{The morphism
  \[
    X_1\longrightarrow X_0\times_Y X_0
  \]
  with components \(d_0\) and \(d_1\) is surjective.}
\item[(iv)]\phantomsection\label{thm:sga3-V-7.1-iv}%
  \emph{If \((d_0,d_1)\) is an equivalence pair, then
  \(X_1\to X_0\times_Y X_0\) is an isomorphism and \(p\) is
  faithfully flat.}\footnote{Translator's note: the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~V, adds
  here, as for \hyperref[thm:sga3-V-4.1]{4.1}~(iv) (see the note at the end of 5~b)), that \((Y,p)\)
  is then also the cokernel of \((d_0,d_1)\) in the category of sheaves for
  the fppf topology, and that formation of the quotient commutes with every
  base change \(Y'\to Y\), in particular with every base change \(S'\to S\):
  \(p\) is faithfully flat and of finite presentation, and
  \(X_1\simeq X_0\times_YX_0\). It also cites a proof of the theorem for
  equivalence relations based on Hilbert schemes, which shows moreover that
  \(Y\) is quasi-projective over \(S\): A.~B.~Altman and S.~L.~Kleiman,
  \emph{Compactifying the Picard scheme}, Adv.\ Math.\ \textbf{35} (1980),
  50--112, Theorem~2.9; see also S.~Bosch, W.~Lütkebohmert and M.~Raynaud,
  \emph{Néron models}, Springer, 1990, \S8.2, Theorem~12.}
\end{enumerate}

Let \((Y,p)\) be the cokernel of \((d_0,d_1)\) in the category of all
ringed spaces. The argument of \hyperref[proof:sga3-V-4.1-c]{4.c)} shows that, to prove
part~\hyperref[thm:sga3-V-7.1-i]{(i)}, it suffices to show that \(Y\) is a scheme and \(p\) a
morphism of schemes. But this question is local on \(Y\); by
Lemma~\hyperref[lem:sga3-V-6.1-i]{6.1(i)}, it therefore suffices to show that every point
\(z\in X_0\) has a saturated open neighborhood \(U_z\) such that the
groupoid induced by \(X_\ast\) on \(U_z\) has a quasi-section. We may even
assume that \(z\) is closed in its fiber over \(S\) (we shall say that \(z\)
is \emph{closed relative to \(S\)}).\footnote{Translator's note: as the Gille--Polo re-edition (2011), Exposé~V,
explains, this suffices: if such a \(U_z\) has been found for every point \(z\)
closed relative to \(S\), the complement \(F\) of the union of the \(U_z\) is
closed, and each fibre of \(F\) over \(S\), being of finite type over a field,
has a closed point if it is not empty; such a point would be closed relative
to \(S\). So \(F\) is empty.} The existence of \(U_z\) then follows
from the lemmas below.
