\chapter*{About this edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About this edition}
This is an English translation of SGA~2, AI-translated from the corrected
French text of the re-edition edited by Yves Laszlo, \emph{Cohomologie locale
des faisceaux coh\'erents et th\'eor\`emes de Lefschetz locaux et globaux
(SGA~2)}, Documents Math\'ematiques~4, Soci\'et\'e math\'ematique de France,
2005 (\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0511279v1}{arXiv:math/0511279v1}).
That French text was typeset by volunteers of Bas Edixhoven's project
(L.~Bayle, N.~Borne, O.~Brinon, J.~Buresi, M.~Chardin, F.~Ducrot,
P.~Graftiaux, F.~Han, P.~Karwasz, L.~Koelblen, D.~Madore, S.~Morel, D.~Naie,
B.~Osserman, J.~Riou and V.~S\'echerre) and adapted to the SMF format by
C.~Sabbah.

The translation follows the text of that re-edition, with its corrections of the
1968 North-Holland edition and a few short additions by its editor, and keeps
its numbering and labels. Two notes by the editor appear, headed
``Editor's note'' (Expos\'es~V and~VIII). Page numbers in the margin refer to
the 1968 edition. Notes headed ``Translator's note'' belong to this translation.

Jacob C. Reinhold's English translation
(\url{https://github.com/jcreinhold/sga}, snapshot
\texttt{e7a259f3f8608ad3edf9bf6eead3fd504dd2d23e}) was consulted for
comparison; its CC~BY~4.0 licence covers that translation, not the French
original.
