# Rights and provenance

To the extent that the publisher holds any rights in the AI-produced translation, corrections and typesetting, they
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 ([RIGHTS_NOTICE.md](RIGHTS_NOTICE.md)). Rights in the underlying
French works, their editions and typography, and in third-party material remain with their holders. The evidence
archive of each release identifies the source text and the translation history of each volume; English comparison
material is never treated as the French source. The releases contain no publisher scans, OCR images or source crops.
